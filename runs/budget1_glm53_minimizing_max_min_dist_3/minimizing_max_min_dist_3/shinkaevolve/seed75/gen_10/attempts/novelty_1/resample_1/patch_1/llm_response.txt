The current optimizer gets 0.22 (ratio≈0.47), so there is a real gap caused by poor local optima. Two targeted improvements:

1. Add high-quality structured initializations that mimic the known Tammes-14 families: two staggered hexagonal rings plus two poles (D6-like symmetry), and an icosahedron shell with two symmetric mid-latitude points. These start the optimizer very close to the global optimum.

2. Replace the single final polish with an iterated "perturb-and-polish" scheme: repeatedly perturb the current best with decreasing magnitude and re-polish with the softmin/softmax gradient, accepting improvements. This escapes shallow local optima deterministically (fixed seed) and monotonically improves the best candidate, all within a modest time budget.
