- **Free-space SLSQP polish (exploiting scale-invariance, bounding dmax ≤ 1) reliably adds robustness** (Gens 23, 25, 27, 28, 29, 32), but alone does not break the 0.21–0.22 ceiling — it corrects the sphere-projection limitation without solving the soft/hard objective gap.

## Ineffective Approaches

- **Soft surrogates without hard-ratio refinement cap at ratio 0.21–0.22**: Gens 23 (0.21), 25 (0.21), 28 (0.21), 29 (0.21), and 32 (0.22) all converge below the optimum despite multi-stage annealing pipelines. The feedback on Gen 23 explicitly notes refinement "gets trapped in local minima rather than the true best arrangement."
- **Over-constrained parametric families limit the optimum**: Gens 24 and 31 (both 0.92) could not exceed their D6 ansatz's own optimum — "the multi-stage pipeline is more expensive than needed for a 3-parameter symmetric family," and 12.91s (Gen 26) or 10.77s (Gen 31) bought no ratio gain over faster programs.
- **Overly long runtimes without hard-ratio refinement waste compute**: Gen 31 spends 10.77s and Gen 26 12.91s on heavy pipelines that still yield 0.22, while the best program's ~28s investment (Gen 30) pays off only because annealing plus perturb-restart strategies escape poor basins.

- **The winning recipe (Gen 30) is: symmetric seeds (D6 rings + icosahedron+poles + random) → annealed soft-min/soft-max L-BFGS-B with temperatures 0.06→0.002 → 8 perturb-and-reanneal restarts around the best configuration → normalization (centroid to origin, dmax = 1)**. This matches and extends the current best program's structure, with perturb-reanneal being the distinctive addition.
- **Exact-ratio polish after smooth annealing remains decisive**: the current best program's coordinate-wise greedy hill-climb on the true ratio closes the soft/hard gap from 0.22 to 0.24 (as originally seen in Gen 8); Gen 30 achieves the same 0.24 via annealed re-perturbation instead — both approaches must directly target the true ratio in the final stage.
- **Scale-invariance exploitation (bounding dmax ≤ 1 in free-space SLSQP) is validated as lossless** and appears in six programs; combined with best-config tracking to guard against regressions (Gen 29), it is a safe, low-risk enhancement.
- **Robustness safeguards remain essential**: try/except fallbacks (current best program's scipy-unavailable relaxation path), best-config tracking, and post-hoc normalization are universal among top performers; prior insight (Gen 11's crash from off-by-one point counts) still applies — size invariants must be guaranteed.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. **Replace the D6-missing trial structure with an explicit D6 hexagonal-ring seed (two staggered rings + 2 poles) as trial 1.** The global analysis shows D6 ring seeds appear in every ratio-0.22 program (Gens 24, 26, 27, 31, 32), and the best program achieved 0.24 only after annealing landed near this basin. Add `P0 = normalize(d6_rings_seed)` as the first trial (before icosahedron and random starts) so annealing begins inside the proven-best basin; verify the seed has exactly 14 points (Gen 11's off-by-one crash).

2. **Add perturb-and-reanneal restarts around the incumbent after the main trial loop** — the distinctive Gen 30 feature that broke the 0.22 plateau. After the 10 trials complete, take `best_pts` and run 8 additional full annealing chains from `normalize(best_pts + 0.08*randn)` through tau [0.06, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005, 0.002], keeping the best. The current program only perturbs at trial initialization; re-perturbing the *incumbent best* is the highest-value compute identified (~0.31s per restart).

3. **Extend the hill-climb with pair moves targeting the binding constraints.** The coordinate-wise polish closed the soft/hard gap, but single-coordinate moves can miss improvements requiring both points of the min-distance pair to shift apart. Add candidate moves that displace the closest-pair points along their separating direction (and the farthest-pair points inward) by ±step in addition to single-coordinate moves — these are the only distances that directly affect the ratio, and the same improvement at larger steps cuts the 31.95s runtime.

4. **Use adaptive per-point step sizes in the greedy polish: 0.05–0.1 for points in the min/max-distance pairs, 0.01 for others.** The current uniform-step sweep spends most of its 14×3×2 evaluations per pass on coordinates far from binding constraints. Directing effort at the constraint-realizing points preserves exact-ratio convergence while significantly reducing runtime, improving the combined score's time component.

5. **Add numerical guards and lower the annealing floor to tau 0.002–0.001.** Use `np.exp(np.clip(±dv/tau, -50, 50))` in the soft-min/soft-max objective (also removing the dead-code logsumexp terms flagged in Gens 25/29/32) and append the extra tau values to the schedule. Sharpening the surrogate toward the true discrete ratio before the hard polish reduces the soft/hard gap the hill-climb must close, potentially exceeding 0.24 from a better starting configuration while the clipping prevents NaN/inf crashes.
