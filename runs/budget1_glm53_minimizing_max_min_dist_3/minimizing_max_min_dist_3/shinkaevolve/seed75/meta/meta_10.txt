This beat all other approaches.
- **Annealed temperature schedules on soft-min objectives are consistently strong**: Gen 5 (`sphere_softmin_optimization`, score 0.91, ratio 0.22) and Gen 7 (`structured_inits_sphere_proj`, score 0.90, ratio 0.22) both used log-sum-exp smoothing with annealed temperatures; all top-4 programs share this pattern.

- **Best-known geometry hint**: Gen 10's feedback notes the best Tammes-14 arrangement is two staggered hexagonal rings plus poles (D6 symmetry) — the top programs converge near it but slightly miss the optimum.

## Ineffective Approaches
- **Naive random placement fails completely**: Gen 0 (random baseline) scored 0.02 with ratio 0.00 — no structured geometry means near-colliding points.
- **SLSQP constrained optimization severely underperforms**: Gen 3 (`slsqp_pairwise_gap_optimizer`) scored only 0.07 (ratio 0.02) despite diverse geometric seeds and 91 pairwise constraints — the linear objective (maximize t) failed to drive packing, far below its own 0.2395 early-exit target.

## Implementation Insights
- **Soft-min/soft-max ratio objective via log-sum-exp**: The best program computes `smin = -tau*log(sum(exp(-d/tau)))` and `smax = tau*log(sum(exp(d/tau)))` and minimizes `-(smin/smax)` — a differentiable surrogate for min/max ratio that L-BFGS-B can optimize directly.
- **Multi-stage annealing with seeded restarts**: Best program chains L-BFGS-B over tau ∈ [0.1, 0.05, 0.02, 0.01], with 6 trials where trial 0 starts from the icosahedron seed and later trials jitter the best solution (0.05 × randn) — cheap exploitation of a strong seed.
- **Post-hoc normalization matters**: Best program translates centroid to origin and rescales so max distance = 1, matching the evaluation's ratio metric directly.
- **Robustness via fallback**: Best program includes a centroid-relaxation fallback if scipy is unavailable, ensuring correctness across environments.
