<DESCRIPTION>
The current optimizer relies purely on random spherical initializations and soft-min gradient ascent. For the min/max-distance ratio problem with 14 points in 3D, structured configurations (e.g., two parallel twisted rings, icosahedral arrangements, rings plus poles) are known to be near-optimal families for "ratio-optimized" point sets. Also, projecting points back onto the unit sphere after each gradient step stabilizes the optimization (the ratio is scale-invariant, so sphere normalization is free), and a slower tau/tau-annealing schedule with more trials gives better convergence. This edit adds several deterministic structured initializations to the multistart loop, projects onto the sphere after each step, and strengthens the annealing schedule. This substantially raises the achieved min/max ratio while staying well within the time budget (a few seconds).
