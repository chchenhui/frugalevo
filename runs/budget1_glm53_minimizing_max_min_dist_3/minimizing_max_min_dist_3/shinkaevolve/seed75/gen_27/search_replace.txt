<DESCRIPTION>
The current program relies on annealed soft-min L-BFGS-B from a single Tammes-like seed plus random starts, reaching ratio ~0.22. The prior best program (score 0.93) showed that the D6 family (two staggered hexagonal rings plus two poles) with a fine grid search over (h, z0), plus an exact greedy hill-climb on the true dmin/dmax ratio, reliably squeezes out extra ratio. I therefore (1) add a parametric grid search over the D6 family to seed the optimizer with the exact symmetric near-optimum, and (2) append a greedy coordinated pair-move hill-climb on the true ratio after the L-BFGS-B annealing, which can make strict improvements that smooth objectives miss. Both stages are cheap and keep runtime well under the timeout.

    # Greedy hill-climb on the true ratio: move the closest pair apart
    # along its separating direction, pull the farthest pair together,
    # and try single-coordinate nudges. Accept only strict improvements.
    def climb(P, steps=(0.02, 0.006, 0.002, 6e-4, 2e-4, 8e-5)):

        P = P / dists(P).max()
