<DESCRIPTION>
Apply the recommended staged tolerance schedule to the pair-move hill-climb: start with a wide near-binding band (tol=0.10) so many constraints get improved early (escaping local optima cheaply), then narrow the band (0.05, 0.02) so the final rounds converge to exact-ratio precision on truly binding pairs. This modifies `pair_moves` to take a tolerance parameter and drives it from the round loop. This matches prior Gen-40 insight that broader multi-pair scope helps early, while precision requires a tight band at the end.
</DESCRIPTION>
