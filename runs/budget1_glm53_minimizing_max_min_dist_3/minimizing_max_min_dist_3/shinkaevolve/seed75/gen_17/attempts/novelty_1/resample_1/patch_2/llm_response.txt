Lower the temperature floor in the gradient polish to 0.001 (reducing smoothing bias in the soft-min/soft-max surrogate), and add a final scipy L-BFGS-B refinement that anneals tau down to 0.001 over multiple jittered restarts around the best configuration. The quasi-Newton optimizer converges the sharpened surrogate toward the true discrete min/dmax ratio much better than the fixed-step normalized gradient.
