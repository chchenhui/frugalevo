I gate Stage 4 on `best < target`, and additionally add a very cheap high-sharpness final SLSQP pass (single beta, few iterations) that is only executed when the target was met, to squeeze out any tiny remaining slack (e.g., the 5th–6th decimal of the ratio) at negligible cost. This keeps the score at 1.00 while cutting roughly 10–20 seconds of redundant SLSQP work from the ~47s runtime, improving robustness against timeout variance across evaluator machines without risking any quality regression.
