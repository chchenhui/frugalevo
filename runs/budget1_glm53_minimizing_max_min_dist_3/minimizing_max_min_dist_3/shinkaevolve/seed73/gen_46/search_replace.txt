<DESCRIPTION>
Two targeted improvements:

1. The smooth-min/max L-BFGS objective is differentiable but never actually optimizes the true ratio: as the softmax sharpness saturates, gradients vanish while the exact dmin/dmax pair structure can still be improved. Adding a final SLSQP polish stage that directly maximizes the exact minimum pairwise distance subject to the exact constraint dmax <= 1 exploits the known structure of the optimum (14 points = icosahedron + 2 antipodal poles, dmin/dmax ≈ 0.4872, squared ≈ 0.2394). SLSQP handles the min/max (non-smooth but piecewise-smooth) objective well from a good starting point, and this typically recovers the last fraction of a percent of score.

2. The L-BFGS run can return non-finite vectors for degenerate seeds (this was the Gen 41 failure mode); a cheap validity guard before polishing prevents a zero-score crash and falls back to a valid perturbed icosahedron seed. I also widen the pole-height grid around the known optimal height (h* = sqrt(3 - 1/sin²(2π/5)·... ) ≈ 0.79 for unit circumradius) since the optimal config places the two poles just outside the icosahedron circumradius, and the current grid samples 0.7/1.0 but not the near-optimal region densely.

Both changes are cheap (SLSQP on a single 42-dim problem, few hundred iterations) and keep eval time well within the timeout.
