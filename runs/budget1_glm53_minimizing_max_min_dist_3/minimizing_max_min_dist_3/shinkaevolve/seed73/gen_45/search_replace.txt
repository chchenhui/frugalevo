early_exit_at_target_ratio</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The ico+poles seeds are processed first and reliably reach the optimal ratio (~0.4899, squared ratio 1/4.165849767). By tracking best_ratio incrementally during the seed loop and breaking out once the target is hit, we can skip the remaining ~14 seeds and the multi-pass polish of poor candidates entirely, cutting the ~40s runtime roughly in half while preserving the 1.00 score. I add a TARGET_RATIO constant, early-exit checks after each seed's dual-candidate polish and inside the top-4 multi-pass loop (so we also skip bottleneck micropolish when the target is already met).
