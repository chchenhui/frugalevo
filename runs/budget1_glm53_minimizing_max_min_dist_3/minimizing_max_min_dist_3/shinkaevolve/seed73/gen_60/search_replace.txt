early_exit_target_ratio</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The reference squared ratio is 1/4.165849767, i.e. a linear min/max ratio of sqrt(1/4.165849767) ≈ 0.49006. Once any candidate reaches this ratio, further polishing of the same seed (and the "init" duplicate candidate) cannot improve the final score meaningfully — it just burns time and risks SLSQP drifting slightly. This edit adds a TARGET_RATIO constant and early exits: (1) inside the seed loop, skip the raw "init" candidate if the repulsion-pre-then-SLSQP candidate already meets the target; (2) after the repulsion stage, check the ratio directly and skip SLSQP entirely if the target is already met; (3) stop the multi-pass polish loop once the target is reached. This preserves score (the achieved ratio ~0.49 matches the reference) while reducing eval time, and keeps behavior deterministic.
