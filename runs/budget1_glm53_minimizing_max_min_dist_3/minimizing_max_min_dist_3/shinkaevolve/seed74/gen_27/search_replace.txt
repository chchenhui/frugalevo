<DESCRIPTION>
The current score already matches the reference (combined_score ≈ 1.00), so any gain requires finding a configuration with squared ratio strictly above 1/4.1658 ≈ 0.2400. Two targeted improvements:

1. Better structured seeds. For n=14 on/around a sphere, a very strong candidate family is the "two staggered hexagonal rings + two poles" geometry (related to the known best Tammes-like arrangements for 14 points, e.g. staggered rings give many nearly-equal contacts). Two ring variants (with/without 30° offset and varied height) plus the existing icosahedron/cube starts broaden the basin coverage. Following the batch recommendation, I add an explicit shape assertion after building each seed so a truncation bug fails loudly instead of silently returning a wrong-size array.

2. Stronger final polish. The single exact-ratio L-BFGS-B polish often stalls at a kink of the min/max ratio (the objective is nonsmooth when the argmin/argmax pair switches). Running several polish restarts, each from the incumbent plus a tiny random jitter, and keeping the best result, reliably squeezes out extra ratio. This is cheap (the exact gradient is O(n²)) and keeps total runtime well under the previous 71s outlier.
