# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns

- **The "diverse structured seeds → smooth/local screening → SLSQP epigraph polish on top-3 → perturbation restarts" pipeline remains the confirmed winning recipe**: The current best program (Gen 43, `constrained_slsqp_polish_top_candidates`, score 1.00, ratio 0.24, 6.48s) applies ~40 diverse seeds (polyhedron-plus-poles with swept radii, Fibonacci sphere, staggered heptagons, random normals), a gradient-style annealed local search on all, then SLSQP constrained polish on the top-3 with jittered restarts at σ ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.15, 0.3} plus short annealing jiggles before re-solving. Gens 38 (1.00, 20.98s), 42 (1.00, 1.68s), and 46 (1.00, 1.79s) all confirm: multi-seed + annealed surrogate + exact/constrained polish reliably reaches 0.24.
- **Swept-radius polyhedron+poles seeds are the decisive exploration device**: Gen 43 sweeps pole radii pr ∈ {0.5…3.0} for cuboctahedron, icosahedron (z-axis and x-axis variants), producing a graded family that brackets the optimal basin. Gen 45's staggered hexagonal double-rings + repulsion-spread randoms (0.93, 0.29s) and Gen 39's weaker seed set (0.78) show seed family quality directly gates the final ratio.
- **Cheap screening → expensive refinement on top-k is the efficiency lever**: Gen 42 (1.00, 1.68s) and Gen 46 (1.00, 1.79s) refine annealed surrogates on ~25–30 seeds but spend exact-gradient/SLSQP effort only on top-3 candidates — matching Gen 37's pattern (1.00, 3.37s). Programs that spend uniformly heavy refinement (Gen 43 at 6.48s, Gen 38 at 20.98s) score the same 1.00 but slower.
- **Multiple-independent-convergence on 0.24 confirms it as the achievable attractor for n=14**: Gen 43's own feedback notes its seeds were "explicitly designed to escape a persistent 0.24 basin," yet still landed at 0.24 — strong evidence this is near-optimal, not a local trap. Gens 37/38/41/42/43/46 all at 0.24.

## Ineffective Approaches

- **Structural/interface bugs still zero out sound algorithms**: Gen 44 (`epigraph_multistart_rescale`, score 0.00) had the correct multi-start + epigraph architecture with hand-coded Jacobians but failed all validation — likely variable-count mismatch or invalid output format. This extends the Gen 29/32/33 pattern: crashes and interface defects, not algorithm quality, dominate total failures.
- **Weak refinement strength or weaker seed families stall below 0.24**: Gen 39 (`slsqp_minmax_14`, 0.78, ratio 0.19) had structured seeds + SLSQP but converged to a local optimum; Gen 45's moment homotopy reached only 0.22/0.93. Seed diversity alone (Gen 34 previously: 0.35) or annealing quality alone does not suffice — both must be strong.
- **Custom force-relaxation dynamics underperform standard smooth annealing**: Gen 40's 900-iteration force relaxation (0.77, 4.90s) previously showed this; the batch confirms the standard log-sum-exp/soft-min anneal with analytic gradients is both faster and more accurate.

## Implementation Insights

- **Gen 43's defensive SLSQP design is what keeps 1.00 achievable**: per-pair closure-built constraints with correct Jacobians (fmin/gmin, fmax/gmax with 2·(xi−xj) fills), try/except returning `(None, 0.0)` on failure, finite-check on `_ratio_sq(p2)`, strict-improvement acceptance (`r2 > best_val`), and `_normalize` before solving and on final output. This mirrors Gen 37's guard structure and directly avoids Gen 44's failure mode.
- **Iteration budget tiering bounds runtime**: Gen 43 spends 1600 local-search iters on the first 20 seeds, 1000 on the rest, 1500 on a re-polish of the incumbent — a simple, effective cost control absent from Gen 38 (20.98s) and Gen 41 (~7s).
- **Soft-ratio gradient mechanics with `np.add.at`-style scatter remain the shared core**: Gens 42, 45, 46 all credit the annealed log-sum-exp surrogate with quotient-rule gradients and temperature schedules (0.3→0.005 or q: 0.6→20) for escaping poor basins; Gen 43's hand-rolled force-style variant (`close`/`far` masks with decayed jitter) works but costs more time.
- **Perturbation restarts at multiple scales around incumbents provide the final margin**: Gen 43's σ-sweep with pre-SLSQP annealing jiggles, Gen 37's scales (0.05/0.15/0.3), and Gen 42's symmetry-breaking restarts are consistently credited for recovering the precise optimal geometry from soft solutions.

## Performance Analysis

- **Score/ratio landscape is saturated at 0.24/1.00; runtime is the remaining differentiator**: Gen 42 (1.68s) and Gen 46 (1.79s) achieve 1.00 fastest; Gen 43 (current best, 6.48s), Gen 37 (3.37s), Gen 41 (~7s), Gen 38 (20.98s) match the score at higher cost. - **Only one failure (Gen 44) in this batch, and again non-algorithmic**: its architecture matched the winners, reinforcing that per-pair loop Jacobians, output validation, finite-checks, and interface compliance are worth more than further algorithmic novelty.
- **Speed-vs-score sweet spot confirmed**: the SLSQP epigraph polish on top-3 adds the final ~0.01–0.02 over exact-gradient polish alone (Gen 35's 0.99 vs Gen 37's 1.00 previously; Gens 42/46 now deliver the full pipeline at ~1.7s).

# Recommendations for Future Mutations

1. **Replace the per-seed full-budget `_local_search` with a two-tier coarse-to-fine screen to cut runtime from 6.48s toward the ~1.7s of Gens 42/46.** Run a cheap pass (iters=300, step0=0.08) on all ~40 seeds, sort by `_ratio_sq`, then give the full 1500–1600-iteration anneal only to the top 5–6 candidates. 2. **Add post-SLSQP rescale-and-resolve iterations to squeeze out the dmax deficit.** After `_slsqp` returns, `p2` may have dmax slightly below 1 (the epigraph only constrains d² ≤ 1, and SLSQP can terminate marginally infeasible). This is nearly free and directly attacks the dmin/dmax numerator/denominator.
