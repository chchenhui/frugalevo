# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns

- **Structured icosahedron+poles initialization remains the dominant success factor**: The current best program (Gen 1, `optimize_min_max_ratio`, score 1.00, ratio 0.24) uses 12 icosahedron vertices plus two poles `[0,0,±3.0]` as its trial-0 start, matching the pattern credited across Gen 12 (score 1.00), Gen 13 (1.00), Gen 15 (0.99), and Gen 18 (0.92). Every score-1.00 program in this generation batch used structured geometric seeds.
- **Progressive sharpening of the log-sum-exp surrogate**: The best program anneals p = 4→16→48 through repeated L-BFGS-B calls, converting a smooth surrogate into the true ratio. Gen 13 (annealed k 8→120) and Gen 18 (k 3→50) replicate this at equal or near-equal quality, confirming that sharpness scheduling — not restart count — drives convergence to the 0.24 basin.
- **Two-stage pipeline: smooth surrogate optimization followed by exact/constrained polish**: Gen 12 (hill climb + SLSQP, score 1.00), Gen 13 (annealed soft-min + SLSQP polish, 1.00), Gen 15 (annealed soft-min/max + exact-ratio polish, 0.99), and Gen 18 (softmin + SLSQP polish, 0.92) all converge to the top via a polish stage on the exact objective or hard diameter constraint t² ≤ d² ≤ 1.
- **Scale-invariant/normalized formulations improve conditioning**: Gen 13's feedback explicitly credits its scale-invariant soft-min (normalizing distances by dmax) with avoiding the ill-conditioning of unnormalized smooth min/max; Gen 15 and Gen 18 similarly recenter/rescale each step (dmax=1).

## Ineffective Approaches

- **Icosahedron seed shape truncation is now a repeated fatal bug**: Gen 16 (`annealed_softmax_gradient_adam`, score 0.00) and Gen 20 (score 0.00) both failed validation with shape (12,3) instead of (14,3) — slicing `ico[:n]` on a 12-vertex icosahedron. This mirrors Gen 9's earlier identical failure; three generations have now been wiped out by the same silent truncation.
- **Penalizing only the single farthest pair caps the ratio**: Gen 17 (score 0.59, ratio 0.14) used a diameter penalty on one pair only; feedback notes the diameter constraint limits dmin growth, leaving it far below the 0.24 basin.
- **SLSQP alone from structured starts, without a smooth-surrogate pre-stage, underperforms**: Gen 19 (score 0.72, ratio 0.17) applied SLSQP directly on max-min distance from diverse seeds and stalled in local optima; its second-pass re-refinement showed diminishing returns. SLSQP works best as a polish after surrogate annealing (Gen 12/13/18), not as the primary driver.
- **Coulomb repulsion pre-stages add cost without benefit**: Gen 17's Coulomb stage did not translate into a good basin (ratio 0.14), whereas direct structured starts reached 0.24 without it.

## Implementation Insights

- **The best program's core mechanics**: exact golden-ratio icosahedron construction, `np.vstack([ico, poles])`, 10 trials (1 structured + 9 random restarts σ=2.0), p = 4→16→48 progressive L-BFGS-B, selection by *true* ratio (d2.min()/d2.max()) rather than surrogate value, then centering and normalization. Selecting the best of 10 by the true objective after sharpening is a simple but robust guard against surrogate bias.
- **Analytic gradients massively cut runtime at equal quality**: The best program's 71.56s (numerical gradients over 10 trials × 3 sharpening stages) is the slowest of all score-1.00 programs; Gen 12 achieved ratio 0.24 in **1.10s** and Gen 15 in **0.87s** using analytic Jacobians/hand-coded constraints — a ~65x speedup at identical score. Gen 13's dead numerical-`grad` function was harmless because L-BFGS-B used analytic gradients.
- **Seed shape validation must precede optimization**: the Gen 16/Gen 20 failures show every seed must be asserted/padded to exactly n=14 before the pipeline runs; silent slicing propagates the wrong shape to the final return.
- **Deterministic seeding aids reproducibility without hurting score**: Gen 17 (seed 12345), Gen 18 (seed 42), and the best program (seed 42) all pass validation reproducibly; fixing the RNG costs nothing in quality.

## Performance Analysis

- **Score plateau at ratio 0.24 across independent methods**: Gen 1, Gen 12, Gen 13 (all score 1.00), and Gen 15 (0.99) all land at ratio 0.24 via different pipelines (numerical-gradient L-BFGS-B, hill-climb+SLSQP, annealed soft-min+SLSQP, annealed LSE+polish), strongly indicating a shared attractor basin — likely the icosahedron+poles geometry — reached from structured starts. However, per prior insights the known optimum is ~0.62, so all current methods remain in a suboptimal regime; scoring saturates at 1.00 despite this.
- **Runtime varies ~180x at identical top score**: Gen 12 (1.10s) vs the best program (71.56s). Efficiency ranking at ratio 0.24: Gen 12 hill-climb+SLSQP (1.10s) ≈ Gen 15 (0.87s) < Gen 13 (20.84s) < Gen 1 (71.56s). Analytic gradients and hard constraints are the primary runtime levers.
- **Polish quality separates near-misses**: Gen 18 (0.92, ratio 0.22) and Gen 19 (0.72, ratio 0.17) show that even with SLSQP polishing, surrogate pre-stage quality determines the final ratio; Gen 19's SLSQP-first approach fell 0.07 short of the plateau.
- **Shape bugs are the dominant failure mode in this batch**: two of nine programs (Gen 16, Gen 20) scored 0.00 purely from the icosahedron truncation bug — algorithmic merit was irrelevant. Validation-before-optimization is higher-value than further algorithmic refinement given the saturated scores.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

# Recommendations for Future Mutations

1. **Replace numerical gradients with analytic log-sum-exp gradients and anneal p more finely.** The current best spends 71.56s on L-BFGS-B with default (numerical) gradients over 10 trials × 3 sharpening stages; Gen 12/15 hit ratio 0.24 in ~1s with analytic Jacobians. Implement the exact gradient: for f = −smin/smax with soft weights w_ab ∝ exp(−p·d²_ab) (min) and w'ᵢⱼ ∝ exp(p·d²_ij) (max), df/dx_a = −(Σ_b w_ab·2(x_a−x_b)·smax − smin·Σ_b w'ᵵᵃᵇ·2(x_a−x_b))/smax², and pass `jac=` to L-BFGS-B with a finer schedule like p = 2→8→32→128.

2. **Diversify structured starts beyond icosahedron+poles to escape the 0.24 basin.** Every top program converges to ratio 0.24 from the same icosahedron+poles geometry while the known optimum is ~0.62, indicating a single shared attractor. Add trial-0 alternatives: cuboctahedron vertices + poles, a 14-point Fibonacci spiral on the unit sphere, icosahedron with poles along a different axis, and pole radii swept over 0.5–3.0 — keeping the true-ratio selection guard to pick the best basin.

3. **Add a repulsion preprocessing stage on random restarts before surrogate annealing.** Pure random starts (σ=2.0) currently cluster and contribute nothing, so all 10 trials effectively search one basin. Run ~200 iterations of projected gradient descent minimizing Σ 1/‖pᵢ−pⱼ‖² (with recentering/rescaling to dmax=1 each step, per Gen 13's scale-invariant insight) on the 9 random starts, then feed these quasi-uniform configurations into the p = 4→16→48 schedule — note Gen 17 shows Coulomb stages don't help *structured* starts, so apply this only to random restarts.

4. **Harden seed construction with an explicit shape assertion before optimization.** Gen 16, Gen 20 (and Gen 9 earlier) all scored 0.00 from silently truncating the 12-vertex icosahedron via `ico[:n]`. Add `assert x0.size == n*d` (or pad with the pole vertices) immediately after building each start, so any future seed-variation mutation fails loudly rather than returning a (12,3) array. This is cheap insurance given shape bugs caused 2 of 9 failures this batch.

5. **Polish the top-3 surrogate solutions on the exact objective with a proper hard diameter constraint, not just the best one.** Gen 12/13/15 all gained from a constrained SLSQP polish stage (t² ≤ d² ≤ 1 on all pairs, unlike Gen 17's single-pair penalty which capped the ratio at 0.14). Select the top 3 candidates by true ratio after sharpening, apply small symmetry-breaking perturbations (0.05–0.3σ) to each, then run SLSQP maximizing dmin subject to max-distance constraints — affordable once recommendation 1's analytic gradients cut the surrogate stage to ~1s.
