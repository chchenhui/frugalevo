# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns

- **The three-stage "soft anneal → exact gradient polish → SLSQP constrained polish on top-k" pipeline is the confirmed winning recipe**: The current best program (Gen 37, `slsqp_constrained_polish_top3`, score 1.00, ratio 0.24, 3.37s) anneals soft-min/soft-max temperature T = 0.3→0.005 via L-BFGS-B with analytic Jacobians, polishes on the exact nonsmooth ratio, then applies an SLSQP epigraph formulation (maximize t s.t. t² ≤ d²ᵢⱼ ≤ 1) on only the **top-3 candidates**, plus symmetry-breaking perturbation restarts around each. This matches the pattern from Gen 41 (1.00, ~7s, annealed surrogate + repulsion-preprocessed random starts) and Gen 31 (0.98, 0.41s) — cheap screening on many seeds followed by expensive constrained refinement only on a few survivors is both fast and accurate.
- **Diverse structured seeds — icosahedron-with-swept-poles especially — remain the decisive exploration factor**: Gen 37 uses icosahedron variants with poles swept along all 3 axes at 3 heights (pz ∈ {1.0, 1.2, 1.5}), cuboctahedron+poles, Fibonacci spiral, cube+face-pushes, jittered copies at two scales (0.03, 0.12), and 10 random starts. Gen 35 (0.99, 2.50s) with ~25 similar seeds and Gen 41 (1.00, staggered hexagonal rings + 16 repulsion-preprocessed randoms) confirm the ≥25-seed structured bank is what reliably reaches the 0.24 basin; Gen 34, which also had ~30 seeds but weaker refinement, stalled at 0.08.
- **Perturbation restarts at multiple scales around the incumbent add reliable final gains**: Gen 37 applies SLSQP re-polish to jittered candidates at scales (0.05, 0.15, 0.3) around each top-3 solution, plus full `refine()` restarts at scales (0.02, 0.05, 0.10) around the global best. Gen 31's basin-hopping around the incumbent was similarly credited with "marginal but reliable gains," and Gen 41's jittered exact polish "effectively escapes kink stalls."

## Implementation Insights

- **The current best program's SLSQP polish is robust by construction**: per-pair loop Jacobian (avoiding Gen 21/29's crash), try/except around the whole SLSQP call with graceful return of the input, finite-check on output (`np.all(np.isfinite(P2))`), recentering and dmax-normalization before solving, and epigraph variable t with constraints `1 − d² ≥ 0` and `d² − t² ≥ 0`. Combined with guard clauses at the end (`best_pts is None` / non-finite fallbacks), this defensive structure directly answers the batch's three 0.00 crashes.
- **Cheap screening → expensive constrained refinement is the key efficiency lever**: Gen 37 refines all ~30 seeds with the fast L-BFGS-B anneal, sorts by true ratio `_ratio_sq` (not surrogate value — a pattern carried over from Gen 30), and spends expensive SLSQP only on top-3 + ~18 perturbation re-solves. This yields 1.00 in 3.37s versus Gen 41's 1.00 in ~7s (repulsion preprocessing + more polish rounds) and Gen 40's 0.77 in 4.90s (900-iteration relaxations per seed).
- **Quotient-rule analytic gradients of the soft ratio via `np.add.at` remain the shared core mechanics**: Gen 37's `_grad_soft` computes weights `w_min`, `w_max` from log-sum-exp with max-shift stabilization, forms `g = −(w_min·soft_max − soft_min·w_max)/soft_max²`, and scatters via `np.add.at(grad, i, ...)` / `(j, −...)`. This same design underlies Gens 31, 35, 36 (≥0.95) and is why they all run in 0.41–3.37s.
- **Correct two-stage gradient of the exact ratio**: Gen 37's `_exact_ratio_grad` adds `2r/dmax·u` for the min pair and `−2r·dmin/dmax²·u` for the max pair, with `dmax = max(dv[iM], 1e-12)` guarding division — a small detail that prevents degenerate-zero failures. The exact polish is only accepted if it strictly improves `_ratio_sq` (with `prev` comparison), never accepted blind.
