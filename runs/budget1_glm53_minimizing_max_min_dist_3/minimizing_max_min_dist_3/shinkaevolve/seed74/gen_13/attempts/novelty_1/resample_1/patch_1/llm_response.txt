<DESCRIPTION>
The current soft-min/soft-max objective is scale-coupled and its Nelder-Mead polish is inefficient. I replace it with: (1) a scale-normalized soft-min objective with k-annealing (k = 8 → 120) under L-BFGS-B, which is smoother early and sharp later; (2) many structured starts (icosahedron+poles, Fibonacci sphere, jittered versions, random sphere points); (3) an exact polish via SLSQP that directly maximizes the true nonsmooth problem: maximize t subject to t² ≤ ||pi−pj||² ≤ 1 for all pairs, with analytic Jacobians, plus a few perturbed restarts of the polish. This targets a true dmin/dmax beyond the reference 0.4897 (squared 0.24).
