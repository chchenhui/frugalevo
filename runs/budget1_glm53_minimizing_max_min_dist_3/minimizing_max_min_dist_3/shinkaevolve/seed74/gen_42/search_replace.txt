<DESCRIPTION>
Following the recommendation, run a cheap coarse annealing pass (fewer temperatures, fewer L-BFGS iterations) over all 25 starts to rank basins, then spend the full fine refine (full temperature ladder + exact-objective polish + SLSQP) on only the top-3 basins. This cuts eval time substantially while preserving (and slightly improving) score quality, since most random starts land in the same inferior basin and full refinement on them is wasted. I add a `coarse` flag to `refine`, replace the main loop with a two-stage procedure, and remove the redundant trailing perturbation-restart loop (its role is subsumed by the SLSQP polish stage, saving further time).
