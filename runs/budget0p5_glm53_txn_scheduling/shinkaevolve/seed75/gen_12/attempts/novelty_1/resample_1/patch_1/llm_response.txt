<DESCRIPTION>
The current beam search + greedy restarts construct schedules from scratch. A powerful complement is a local search phase: take the best schedule found and repeatedly apply improving moves (adjacent swaps and reinsertion of transactions at other positions), accepting any move that lowers the true makespan. This exploits the fact that conflict-induced makespan is sensitive to local ordering; small reorderings near conflict points often yield large gains. Combined with more greedy restarts, this polishes the best construction into a local optimum, which typically reduces makespan well below pure constructive methods.
