<DESCRIPTION>
The current local search uses insertion and swap moves. A classic improvement for sequencing problems is adding an Or-opt move: move a segment of 2-3 consecutive transactions to another position (possibly reversed). Segments of interacting transactions often need to move together — single-element insertion cannot reach these local optima in one move. Adding Or-opt as a third neighborhood in the random move sampling increases neighborhood coverage per round without extra rounds, and since cost evaluation is cached, repeated evaluations stay cheap. I sample segment moves with ~20% probability, keeping insertion and swap as dominant moves. This should escape a different class of local minima and reduce makespan further.
