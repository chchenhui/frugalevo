<DESCRIPTION>
The current version combines greedy sampled construction with local search but restarts use fixed parameters (6/0.95) and the swap_improve is applied only once to the best sequence. Two targeted improvements: (1) per-restart parameter annealing — early restarts exploit the greedy signal with high sample_rate and few samples (fast), later restarts diversify with lower rate and more samples, as recommended for the confirmed productive 0.85–0.95 rate region; (2) apply swap_improve to each greedy restart's result and keep the globally best improved sequence, plus a small evaluation cache to avoid recomputing makespans for repeated candidate sequences during local search. This should yield better schedules by combining diversification across restarts with intensification (local search) per restart rather than only on the single best greedy output.
