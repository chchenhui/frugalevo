<DESCRIPTION>
Two improvements: (1) add a memoization cache around `get_opt_seq_cost` so the annealing search, which repeatedly re-evaluates similar sequences, avoids redundant expensive evaluations — this dramatically increases the number of search iterations within the time budget; (2) add a quick best-improvement swap sweep immediately after greedy construction so the deep insertion search starts from a swap-polished solution. Swap moves (exchanging two transaction positions) are complementary to insertions and cheap to scan, and greedy output typically contains swap-improvable disorder.
</DESCRIPTION>
