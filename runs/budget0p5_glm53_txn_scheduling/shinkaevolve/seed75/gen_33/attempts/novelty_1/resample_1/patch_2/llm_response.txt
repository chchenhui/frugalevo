<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the insertion-only local search with a hybrid insertion+swap neighborhood and slower annealing. Insertion and swap moves reach different orderings in one step, so combining both neighborhoods escapes local optima that either alone cannot. Slower cooling (0.95 decay with a small floor) keeps the search alive longer — the cost cache makes repeated evals cheap. Global-best tracking already protects against worsening moves, and the greedy construction, caching, and restart loop stay intact (preserving the 3412.97 baseline structure). Step-by-step: (1) each round, sample insertion moves and nearby-biased swap moves, tracking the best move across both neighborhoods; (2) apply the best accepted move; (3) cool slowly and stop after several stagnant rounds.
