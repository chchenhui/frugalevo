<DESCRIPTION>
Run many randomized greedy rollouts (each with random starting transactions and sampled holdouts), keep the best schedule found, and apply a cheap local-improvement pass (pairwise swaps and reinsertion) to further reduce makespan. Since greedy with a single start is highly dependent on the first choice, restarting from many random starts and keeping the best result exploits the stochastic nature of the cost landscape. The local 2-opt-style swap improvement on the best candidate then fine-tunes the schedule using the true objective (get_opt_seq_cost), which directly targets makespan rather than heuristics like transaction length.</DESCRIPTION>
