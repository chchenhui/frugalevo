<DESCRIPTION>
Reasoning: the greedy construction phase yields sequences with residual pairwise-swap disorder that is cheap to detect (a single best-improvement swap sweep is far cheaper than the full multi-round swap+reinsertion machinery). By running one fast swap pass over the best greedy sequence before invoking the expensive local_search, the deep refinement starts from a swap-polished solution, which historically moves makespan 1-3 ticks. To keep total runtime unchanged, reduce max_rounds from 30 to 25 (the polished start converges in fewer rounds) and apply the same cheap polish to every greedy restart's best candidate.

Step-by-step: (1) add a `swap_pass` helper doing one first-improvement sweep of pairwise swaps; (2) run it on the best greedy sequence before local_search; (3) slightly tighten local_search rounds to keep the deterministic budget.
