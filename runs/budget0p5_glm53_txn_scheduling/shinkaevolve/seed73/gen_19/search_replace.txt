<DESCRIPTION>
The main compounding error in beam search happens early, where a wrong pruning eliminates large swaths of the solution space. I make two targeted improvements: (1) use a wide beam during the first 25% of placements (where branching decisions compound most) and narrow it later — this concentrates the search budget where pruning error matters, as recommended; (2) scale the number of restarts with a time check rather than a hard cap so the full time budget is used across restarts (more diverse starting points), and track time more tightly so local search still gets its share. Additionally, in local search I add a randomized perturbation restart (accepting slightly worse solutions) when insertion/swap moves stagnate, which helps escape local minima.
</DESCRIPTION>
