<DESCRIPTION>
The current greedy is a single random-start construction with a fixed sample rate of 1.0 (which, at sample_rate=1.0, means every step takes up to 10 random candidates and picks the best — but with a single random starting transaction it's very sensitive to that initial choice). Improvement plan:

1. **Multiple restarts:** Run the greedy construction many times (e.g., 30+ restarts) with random starting transactions and randomized candidate sampling, keeping the best schedule found. Different starting points lead to very different schedules due to conflict dependencies.

2. **Lower sample rate / higher samples:** Use a moderate sample rate (<1) for exploration diversity and increase `num_samples` so more candidates are evaluated per step.

3. **Local search (2-swap improvement):** After each greedy construction, iteratively try swapping pairs of transactions in the schedule (adjacent and random swaps) and keep swaps that reduce the makespan, recomputing cost with `get_opt_seq_cost`. This escapes poor orderings created by greedy myopia since conflict delays are highly position-dependent.

This combination directly targets makespan since every acceptance decision (greedy candidate, swap) is based on the actual evaluated schedule cost rather than a heuristic proxy.
