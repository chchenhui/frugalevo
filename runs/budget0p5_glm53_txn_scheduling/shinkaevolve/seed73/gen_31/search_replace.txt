heuristic_plus_random_seeds</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The beam search currently uses purely random starting transactions. Since the first transactions placed have outsized impact on the final makespan (they determine the baseline cost before conflicts accumulate), seeding beam restarts with the cheapest single-transaction candidates gives the beam a strong deterministic foundation, while random seeds still provide diversity to escape local optima. I compute each transaction's standalone cost via `get_opt_seq_cost([t])`, take the top-2 cheapest as seeds, and add a few random seeds for exploration. This merges the deterministic lookahead strength that made greedy effective with restart diversity, and it's a minimal change to the `starts` construction. I also bump the number of random restarts from 4 to 5 since seeds are now more informative and low-cost.
