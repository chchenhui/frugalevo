<DESCRIPTION>
The greedy multi-restart with adjacent-swap local search reached 316. Adjacent swaps are too weak to escape local optima. I add a simulated-annealing refiner with a low initial temperature and mostly-downhill acceptance (fast-decaying T), using random insertion moves (move a transaction to a different position), which explore more of the neighborhood than adjacent swaps while staying cost-guided via `math.exp(-Δ/T)`. All imports (math, time, random) are verified at the top of the block. Time budget is split: multi-restart greedy first (shorter), then SA refinement seeded with the best sequence found.
