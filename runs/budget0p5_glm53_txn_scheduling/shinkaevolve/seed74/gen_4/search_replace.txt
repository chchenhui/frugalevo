<DESCRIPTION>
Run the existing greedy cost-sampling construction many times with random starting transactions, keep the best schedule found, and add a cheap 2-opt style local search (swapping adjacent transactions) to polish the winner. Since the greedy is randomized (random start + random sampling), restarting it repeatedly explores different regions of the schedule space, and best-of-N selection reliably lowers makespan at negligible implementation risk. Adjacent-swap hill climbing then fixes local ordering mistakes the greedy made. Time budget is controlled with a deadline so it stays fast.</DESCRIPTION>
