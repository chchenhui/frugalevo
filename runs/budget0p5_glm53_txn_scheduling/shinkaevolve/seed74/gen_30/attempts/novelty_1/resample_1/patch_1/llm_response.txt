<DESCRIPTION>
The prior results show that randomized-restart greedy + local search (current, 277) underperforms the iterated-local-search variant (254), which perturbs the incumbent best with ruin-and-recreate and re-polishes each candidate with a bounded local-search slice. The key reasons: (1) pure restarts waste budget re-running the expensive greedy construction, (2) perturbing the best solution concentrates search in promising basins, and (3) bounded local-search slices prevent one candidate from consuming the whole budget. I restore that ILS structure and additionally mix in occasional greedy restarts for diversification, and alternate perturbation move types (segment ruin-and-recreate, relocation, swap) so the incumbent keeps escaping different local optima. Every candidate is polished with a time-sliced local search and accepted only on improvement of the global best.
