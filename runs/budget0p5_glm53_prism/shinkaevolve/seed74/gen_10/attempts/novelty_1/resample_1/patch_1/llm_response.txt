<DESCRIPTION>
The current greedy assigns each model to the GPU with the lowest current KVPR, ignoring the effect of the model being added. A better greedy choice is to pick the GPU that minimizes the resulting KVPR after placing the model (weighted sum including the new model divided by remaining memory), which directly targets the objective. Also prefer GPUs with more remaining memory as a tie-breaker to avoid fragmenting memory.
</DESCRIPTION>
