<DESCRIPTION>
The current greedy assigns each model to the GPU with the lowest *current* KVPR. A better heuristic is to consider the resulting KVPR *after* adding the model, i.e., (w + r/s)/(mem - size), which accounts for both memory consumption and pressure. Combined with a fallback that tries to minimize the maximum KVPR across GPUs, this lowers the final max KVPR while remaining fast.
