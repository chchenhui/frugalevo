<DESCRIPTION>
The greedy construction assigns models once and can leave the max-KVPR GPU unbalanced. Adding a cheap deterministic local search (best-improvement moves of single models between GPUs) after construction reduces the maximum KVPR while preserving feasibility and fast execution. Ties in the greedy candidate selection are also broken toward larger remaining memory for tighter packs.
