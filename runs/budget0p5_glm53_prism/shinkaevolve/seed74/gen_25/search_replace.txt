<DESCRIPTION>
The current local search only tries single-model moves off the hottest GPU. When memory is tight, moves often fail where a swap (exchange a model on the hottest GPU with a differently-sized model on another GPU) would succeed and strictly reduce the max KVPR. I extend the refinement pass to also consider swaps between the hottest GPU and every other GPU, accepting any strict decrease of the maximum KVPR. This targets the exact bottleneck (the worst GPU's KVPR) and is a cheap, safe extension of the existing loop structure.</DESCRIPTION>
