best-fit-after-placement-kvpr</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The current greedy heuristic picks the GPU with the lowest KVPR *before* placing the model, which ignores the model's own contribution. A better greedy choice is to evaluate each candidate GPU's KVPR *after* hypothetically placing the model on it (i.e., (w_j + r/s) / (mem - size_j)) and pick the GPU that minimizes the resulting KVPR. This directly targets the objective (minimizing max KVPR) and avoids dumping large models onto a GPU where they dramatically raise the pressure. We also add a tie-break preferring the GPU with the most remaining memory, which helps future placements fit.
</DESCRIPTION>
