<DESCRIPTION>
The greedy choice currently selects the GPU with the lowest KVPR *before* placing the model. A better greedy criterion is to select the GPU that yields the lowest KVPR *after* adding the model (i.e., minimize (w + r/s)/(mem - s)), which directly minimizes the objective. This is the classic best-fit decrease variant of Algorithm 1.
