<DESCRIPTION>
After the greedy placement, add a local refinement phase. Repeatedly compute per-GPU KVPRs, find the hottest GPU, and try to move one of its models to another GPU if doing so reduces the overall maximum KVPR (and memory fits). Also stop after a fixed number of rounds to keep runtime negligible. This directly targets the objective (minimize max KVPR) and typically improves the greedy solution significantly.
