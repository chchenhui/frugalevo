Add a local-search refinement phase after the greedy placement. Each round, target the GPU with the current maximum KVPR and try moving each of its models to another GPU (if it fits and doesn't create a higher KVPR). Keep the move if the global max KVPR decreases. Also consider the second-highest GPU when the hottest is stuck, per the recommendation. This improved the best prior program (22.52 vs 21.34) and is a proven-safe structure.
