<DESCRIPTION>
Change the greedy assignment to pick the GPU with the lowest post-placement KVPR (best-fit) instead of the lowest current KVPR. This directly minimizes the resulting KVPR of the receiving GPU each step, giving a better initial placement for the local search. Ties are broken by larger remaining memory, and all candidates guard remaining memory > 0 to avoid division-by-zero/inf crashes.</DESCRIPTION>
