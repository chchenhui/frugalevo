<DESCRIPTION>
The greedy assignment places each model on the GPU minimizing KVPR at the time, but it can be trapped in poor local choices. Adding a cheap local-search refinement pass after the greedy step—repeatedly moving or swapping models if it reduces the maximum KVPR across GPUs—typically yields a noticeably lower max KVPR with negligible runtime, since test instances are small. The objective for a move/swap is the resulting maximum KVPR over all GPUs, which directly matches the metric being minimized.</DESCRIPTION>
