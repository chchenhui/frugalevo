Keep the greedy construction (which achieves 100% success rate) but add a local search refinement phase that tries moves and pairwise swaps between GPUs to reduce the maximum KVPR. The earlier failed version crashed because `gpu_kvpr` divided by zero when a GPU's free memory hit exactly 0; this version guards with `free > 0` checks (treating empty/zero-free GPUs carefully) and uses a bounded number of improvement passes to guarantee termination. Swapping models between GPUs can reduce max KVPR when simple moves cannot (e.g., all GPUs are heavily loaded).
