# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. **Replace random ILS kicks with bottleneck-targeted destroy kicks**: In the ILS inner loop, with probability ~0.8 pick `src` as the GPU with highest KVPR in `cand` (rather than `ils_rng.choice(srcs)`) and relocate its highest `req_rate/slo` model to the destination with the lowest analytically computed post-placement KVPR `(g.load + rps) / (g.remaining_mem - model_size)`, falling back to random kicks otherwise.
