<DESCRIPTION>
Add a 3-cycle rotation move to local_search(): after single-model moves and pairwise swaps fail, pick the hottest GPU and two hottest other GPUs, and try cyclic rotations of the form (model A from hot -> g1, model B from g1 -> g2, model C from g2 -> hot). Each move is applied tentatively with full feasibility checks, accepted only if the new max KVPR strictly decreases. This extends the neighborhood beyond 1-opt moves and 2-swaps, which helps escape local minima when simple moves/swap cannot reduce the hot GPU's pressure. The subsequent ILS loop benefits from the stronger local optimum baseline, stacking with existing perturbation-based improvements.
