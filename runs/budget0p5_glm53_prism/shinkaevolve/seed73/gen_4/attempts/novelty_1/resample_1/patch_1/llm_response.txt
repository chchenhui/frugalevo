<DESCRIPTION>
The current greedy picks the GPU with the lowest *current* KVPR, ignoring how the new model changes the pressure. A better choice is the GPU that minimizes the KVPR *after* placing the model, i.e. (w + r/s)/(mem - size). This directly targets the objective (minimize max KVPR) and balances load with remaining memory, typically lowering the resulting maximum KVPR.
