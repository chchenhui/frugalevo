<DESCRIPTION>
The current greedy assigns each model to the GPU with the lowest current (pre-assignment) KVPR, which ignores how the model itself changes that GPU's pressure. Instead, pick the GPU that minimizes the resulting KVPR after placing the model. Then add a simple local-search refinement (move/swaps) to further reduce the maximum KVPR. This directly targets the objective and typically lowers the max KVPR while remaining fast.
