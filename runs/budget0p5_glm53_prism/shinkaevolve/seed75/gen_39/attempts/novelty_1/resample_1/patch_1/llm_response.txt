<DESCRIPTION>
Add best-of-N randomized greedy restarts with epsilon tie-breaking. During greedy placement, collect all GPUs whose resulting KVPR is within 1e-9 of the best and choose uniformly at random (seeded RNG for reproducibility). Run the full pipeline (4 orderings × N=8 restarts) and keep the lowest max KVPR. This diversifies local-search seeds at negligible cost, since runtime is uniformly 0.00s.</DESCRIPTION>
