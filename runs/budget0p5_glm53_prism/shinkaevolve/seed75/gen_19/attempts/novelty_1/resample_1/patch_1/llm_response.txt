<DESCRIPTION>
Add a pairwise swap operator to the local search. After the move-based phase exhausts improvements, try exchanging a model on the worst-KVPR GPU with a model on another GPU (memory-feasible in both directions) when it strictly reduces the global max KVPR. This escapes local optima where moving any single model doesn't help but an exchange would.
