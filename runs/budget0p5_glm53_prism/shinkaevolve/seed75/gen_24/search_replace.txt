<DESCRIPTION>
The current local-search loop only tries 1-for-1 moves, and its measured score (0/1) is far worse than the plain greedy (21.89), suggesting the move-only refinement is both weak and problematic. Following the recommendation, I extend the refinement phase to also perform swap moves: exchange one model on the worst GPU with one model from another GPU (1-for-1), and additionally try replacing one model on the worst GPU with two models from GPU g (2-for-1), reusing the same feasibility pattern (recompute both GPUs' free memory, skip if any denominator drops to ≤0, accept only on strict 1e-12 improvement of the global max KVPR). This handles size-vs-rate asymmetries that pure moves provably cannot fix, and strictly-decreasing acceptance guarantees termination and correctness.</DESCRIPTION>
