<DESCRIPTION>
Add 2-for-1 exchange to the swap phase: replace one model on the target GPU with two models from another GPU (both must fit in target's remaining memory, and the single model must fit back). This handles size-vs-rate asymmetries that 1-for-1 swaps cannot fix. Strict 1e-12 improvement on global max, same feasibility checks as existing code.
