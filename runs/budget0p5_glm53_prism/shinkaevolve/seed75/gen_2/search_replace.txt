<DESCRIPTION>
The current greedy assigns each model to the GPU with the lowest *current* KVPR (weighted rate / remaining mem), which ignores the effect of adding the model. Better: compute the resulting KVPR after hypothetically placing the model on each GPU, and pick the GPU minimizing that. This directly optimizes the objective and balances large-memory models better.
</DESCRIPTION>
