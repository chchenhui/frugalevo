<DESCRIPTION>
Add tensor-only fast paths for layouts where hierarchical group packing cannot improve the result. A single logical expert has a uniquely determined replica allocation regardless of node/GPU topology, and a single GPU has no placement decision at all. Bypassing sorting, LPT packing, permutation inversion, and replica-selection loops in these cases reduces planner latency while retaining the same validated physical-to-logical, rank, count, and inverse-map contract.
</DESCRIPTION>
