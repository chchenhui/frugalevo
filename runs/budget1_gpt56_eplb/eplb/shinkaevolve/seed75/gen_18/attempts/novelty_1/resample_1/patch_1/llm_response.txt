<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the Python-level all-pairs swap refinement from `balanced_packing`. The refinement has quadratic work in the number of items per pack for every candidate pack pair and is invoked both for node placement and GPU placement. The observed metrics show that it materially increases algorithm and inference time without improving either reported balancedness score. The heap-based LPT assignment remains the primary load-balancing heuristic, preserves the fixed cardinality constraint exactly, and assigns ranks directly during placement to avoid maintaining and rescanning per-pack Python lists.
</DESCRIPTION>
