swap_refinement
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add an optional bounded pair-swap refinement to the cardinality-constrained LPT packing. LPT is fast but can leave avoidable imbalance because it never revisits early assignments. After the initial heap-based placement, the refinement swaps one item between the currently heaviest and lightest packs when that strictly reduces their squared-load objective. This preserves the exact number of experts per pack and rank uniqueness while reducing load variance. The iteration budget is bounded by the number of packs, making the additional work small for typical node/GPU counts.
</DESCRIPTION>
