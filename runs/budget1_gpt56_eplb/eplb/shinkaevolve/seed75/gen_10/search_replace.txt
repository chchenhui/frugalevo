<DESCRIPTION>
Refine the cardinality-constrained LPT/heap packing with a small bounded local-search phase. The heap pass remains the fast construction heuristic, while pairwise swaps between packs reduce the sum of squared pack loads without changing the number of experts assigned to any pack. This specifically addresses cases where greedy placement leaves a heavy item in a relatively overloaded GPU/node pack and a lighter item in an underloaded pack.

The refinement is deliberately bounded to four improving swaps per layer, which keeps the added cost small for typical expert and GPU counts while improving the final GPU load distribution. It preserves deterministic tie behavior and reconstructs `pack_index` and `rank_in_pack` after refinement.
</DESCRIPTION>
