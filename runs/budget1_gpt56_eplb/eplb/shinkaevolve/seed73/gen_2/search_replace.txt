<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the inner linear scan over all packs with a min-heap. The original constrained LPT packing repeatedly finds the least-loaded non-full pack in O(num_packs), making packing expensive when there are many GPUs/nodes. A heap preserves the same greedy policy while reducing this selection to O(log(num_packs)). Converting the already CPU-resident per-layer weights and sorted indices to Python lists also avoids repeated scalar Tensor operations and tensor-to-Python comparisons inside the hot loop.

This is especially beneficial because balanced_packing is called for both node placement and GPU placement across every MoE layer. The resulting assignment remains the same greedy largest-first load balancing strategy, including the exact cardinality constraint per pack.
</DESCRIPTION>

    cpu_weight = weight.float().cpu()

    pack_index = torch.full(
        weight.shape, fill_value=-1, dtype=torch.int64, device="cpu")
    rank_in_pack = torch.full_like(pack_index, fill_value=-1)
    for i in range(num_layers):
        # Entries are (current_load, pack_id, item_count).  A full pack is
        # simply not pushed back, enforcing the equal-size constraint.
        available_packs = [(0.0, pack, 0) for pack in range(num_packs)]
        heapq.heapify(available_packs)
        for group in indices[i].tolist():
            pack_weight, pack, item_count = heapq.heappop(available_packs)
