<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the Python linear scan over every non-full pack with a min-heap. The previous LPT-style assignment selected the least-loaded eligible pack in O(num_packs) time per group, while a heap performs the identical selection in O(log(num_packs)). Converting sorted indices and row weights to Python lists also avoids repeated CPU tensor scalar extraction and tensor arithmetic inside the innermost loop. This preserves the balanced-packing policy while substantially reducing rearrangement overhead for many groups, nodes, or GPUs.
</DESCRIPTION>

    weight_rows = weight.float().cpu().tolist()
    pack_index = torch.full_like(weight,
                                 fill_value=-1,
                                 dtype=torch.int64,
                                 device="cpu")
    rank_in_pack = torch.full_like(pack_index, fill_value=-1)
    for i, row_indices in enumerate(indices):
        pack_items = [0] * num_packs
        # (current load, pack id); pack id gives the previous deterministic
        # lowest-index tie break.
        available_packs = [(0.0, pack) for pack in range(num_packs)]
        heapify(available_packs)
        row_weight = weight_rows[i]
        for group in row_indices:
            pack_weight, pack = heappop(available_packs)
