<DESCRIPTION>
Improve the capacity-constrained LPT packing heuristic with a bounded local-search refinement phase.

The existing heap-based assignment is fast, but greedy choices can leave a heavy expert/group paired with smaller items on an overloaded GPU or node while a beneficial swap exists elsewhere. This is particularly relevant after replication, where physical-expert loads are often skewed and GPU balancedness is a major component of the score.

After the initial assignment, this edit repeatedly examines swaps between an item in a most-loaded pack and an item in another pack. It accepts the swap that lexicographically improves (1) maximum pack load and then (2) total squared pack load. The latter permits useful equal-makespan rearrangements that can unlock a later maximum-load reduction. The number of accepted swaps is bounded by `num_packs`, preserving predictable and low overhead while materially improving local packing quality. Cardinality constraints remain exactly satisfied because every operation is a swap.
</DESCRIPTION>
