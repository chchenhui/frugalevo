<DESCRIPTION>
Replace repeated scalar tensor indexing in the CPU greedy packer with Python row lists. The packing decision is inherently sequential, so tensor scalar reads/writes add dispatch and conversion overhead without providing vectorization benefits. Materializing each row once allows heap operations and assignments to remain entirely in Python until the final compact tensor conversion, while preserving the established largest-first, capacity-constrained hierarchical placement policy and its deterministic tie behavior.
