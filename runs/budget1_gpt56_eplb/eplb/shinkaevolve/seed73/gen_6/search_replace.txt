<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded local-search refinement after largest-processing-time packing. The existing heap policy is fast, but it can become trapped when equal-cardinality packs contain a heavy item paired with a medium item while a lighter pack has a compensating item. A swap preserves the mandatory number of experts/groups per pack and can reduce the maximum GPU/node load without changing replica counts or mappings.

The refinement evaluates swaps only between the currently most-loaded pack and less-loaded packs, chooses the best max-load reduction, and caps work to two improving swaps per layer. This keeps the additional CPU cost small while directly optimizing the bottleneck load that determines expert-parallel execution time.
</DESCRIPTION>
