<DESCRIPTION>
Preserve the greedy `argmax(weight / logcnt)` replica-selection rule exactly while avoiding a full `[layers, experts]` division on every redundant-replica assignment. Maintain the current per-replica load score in a reusable tensor and update only the selected expert in each row after its replica count increases. This reduces temporary allocation and elementwise work, especially when many experts are present, without changing selection order, tie-breaking, replica ranks, or balancing behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
