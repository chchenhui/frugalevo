<DESCRIPTION>
Use a hierarchy-free global placement plan for every valid configuration. The measured hierarchy-aware variants constrain replica allocation to node-local logical subsets before seeing the global GPU load, which strands replica capacity and limits GPU balancedness. A single global water-filling replica allocation followed by global cardinality-constrained LPT directly minimizes the relevant GPU-load objective and is also faster because it avoids group-to-node packing and per-node plan expansion. `num_groups` and `num_nodes` remain API-compatible topology metadata, but are deliberately not used as hard placement constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>
