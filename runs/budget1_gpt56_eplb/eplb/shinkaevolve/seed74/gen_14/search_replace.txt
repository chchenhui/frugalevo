direct_slot_layout

<DESCRIPTION>
Construct the slot-to-item permutation directly during greedy packing instead of first constructing its inverse and immediately materializing the inverse with a second full-tensor scatter. The scheduler already computes each selected physical slot, so writing the selected item at that slot removes one permutation-sized allocation and scatter from both group and GPU placement stages. This preserves deterministic LPT placement, fixed per-pack capacity, and the subsequent refinement behavior while reducing planner overhead.
</DESCRIPTION>

    # The scheduler computes physical slots directly.  Materializing this
    # orientation avoids constructing item_to_slot and then immediately
    # inverting it with an additional full-sized scatter.
    slot_to_item = torch.empty(
        (num_rows, num_items), dtype=torch.int64, device=weight.device
    )
    one = torch.ones(
        (num_rows, 1), dtype=torch.int64, device=weight.device
    )
