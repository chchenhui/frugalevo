<DESCRIPTION>
Use the global placement policy for all configurations rather than preserving hierarchical group-to-node locality whenever the group count divides evenly. The measured GPU-balancedness gap indicates that node-local replication constrains high-load experts to an unfavorable subset of GPUs. A single global planning domain lets water filling allocate replicas and LPT distribute every resulting shard across all GPUs, which directly optimizes the scored GPU load objective. It also removes the group packing and node-order permutation work from the common path, reducing planner execution time.
</DESCRIPTION>
