<DESCRIPTION>
Improve the GPU/node packing local-search phase with a lexicographic objective. The current refinement chooses an arbitrary swap among swaps with the same best resulting maximum pack load, and accepts only strict makespan reductions. This misses zero-makespan swaps that reduce load variance and can expose a better makespan-reducing swap in a later iteration.

Select, among all makespan-optimal candidate swaps, the one with the greatest reduction in the sum of squared pack loads. Such swaps preserve the primary maximum-load objective while improving overall GPU balance. They are accepted when they either strictly lower the maximum load or keep it unchanged while strictly reducing variance. The variance delta is computed algebraically from only the source and destination loads, so it does not require materializing all pack loads per candidate.
</DESCRIPTION>
