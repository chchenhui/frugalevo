<DESCRIPTION>
The current hexagonal rows 5-4-5-4-5-3 leave corner slack: the end circles of each row sit against the side walls but the top/bottom rows only touch two walls, and the four corner regions of the square are underused. Two targeted improvements:

1. **Corner-aware initial placement**: shift the first/last circles of the top and bottom rows diagonally into the corners (x near r, y near r, etc.), so those four circles can touch two walls simultaneously. The LP solver will then naturally assign them larger radii since the wall constraints bind in both directions. Also try squeezing the 3-circle last row against the bottom wall.

2. **Multi-start hill-climbing**: instead of a single random-seed search, try several perturbed initial layouts (different row staggers and corner offsets) and keep the global best. This diversifies basins at modest extra cost (LP with HiGHS is fast).

3. Increase iterations slightly and shrink jitter faster late in the search to fine-tune the best packing found. This pushes the sum of radii from ~2.44 toward the AlphaEvolve target of 2.635.
