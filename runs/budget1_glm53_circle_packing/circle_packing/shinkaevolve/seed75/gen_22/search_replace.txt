<DESCRIPTION>
After the hill-climb converges, add a deterministic greedy "growth pass": for each circle, try small shifts of its center (±step in x, y, and diagonals), re-solve the LP, and accept any shift that increases the total sum. This cheap local search directly exploits the fact that the LP solves radii exactly for fixed centers, so any center improvement translates monotonically into score. Using a small step (0.01 down to 0.002) and iterating a few times converges quickly while keeping the same runtime budget (~400 LP solves × 8 directions). Combined with the recommendation header, this targets the headroom in irregular center placement left by random-jitter hill-climbing while preserving guaranteed feasibility via the wall-bounded LP.
</DESCRIPTION>
