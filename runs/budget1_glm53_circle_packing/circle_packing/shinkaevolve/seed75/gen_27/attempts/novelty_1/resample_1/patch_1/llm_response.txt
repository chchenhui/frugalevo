<DESCRIPTION>
The current hill-climb pushes every wall-touching circle away from the wall it touches. That is correct for interior-edge circles, but wrong for corner circles: a circle touching two walls (e.g., r ≈ x ≈ y) has three-sided slack and gains radius by sliding INTO the corner, since only the diagonal neighbor constraint binds. The recommendation explicitly identifies asymmetric corner growth as the path toward the ~0.15 remaining headroom. This edit adds corner detection to the directed-move step: circles touching two perpendicular walls are pushed diagonally into the corner, while single-wall circles are pushed away as before. To keep the eval time within budget while the greedy growth pass (which is the most reliable improver) still runs, the random hill-climb iterations are modestly reduced. This should let the LP assign large radii to the four corner circles (three walls + one neighbor), a hallmark of the best-known n=26 packings, raising sum_radii beyond 2.39.
