<DESCRIPTION>
The best prior program (score 0.93, sum 2.44) combined an LP-based optimal radius solver with a stochastic hill-climb over circle centers. The current program has the LP but lacks the center-position optimization, capping it at 2.00. I add a hill-climbing stage after the initial LP solve: iteratively jitter the centers (with directed wall-avoidance moves for wall-limited circles) and re-solve the LP, keeping improvements. This directly exploits the fact that the LP gives the exact best radii for any candidate center set, so the search only optimizes the one thing the LP cannot — center positions. Expected result: match or exceed the 2.44 prior best within a modest time budget.

        sigma = 0.005 + 0.04 * frac

            x, y = cand[i]

            if r >= y - 1e-7:

                cand[i, 0] -= 0.5 * sigma
