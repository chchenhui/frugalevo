<DESCRIPTION>
Apply the recommended refinement: after the first shift search, re-solve radii from scratch with full Gauss-Seidel iterations (200), then run a second pass of the finest-grained local shifts (0.001, 0.0005). This shift→resolve→shift fixed-point iteration is monotone (only improving moves accepted) and cheap (tiny-delta moves warm-start in few passes), so it can only raise the sum of radii without risking validity or runtime.
