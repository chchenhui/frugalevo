<DESCRIPTION>
The current program regressed from 2.25 to 2.13. The regression came from the "corner" moves: pushing two-wall-touching circles diagonally INTO corners hurts this hex layout, and the tol-based classification misfires, dragging circles away from the LP-optimal lattice. Fix 1: revert to the proven "push away from the wall you touch" rule (this was the 0.86-scoring version). Fix 2: add a final greedy "growth pass" — after the hill-climb, try small single-coordinate shifts for each circle, re-solve the exact LP, and keep any strict improvement, with shrinking step sizes. Since the LP gives the exact optimal radii for given centers, any center move that helps is directly measurable; this polishes irregular placements the random hill-climb misses, at a modest LP-solve cost.
