<DESCRIPTION>
Add diagonal (±delta/√2, ±delta/√2) shift candidates to `_local_shift_search`. Corner-region circles are often limited by three constraints (two walls + one neighbor); moving along axes wastes one constraint's slack, while diagonal moves toward/away from corners can increase the three-way limited radius. Warm-started re-solves make each extra candidate nearly free and the eval budget (0.33s vs presumably ~few-second cap) has headroom. Also add mixed (±delta, ±delta) full-size diagonal moves for stronger escape from local minima.</DESCRIPTION>
