<DESCRIPTION>
The current refinement is a single greedy pass that easily gets stuck. Key expert insight: for n=26, equal-radius square/hex grids (25 circles at r=0.1 → sum 2.5) already beat the current 2.36, and larger sums come from variable radii with corner circles exploiting boundary space. I improve the constructor by (1) adding more candidate seed layouts including a plain 5×5+1 grid (guaranteed sum ≥ ~2.5 before refinement), (2) running a deterministic multi-start refinement: each candidate is jittered with a fixed-seed RNG and re-grown, keeping the best, and (3) increasing refinement iterations. This gives the growth heuristic many chances to escape local optima and let corner/edge circles enlarge, pushing the sum toward the 2.6+ range while keeping feasibility (radii only grow under the validated grow_radii routine).
