multi_start_slsqp_and_fast_grow</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Two targeted improvements: (1) Instead of polishing only the single best heuristic candidate with SLSQP, collect all refined candidates, rank them by achieved sum, and run the SLSQP continuous polish on the top-5 distinct basins, keeping the overall best. Different hex-row count sets lead to genuinely different local optima; polishing several of them dramatically increases the chance of landing in a near-global basin (the AlphaEvolve n=26 solution is asymmetric, so multiple distinct starting basins matter). (2) Vectorize the inner sweep of `grow_radii` with NumPy broadcasting (radii += where growth possible via a single vectorized candidate computation). Since `grow_radii` is called from inside the 60-iteration refine loop for dozens of candidates, this cuts eval time substantially, freeing budget for the extra SLSQP restarts.
</DESCRIPTION>
