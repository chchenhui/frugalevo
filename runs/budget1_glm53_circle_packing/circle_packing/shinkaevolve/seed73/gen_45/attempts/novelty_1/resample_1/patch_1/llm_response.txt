<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation targets the identified bottleneck: seed diversity. Currently only `cands[0]` receives 4 jittered variants; the other ~8 valid row-set layouts get none, and `r_init` is fixed at 0.05 for all candidates. Prior generations showed that multi-start jittered diversity was the single biggest gain (2.42 → 2.61). I propose: (1) generate 2 deterministic Gaussian-jittered variants (σ=0.015) per valid layout using a distinct per-layout RNG seed, (2) add radius-level diversification by scaling `r_init` by 0.9/1.0/1.1 per candidate, and (3) propagate the per-candidate `r_init` through `construct_packing` into `_refine` so the radius initialization scale also diversifies. This roughly triples the candidate pool cheaply (SLSQP per-candidate cost unchanged), greatly increasing the chance that at least one seed basin leads SLSQP past 2.61 toward the 2.635 target.</DESCRIPTION>
