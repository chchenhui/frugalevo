<DESCRIPTION>
Currently only the single best lattice candidate receives the expensive SLSQP restart polish and COBYLA refinement. However, the crude lattice seed sum is a noisy predictor of post-polish quality — a slightly worse seed can polish to a better local optimum due to different tangency structure. This is a well-known phenomenon in circle packing optimization: the basin of attraction of the constrained NLP solver is irregular. Polishing the top-k candidates (k=3) and keeping the overall best costs modest extra runtime (eval_time was ~9.7s with ample headroom) and directly targets the remaining ~0.025 gap to 2.635. Additionally, feeding the repaired point back as the warm start for the next SLSQP restart inside `_polish` already exists; we also increase rounds slightly and make COBYLA's rhobeg smaller (0.005) for finer tangency adjustments.
