<DESCRIPTION>
The current best programs show that center relaxation after the hex-lattice initialization boosts sum_radii from 2.36 to ~2.37. I propose: (1) keep the fast greedy radius growth, (2) add a damped repulsion relaxation of the centers (away from tight neighbors/walls) with best-so-far tracking, and (3) follow the recommendation of a final per-circle "contact relaxation" pass, where each center is moved to maximize its own locally-grown radius (Voronoi-like local maximization). This directly targets sum_radii at convergence and stays within the runtime budget since the growth is cheap and vectorized.
