<DESCRIPTION>
Two consistent changes, following the recommendation that multi-start breadth beats deeper local search:

1. **Broader candidate pool with per-variant seeds and radius-level stretch.** Instead of jittering only from a single RNG stream, each seed layout gets its own deterministic RNG seed, generating 2 jittered center variants (σ=0.015) plus 2 radius-stretched variants (r_init scaled by 0.9 and 1.1) with mild jitter. This roughly triples the candidate pool cheaply and adds a diversification axis (initial radius balance) that the center-only jitter could not explore. The radius stretch is threaded through `refine_layout` into `grow_radii` via a new `r_init` parameter.

2. **Greedy per-circle hill-climb polish in `refine_layout`.** After the existing vector-nudge loop, each circle is tested against 8 small directional displacements (plus staying), keeping the first move that strictly increases the total sum. This is a cheap, deterministic coordinate-descent step that exploits leftover slack (e.g., circles that can slide toward a corner) which the simultaneous nudge pass misses. Two sweeps keep runtime bounded.
