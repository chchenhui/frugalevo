<DESCRIPTION>
Broaden the multi-start pool: generate jittered variants of every valid row-set candidate (not just cands[0]) with different fixed seeds, plus radius-level stretch jitter. Diversification of starting layouts has consistently been the highest-yield improvement across generations (hex rows → multiple row sets → each improvement came from breadth). Each candidate gets 2 deterministic jittered copies, then the best is refined further by the existing void-filling loop. This should push past 2.42 toward the ~2.6 range.

    # Diversify: deterministic jittered copies of every candidate layout,

    for ci, cand in enumerate(candidates):
        pool.append((cand, r0))
        for seed, sig in ((101 + ci, 0.01), (202 + ci, 0.02)):
