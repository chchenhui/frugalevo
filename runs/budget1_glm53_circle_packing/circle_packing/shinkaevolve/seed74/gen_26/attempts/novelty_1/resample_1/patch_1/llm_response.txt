<DESCRIPTION>
All prior variants plateau at 2.36-2.40 because single-circle moves can't rearrange the lattice; the local triangular geometry forces every one-circle perturbation to be a net loss. The recommendation suggests rigid multi-circle moves: (1) translating an entire horizontal row of the hex layout by a small delta preserves all intra-row and most inter-row distances, letting rows collectively slide toward better wall alignment; (2) shifting a spatially coherent cluster (centers within 0.25 of a chosen circle) by a common delta moves a whole local patch rigidly. Both moves keep the LP radii valid and are accepted only if the LP total improves, so correctness is guaranteed. I add these move types with ~10% probability each into the existing hill climb, alongside the current single-circle slack-directed move (applied the remaining ~80% of the time). I also slightly reduce the per-move LP cost by keeping the same time budget (1.6 s).
