Expand the seed template list with denser-edge and boundary-heavy row patterns ([6,5,5,5,6], [6,5,6,5,4], [5,6,4,6,5]) and add vertical mirrors of each layout, since the sparse LP screen already handles any center configuration at negligible cost. These patterns concentrate circles near the square's edges where the AlphaEvolve 2.635 regime benefits from boundary-hugging circles (wall constraints allow larger radii at corners/edges), and mirroring doubles the effective seed pool for free, improving the hill-climb starting points. Also broaden the starting-pool for hill-climbing slightly (top 3 → top 4) to leverage the larger seed set, and add a mid-density vscale value.</DESCRIPTION>
