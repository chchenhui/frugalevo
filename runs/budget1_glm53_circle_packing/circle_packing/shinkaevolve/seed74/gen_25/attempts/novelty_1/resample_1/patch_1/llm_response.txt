<DESCRIPTION>
Two improvements: (1) deduplicate/expand the seed candidate list — the current list still wastes LP solves and lacks dense edge-row patterns like [6,5,4,5,6] variants and fully staggered 5-row patterns; adding genuinely distinct seeds costs microseconds vs the hill-climb and improves the starting basin. (2) Multi-start the hill-climb: currently only the single best seed gets climb time; instead run a short climb from the top few seeds and keep the best result — different seeds reach different local optima, and AlphaEvolve-level configurations often sit in basins not reachable from the single best LP seed.
