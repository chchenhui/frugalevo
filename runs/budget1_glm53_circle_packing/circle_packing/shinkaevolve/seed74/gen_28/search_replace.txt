<DESCRIPTION>
The current hill-climb only accepts strictly improving moves, so it gets trapped in the first local optimum it reaches — likely far below AlphaEvolve's 2.635 (we're at 2.37). The previously validated insight from the program history is that stochastic scheduling (adaptive step sizes) already helped; the natural extension is Metropolis-style acceptance of slightly worse moves at temperature T, geometrically decaying, with reheating after a streak of rejections to escape local basins. I add a `best` snapshot (best centers/radii/sum) so worse exploratory moves never cost us the final answer, decay T from 0.01 to 1e-4, reheat after 50 consecutive non-improving acceptances (capped at 2 reheats, restoring the best configuration), and adaptively grow the step when moves are rejected so the search doesn't stall. All moves are still validated by the exact LP, so validity stays at 1.00.</DESCRIPTION>
