Replace the simulated annealing block with the recommended multi-start jittered greedy hill-climb: each of 4 starts perturbs the winning lattice with its own ±0.01 Gaussian jitter (distinct seed per start, decorrelating the basins per the Gen-8 lesson), uses the proven adaptive-step move set (1–3 circles per move, shrink-when-stuck / occasional-grow), and receives a shrinking per-start time share `(deadline − now)/(n_starts − start)` so early starts explore broadly while the last start does fine polish. Total budget is capped at 1.4s to keep eval_time near the regime that scored 0.91, while the multi-start jitter gives more independent chances to escape local optima that a single greedy or SA trajectory cannot.

    try:

            rng = np.random.default_rng(1000 + start)
            cur = centers + rng.normal(0.0, 0.01, size=centers.shape)
            cur = np.clip(cur, 0.01, 0.99)
            cur_radii = lp_radii(cur)
            cur_score = np.sum(cur_radii)

                centers, radii, sum_radii = cur.copy(), cur_radii, cur_score
