global_first_replication</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Gen 9's per-node replication (Step 2 operating on node-local expert subsets) caps achievable expert balance: each node can only replicate among its own experts, and a hot expert confined to one node cannot receive replicas from the global redundant pool. We restructure the hierarchy to replicate globally first: run `replicate_experts` on the full logical expert set with the full `num_physical_experts` pool, so the water-filling greedy allocates every redundant slot to whichever expert has the highest per-replica load across all layers, unconstrained by node membership. Then the physical replicas (with their halved/shared loads) are packed to GPUs with a single global `balanced_packing` call. This swaps the order of Steps 1/2 as suggested, and should substantially raise `balancedness_score_expert` (the weakest metric at 0.25) while keeping GPU-level LPT packing quality, since the packing stage now sees globally balanced replica weights.</DESCRIPTION>
