<DESCRIPTION>
Two changes:

1. **Revert the snake packing** in `balanced_packing` back to the greedy least-loaded assignment. The measured metrics show the vectorized snake assignment regressed both `times_algorithm` (0.55 → 0.00) and `times_inference` (0.97 → 0.26) while leaving balancedness unchanged — it is strictly worse than the original greedy version, so restore the greedy loop.

2. **Replicate experts globally before packing**, per the recommendation. The per-node replication in old Step 2 caps achievable expert balance: each node only redistributes its own `num_log/num_nodes` experts over its own slot budget, so a hot expert cannot gain replicas by borrowing capacity from other nodes (expert balancedness is only 0.25). Instead, run `replicate_experts` once on the full logical expert set with the full `num_physical_experts` budget (marginal-gain `weight/cnt` picks the globally hottest experts for replication), then pack the resulting physical replicas directly onto all GPUs with `balanced_packing`. This gives every hot expert access to the whole replica pool (Step 1's node grouping was only a communication heuristic; replica slots need not respect it), and the greedy packing then equalizes per-GPU load across the entire pool. The final `phy2log`/`phyrank`/`logcnt` shapes are unchanged, so the `rebalance_experts` entry point and its `log2phy` scatter still work verbatim.
