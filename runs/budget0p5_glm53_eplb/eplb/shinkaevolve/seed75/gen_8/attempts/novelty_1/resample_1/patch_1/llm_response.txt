<DESCRIPTION>
The main performance bottleneck is `balanced_packing`: it runs a pure-Python double loop (num_layers × num_groups iterations with scalar `min(...)` calls), and it's invoked twice per rebalance. The combined score is dragged down by a speed_score of 0.00 (times_algorithm 0.58).

The fix vectorizes the inner loop across layers while keeping the *exact same* greedy LPT-with-capacity policy (each item goes to the least-loaded pack that still has capacity, ties broken by lowest pack index, same as the original `min(..., key=pack_weights.__getitem__)`). Now there are only `num_groups` Python iterations, each performing batched tensor ops over all layers at once, instead of `num_layers * num_groups` scalar iterations. This preserves the balancedness quality of the original algorithm (no approximation like snake/zigzag packing) while reducing the Python-level work by roughly a factor of `num_layers`, directly improving the execution-time component of the combined score.
