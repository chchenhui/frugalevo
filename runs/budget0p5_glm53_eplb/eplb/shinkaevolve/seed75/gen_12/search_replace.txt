<DESCRIPTION>
Two targeted improvements:

1. **`balanced_packing` refinement**: The current greedy (LPT-style) assignment can leave the heaviest and lightest packs far apart. I add a cheap local-search pass that repeatedly swaps items between the heaviest and lightest packs whenever the swap strictly reduces the maximum pack weight. This lowers the per-GPU load spread (improving both GPU balancedness and the per-replica load uniformity that drives the expert balancedness score), at negligible cost since it operates on plain Python lists.

2. **Policy selection in `rebalance_experts`**: The hierarchical policy (group→node packing) constrains where experts can live, which can hurt balance when the group loads are uneven. The algorithm is already very fast (0.01s), so I additionally compute the global policy and directly measure per-GPU load balance of both candidate layouts (sum of squared deviations from the per-layer mean GPU load), keeping whichever is better. This gives the best of both policies without runtime concerns.

    num_layers, num_logical_experts = weight.shape
    weight = weight.float().cpu()

    def gpu_imbalance(phy2log: torch.Tensor, logcnt: torch.Tensor) -> float:
        """Sum of squared deviations of per-GPU loads from the layer mean."""
        per_replica = (weight / logcnt).gather(-1, phy2log)
        loads = per_replica.view(num_layers, num_gpus, -1).sum(-1)
        mean = loads.mean(-1, keepdim=True)
        return ((loads - mean) ** 2).sum().item()
