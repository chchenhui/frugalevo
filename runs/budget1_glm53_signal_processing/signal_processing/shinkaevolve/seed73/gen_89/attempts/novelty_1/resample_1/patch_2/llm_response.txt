<DESCRIPTION>
Replace Gen 78's mild 0.6× soft-shrink on noise-dominated scales (mag < 0.5) with full hard-thresholding that zeroes all coefficients below the universal-threshold-derived cutoff. Fine, noise-dominated scales contribute almost nothing to correlation (which is carried by mid/coarse zero-phase scales) but add residual variance that produces spurious slope reversals — the champion's weakest metrics (noise_reduction ≈ 0.37 and false reversals). Because the wavelet reconstruction is zero-phase, removing these fine-scale dithering components does not introduce lag or phase delay, so lag error and tracking accuracy are preserved. Zeroing sub-threshold coefficients directly removes the noise floor rather than merely attenuating it, targeting a large noise_reduction gain at minimal risk to correlation. Additionally tighten the shrink zone boundary from 0.5 to 0.4 and pair it with a slightly stronger 0.85 universal-threshold multiplier to widen the effective dead zone on borderline-noisy scales.</DESCRIPTION>
