<DESCRIPTION>
Noise reduction (0.37) is the champion's weakest metric. In `_threshold_details`, the noise-dominated branch (mag < 0.5) currently applies soft-thresholding then keeps 0.6x of the residual, leaving fine-scale noise in the reconstruction. Since the a trous wavelet is zero-phase and signal-bearing scales pass essentially untouched, fully zeroing coefficients below the soft-threshold in noise-dominated scales should drop residual variance with negligible risk to correlation (0.89) or lag. As an atomic pair, tighten the universal threshold multiplier from 0.75 to 0.85 so more coefficients fall under the threshold in those scales, while the transitional/signal branches are unchanged to protect tracking accuracy.
