<DESCRIPTION>
The current Stage 3 reintegrates gated slopes as a pure integrator: any slope bias accumulates as drift, decoupling the output from true signal levels. This hurts lag_error (0.42), correlation (0.83), and noise_reduction. The previous-generation variant that blended reintegrated slopes back toward the level-anchored Holt output scored better on lag while keeping slope churn low. This edit: (1) blends the reintegrated path with the Holt level path (0.7 hs / 0.3 reint) to prevent integrator drift, (2) widens the slope median filter to a 5-tap window for stronger inter-window slope consistency, and (3) adds forward extrapolation along the Kalman slope estimate to compensate the smoothing group delay, reducing lag error. These directly target the multi-objective trade-off: slope-change suppression comes from the wider median, tracking accuracy comes from level anchoring, and responsiveness comes from lag compensation.

    # slope consistency that a 3-tap filter cannot achieve.
    med_slope = est_slope.copy()
