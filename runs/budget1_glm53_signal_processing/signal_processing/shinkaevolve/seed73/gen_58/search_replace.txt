<DESCRIPTION>
The current NIS adaptation `q_scale = q_base * (1.0 + 10.0 * min(nis, 50.0) / 10.0)` allows up to ~51× the baseline process noise. This injects noise into the velocity state, which then propagates into the level estimate upstream of the SG window — a likely contributor to the 41 residual slope changes and 36.6 false reversals. Capping the multiplier at 4× and raising q_base from 0.01 to 0.02 keeps tracking responsive enough (responsiveness is 0.71, with margin) while substantially reducing spurious slope churn fed downstream. This is a minimal single-variable mutation against the champion baseline to isolate its effect on slope_changes/false_reversals vs correlation.</DESCRIPTION>
