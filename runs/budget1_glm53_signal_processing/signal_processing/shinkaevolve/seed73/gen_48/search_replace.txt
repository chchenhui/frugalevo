<DESCRIPTION>
The locally adaptive σ threshold is already implemented but is applied at 0.5σ — identical to the previous global constant, so its adaptivity isn't exploited. Responsiveness (0.71) has the most headroom of all sub-scores while lag_error remains 0.41. Raising the snap threshold to 0.7× local σ and strengthening the median blend (0.6 med / 0.4 y) means noisy non-stationary segments (large local σ) get aggressively snapped toward the running median — killing spurious slope reversals and reducing lag-inducing dither — while clean segments (small local σ) keep essentially untouched dynamics. Since the threshold now scales with local noise, raising the multiplier does not sacrifice genuine trend changes the way raising the old global threshold would. This directly targets lag_error and slope_changes while keeping the false-reversal advantage, and stays fully vectorized (efficiency 1.00 preserved).</DESCRIPTION>
