(c) The final snap pass used sign hysteresis: a sample is snapped toward its local median only when its incoming step is sub-threshold AND either its sign disagrees with the previous step or it is far below threshold (0.5×). The current program snaps whenever the step is merely small (<0.7×local σ), which flattens genuine slow drifts and hurts lag error / responsiveness. The hysteresis condition preserves monotone (trending) segments with sub-noise steps — directly targeting lag_error 0.41 while keeping the reversal suppression that gives the low slope_changes/false_reversals.
