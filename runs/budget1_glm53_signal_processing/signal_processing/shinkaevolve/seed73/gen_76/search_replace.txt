<DESCRIPTION>
False reversals remain the strongest rank predictor of the combined score (21.2 → 0.64; 23–26 → 0.62–0.63; 28+ → 0.58). The current Gen uses a 0.7σ step threshold with a 0.6 median blend. The recommendation is to co-sweep the Stage 5 snap parameters as an atomic pair: lower the threshold to k=0.45 (catching more sub-noise steps before they become sign flips) while raising the blend to 0.75 (removing those flagged steps more completely). The sign-hysteresis mask logic stays byte-identical — Gen 70's mechanism design is validated; only the permissiveness/completeness balance is untested. Prior generations show that adding new damping mechanisms (Gen 66/67) fails, so we tune this existing mechanism instead. The threshold feeds both Pass 1 and Pass 2 (both reference `thr`), so a single constant change propagates consistently; the Pass 2 snap blend is also tuned to complete the removal more aggressively. Lower lag risk is bounded since genuine trend steps still exceed 0.45σ locally and remain untouched.</DESCRIPTION>
