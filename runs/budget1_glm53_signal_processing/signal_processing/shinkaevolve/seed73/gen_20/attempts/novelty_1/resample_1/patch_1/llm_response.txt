<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a Normalized Innovation Squared (NIS)-based adaptive process noise mechanism with a strict cap. In non-stationary regions the filter needs more process noise to track genuine dynamics, but uncapped scaling lets noise leak into the velocity (slope) state and produces spurious slope churn and false reversals. The recommendation is to scale Q by a capped multiplier driven by the NIS: multiplier = 1 + 2 * min(nis, 6)/6, which saturates at 3× (keeping genuine dynamics trackable while suppressing noise-driven velocity spikes). To compensate for the lower peak adaptivity, q_base is raised slightly (q_slope especially), so steady-state tracking is preserved. This directly targets the slope_changes (103) and false_reversals (85.4) metrics that dominate the composite score, while the capped adaptivity keeps lag_error bounded since the filter still opens up quickly on genuine regime changes.
