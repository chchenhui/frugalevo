I sweep the SG window from 2W-1 to 2W+3 with order-2 polynomials, keeping the proven e^-1.5 recency tilt and all convolution vectorized. The quadratic fit and recency weighting should absorb the extra group delay while the wider window further suppresses noise-induced slope reversals.</DESCRIPTION>
