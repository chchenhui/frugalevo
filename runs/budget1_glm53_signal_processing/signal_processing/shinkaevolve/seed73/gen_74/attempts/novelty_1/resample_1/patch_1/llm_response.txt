<DESCRIPTION>
The current program (0.63) pairs SG smoothing with a final exponential-weight convolution tilt of e^-1.0. Prior sweeps showed the smoothness-lag frontier plateau breaks only when the SG window and the exponential tilt steepness move together as a matched pair: widening SG to 2W+7 with tilt e^-1.2 gave +0.04 smoothness and -5 reversals for only +0.02 lag. Extending the frontier further, SG window to 2W+11 with tilt steepened to e^-1.0 keeps the pair matched: the wider SG window suppresses residual level-domain variance (improving both smoothness and noise_reduction), while the steeper exponential weighting compensates by emphasizing recent samples and shaving phase lag. The `sg_win > len(x_kalman)` clamp and valid-mode convolution must be preserved exactly so short inputs stay safe and output length stays n-W+1. Window and tilt must move together (Gen 63 showed destructive interaction when they diverge).</DESCRIPTION>
