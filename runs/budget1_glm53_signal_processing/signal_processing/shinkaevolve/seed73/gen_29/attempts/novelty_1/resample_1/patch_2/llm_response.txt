cap_nis_qscale_and_raise_qbase</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The current NIS-driven process-noise multiplier `1.0 + 10.0 * min(nis, 50.0) / 10.0` can inflate Q to ~51× q_base on innovation bursts, which leaks noise into the velocity state and produces spurious slope reversals (the 41 slope changes and false reversal penalty of 36.4). Since false reversals are the metric that predicts rank almost perfectly, we cap the multiplier at 4× and raise q_base from 0.01 to 0.02 so that genuine turns are still tracked quickly while noise-driven velocity jitter is suppressed.</DESCRIPTION>
