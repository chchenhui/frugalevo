<DESCRIPTION>
False reversals (85.2) and slope changes (108.2) are the dominant score drivers; prior data shows programs under ~60 reversals score ≥0.57. The current Kalman pre-filter inflates process noise by up to 50×r when NIS spikes (q_dyn = r * min(nis-1, 50)), which leaks measurement noise directly into the slope state and produces spurious directional reversals. Capping the dynamic inflation at 3×r (and reducing its coupling into the slope state from 0.25 to 0.1) keeps the velocity estimate clean while still allowing genuine turns to be tracked via the baseline Q. To further cut noise-induced reversals at negligible phase cost, the 5-tap binomial post-smoother is extended to a 7-tap binomial kernel (zero-phase interior, still near-zero lag). This trades a tiny amount of responsiveness for a large reduction in false reversals and slope changes, which the metric rankings show is the highest-value exchange.
