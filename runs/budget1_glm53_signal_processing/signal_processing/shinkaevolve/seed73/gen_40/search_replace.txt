<DESCRIPTION>
The metrics plateau at 0.62 across many filter tweaks: lag_error stuck at 0.41, responsiveness at 0.71, while noise metrics barely move. The bottleneck is the structural phase/group delay of the SG + convolution pipeline, not the post-hoc snapping. A principled fix is predictive lag compensation: after smoothing, extrapolate the local quadratic trend forward by roughly the effective group delay (half the SG window + half the convolution window ≈ window_size samples). This is a classic zero-phase-lag technique (equivalent to "smoothed prediction" / forward-stepping the SG polynomial fit). It directly targets lag_error and responsiveness without adding noise, since extrapolation uses the already-denoised, curvature-aware quadratic fit rather than raw data. Compute the slope (first difference) and half-acceleration (second difference) of x_smooth via vectorized gradients, then step y forward: y_pred = y + delay*(dy + 0.5*delay*d2y). Clip the correction by the local noise sigma to avoid over-shooting in flat noisy regions (safety cap), preserving reversal performance. This is O(n), adds negligible execution time, and leaves all proven snapping machinery intact.

    # The SG window (2W+3) plus the convolution window (W) impose a group
    # delay of roughly `window_size` samples. Extrapolate the denoised
    # local quadratic trend forward by that delay to cancel the lag:
    #   y_pred = y + L*(dy + 0.5*L*d2y)
    # The correction is capped by the local noise scale so flat, noisy
    # regions are not over-extrapolated (protects reversal performance).
    L = float(window_size)
    dy = np.gradient(x_smooth)
    d2y = np.gradient(dy)
    d = np.diff(x_smooth)
    block = 51  # +/- 25 samples

    if len(d) >= block:
        swv = np.lib.stride_tricks.sliding_window_view(d, block)
        local_mad = np.median(np.abs(swv - np.median(swv, axis=1, keepdims=True)),
                              axis=1) / 0.6745
        pad_len = (block - 1) // 2
        local_mad = np.concatenate([
            local_mad[:1] * np.ones(pad_len), local_mad, local_mad[-1:] * np.ones(pad_len)
        ])
    else:
        med_d = np.median(d)
        local_mad = np.full(len(d), np.median(np.abs(d - med_d)) / 0.6745)
    sigma_local_pred = np.maximum(local_mad / np.sqrt(2.0), 1e-12)
