weaker_bidirectional_kalman_prepass</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The lineage's validated lesson is that structural Kalman pre-passes were worth +0.01 and that matched pairs of knobs must move together. Currently Stage 1 runs a single bidirectional adaptive-Kalman pass (q_base=0.01). I propose adding a second, *weaker* bidirectional Kalman pre-pass (q_base=0.005, i.e. half strength, per the recommendation of q_base = 0.005×r-scale regime) applied to the despiked signal *before* the existing Stage 1. The weak pre-pass strips the coarsest measurement noise so the main adaptive pass sees a cleaner innovation sequence; its NIS adaptation then spends its responsiveness budget on genuine dynamics changes rather than on noise bursts, which should reduce spurious slope reversals (false_reversals 13.0, slope_changes 14.6 are the biggest score drags) without adding group delay — bidirectional fusion keeps the interior phase error near zero. Both stages move as a matched pair: pre-pass is deliberately half-strength so the main pass remains the dominant smoother, avoiding over-smoothing that would hurt lag_error and responsiveness.
