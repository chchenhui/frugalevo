soften_nis_q_multiplier</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The current NIS-driven adaptation allows q_scale to reach up to 51× baseline (q_base * (1 + 10*min(nis,50)/10) = 0.01 * 51), injecting excessive process noise into the velocity state. This noise propagates through the SG window and the median-snap pass, and is a likely contributor to the residual slope changes and false reversals. Softening the adaptation — capping q_scale at 4× and raising q_base from 0.01 to 0.02 — keeps the filter adaptive to genuine dynamics changes while suppressing noise-driven responsiveness spikes. Responsiveness (0.71) has margin to absorb slightly heavier smoothing, and improved noise reduction should raise correlation, smoothness, and accuracy, which dominate the combined score.
</DESCRIPTION>
