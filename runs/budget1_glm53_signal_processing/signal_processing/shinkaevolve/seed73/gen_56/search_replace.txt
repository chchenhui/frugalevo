<DESCRIPTION>
The pipeline's only remaining Python-level loop is the 5-tap running median snap (`np.median(pad[i:i+5]) for i in range(len(y))`). Replacing it with `sliding_window_view` + a single vectorized `np.median(..., axis=1)` computes identical numerics (same edge padding, same 5-sample windows, same median operation) while removing O(n) interpreter overhead and eliminating the fragile per-index bounds-checking pattern that multi-stage pipelines carry as a crash risk. This is a strictly-safe mutation: every metric-earning mechanism (Kalman stage, robust SG, recency-weighted convolution, snap threshold) remains intact, and the median stage becomes shape-safe by construction. Expected effect: slightly lower execution time with identical or marginally better slope-change/smoothness metrics, preserving the combined score while reducing correctness risk.</DESCRIPTION>
