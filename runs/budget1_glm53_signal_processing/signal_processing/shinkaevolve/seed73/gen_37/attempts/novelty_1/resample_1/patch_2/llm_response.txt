local_adaptive_snap_threshold</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single global MAD-of-diffs σ used for the snap threshold with a locally adaptive rolling robust σ computed over ±25-sample blocks of the smoothed signal's diffs. Volatile non-stationary segments get aggressive snapping (low local σ → lower threshold), while clean segments keep their dynamics (high local σ → snapping disabled), directly reducing lag error without increasing spurious reversals. The rolling σ is computed fully vectorized via sliding_window_view over padded diffs with median absolute deviation, so computational cost stays negligible. Threshold scaled at 0.6× local σ and applied per-sample to decide snapping.
</DESCRIPTION>
