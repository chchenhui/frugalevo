<DESCRIPTION>
The metrics show the pipeline already achieves good lag (0.35), correlation (0.95), and accuracy (0.95), but smoothness (0.29) and slope changes (48.40) / false reversals (34.80) are the weak points. Two targeted improvements: (1) The Stage 4 lead compensation currently uses `np.gradient(smoothed)` on the doubly-SG-smoothed signal — but the RTS smoother already produced an optimal, low-noise *smoothed slope* estimate (second state). Using that slope for the lead term both restores dynamics more faithfully and injects far less residual noise back into the output, directly reducing slope reversals. A small zero-phase SG polish of that slope suppresses remaining jitter. (2) Add a reversal gate: where the slope magnitude is small relative to local volatility (i.e., trend is not statistically meaningful), zero the lead term — this prevents the lead compensation from manufacturing false reversals in flat/noisy regions, cutting false_reversals while keeping the responsiveness gain where a genuine trend exists. Q, the 2-state model, and the proven SG/RTS structure are untouched.
