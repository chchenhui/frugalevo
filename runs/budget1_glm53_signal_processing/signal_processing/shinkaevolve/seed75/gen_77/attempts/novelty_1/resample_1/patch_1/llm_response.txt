<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the gradient-slope blend in Stage 4 (set slope = slope_rts) as a minimal single-change mutation. The 0.3 gradient weight is the last identified noise leak: np.gradient(smoothed) reintroduces high-frequency content that the three-scale SG bank and RTS smoother already removed, and it feeds both the lead compensation and the reversal gate, inflating slope_changes and false_reversals while only marginally helping lag. Historically, pure RTS-slope variants held lag at 0.25–0.40 with best-in-class reversal counts. Everything else (q_scale2, gate constants, lead, Stage 4b final pass) stays verbatim so the score delta is attributable to the blend removal alone. Since slope_rts is the full magnitude of the RTS slope (previously down-weighted to 0.7), the lead compensation retains full strength, which also helps lag responsiveness — the gradient component's contribution to responsiveness was small because the gate attenuates it in flat regions anyway.
