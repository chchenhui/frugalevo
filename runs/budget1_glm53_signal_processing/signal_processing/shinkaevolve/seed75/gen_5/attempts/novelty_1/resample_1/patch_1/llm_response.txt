<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the weighted moving average with a zero-lag adaptive Kalman filter combined with adaptive process noise. A steady-state 1D Kalman filter has essentially zero phase delay (unlike the moving average which lags by ~(W-1)/2 samples), directly improving lag_error and tracking accuracy. The process noise Q is adapted online from the local signal variance so fast trends are tracked (responsiveness) while noise is suppressed when the signal is quiet — reducing slope changes and false reversals. Additionally, a two-pass forward/backward smoothing is avoided to keep it causal/real-time; instead the measurement noise R is estimated from local high-frequency residual variance. Output length must remain len(x)-window_size+1 for compatibility, so the filter runs over the full signal and the last (output_length) samples are returned, which minimizes alignment delay (delay becomes ~0 effectively).
