<DESCRIPTION>
The current 2-state model has excellent noise reduction (0.81) and correlation (0.97), but lag_error (0.24) and slope_changes (121.8) still trail the best generation (Gen 4: lag 0.26, slope_changes 79.6). The RTS smoother already produces a denoised slope state `smoothed[:, 1]` for free — it is model-consistent, zero-phase, and carries none of the high-frequency energy that made raw `np.gradient`-based lead compensation fail in prior generations. Adding a first-order lead term `lead * slope` to the level estimate compensates the remaining group delay of the RTS smoother without re-injecting noise. I keep the lead modest (0.3 × window_size/4, capped at 2.0) — per the prior feedback, aggressive leads only failed because they used noisy slope channels; with the RTS slope state a moderate lead is safe. The lead also sharpens turning points, which should reduce the lag error while the downstream localized hysteresis stage continues to suppress any residual slope-induced micro-oscillations (and now acts on the lead-compensated signal, which is the correct order).</DESCRIPTION>
