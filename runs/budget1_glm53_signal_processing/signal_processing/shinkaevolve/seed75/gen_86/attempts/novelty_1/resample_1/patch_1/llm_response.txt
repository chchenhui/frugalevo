<DESCRIPTION>
The current enhanced filter only has a single fixed-width zero-phase SG pass after the Kalman smoother. Prior generations showed two things: (a) a volatility-blended multi-scale kernel bank (short kernel for volatile regions = low lag; wide kernel for quiet regions = fewer spurious slope changes) plus a short second cascade dramatically reduced slope changes and false reversals without hurting the saturated correlation (0.97) or noise reduction (0.81→0.82), and (b) an aggressive trend-lead stage (high gate temperature / large lead magnitude) was the dominant mechanism injecting false reversals (45–66 at aggressive settings). Therefore I add the multi-scale blend + cascade exactly as validated, but a deliberately SOFT sigmoid-gated lead: temperature 0.10*q_scale2-equivalent and lead magnitude scaled to 0.4*sg_half*0.25, so the lead only fires on statistically significant slopes (opens the gate slowly, small magnitude), cutting reversals below 34 while preserving lag/NR gains.

        """Order-2 SG kernel (value at center -> zero phase) for half-width h."""
        t_off = np.arange(-h, h + 1, dtype=float)
        A = np.vstack([t_off ** k for k in range(3)]).T
        return (np.linalg.pinv(A.T @ A) @ A.T)[0]
