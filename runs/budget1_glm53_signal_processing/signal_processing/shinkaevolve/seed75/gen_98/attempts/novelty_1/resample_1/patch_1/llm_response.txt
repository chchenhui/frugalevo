<DESCRIPTION>
The current best score (0.65) is dominated by false_reversals (42.2) and slope_changes (62.2), while lag (0.27) and correlation (0.97) are already good. The Stage-4 lead consumes the RTS slope state polished only by a 5-tap kernel; residual ripple in the slope channel is the main injector of spurious reversals through the sigmoid gate. Replace the fixed 5-tap slope polish with a wider, zero-phase SG kernel of half-width `sg_half // 2 + 1` (still short relative to the window, so responsiveness is preserved), and slightly raise the gate center (1.5 -> 1.75) so only more statistically significant slopes open the gate. This suppresses noise-driven lead spikes without materially raising lag, since the gate remains ~0 in quiet regions (no added delay there) and full on confident trends.
