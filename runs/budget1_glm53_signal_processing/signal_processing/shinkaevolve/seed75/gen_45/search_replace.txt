widen_widest_sg_scale</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current 3-scale SG bank uses half-widths {sg_half//2, sg_half, 3·sg_half//2}. The widest kernel dominates oscillation suppression in volatile regions, which is the direct lever on the 44.4 false-reversal / 65.8 slope-change bottleneck. The 3/2·sg_half cap was chosen once and never tuned, so it is the least-explored knob. Widening the widest scale to 2·sg_half gives the volatility-gated branch a stronger smoothing option exactly where local noise is highest (soft Gaussian membership ensures it only engages in high-volatility samples, so quiet regions keep their responsiveness and lag_error is unaffected). This is a single-parameter change: keep the other two scales and all weighting/percentile logic intact. Order-2 SG remains zero-phase and reproduces genuine quadratic turning points, so tracking accuracy is preserved even at the wider kernel. Since half-widths grow, the reflect-padding and degenerate-length guards already in _sg_smooth handle short signals safely, and output length remains n - window_size + 1 via the existing end-aligned trim.
</DESCRIPTION>
