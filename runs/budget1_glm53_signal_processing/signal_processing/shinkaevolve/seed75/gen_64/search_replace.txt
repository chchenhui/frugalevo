<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the explicit matrix inverse in the RTS smoother gain with a numerically more stable solve-based formulation, G = solve(Pm^T, (P_filt[i] @ F^T)^T)^T. This is equivalent mathematically but avoids ill-conditioned inversions of the predicted covariance, which historically (Gen 35) yielded more stable and smoother RTS passes. Q stays strictly fixed at 0.05·q_scale² — Q-scheduling by volatility was confirmed across four rounds (Gens 53, 54, 59, 61) to degrade smoothness and inflate reversals, so no change is made there. The 3-state CA model and TV/Condat filtering remain excluded as confirmed dead ends. This is a zero-cost numerical-stability improvement targeting slope_changes/false_reversals without touching lag or accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
