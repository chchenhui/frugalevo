<DESCRIPTION>
The current composition sits at 0.64 while the leaner Gen 3 anchor hit 0.65 with fewer slope changes (65.2 vs 66.8) and equal lag. The diagnosis: Stage 4's unconditional second-cascade SG pass over-smooths volatile regions, flattening genuine fast dynamics (adds lag + spurious reversals at trend re-entry), while Stage 5's sigmoid gate threshold of 2.0 MAD is too conservative, leaving residual lag on genuine trends.

Two targeted fixes:
1. **Volatility-gated cascade**: blend the cascaded (h2) result with the pre-cascade signal using a linear ramp in volatility-percentile space — full cascade weight only in the quietest 20-80th percentile band, zero cascade in genuinely volatile regions. This preserves the ripple-killing benefit where the signal is flat (fewer slope changes/false reversals) while keeping responsiveness exactly where lag matters.
2. **Softer lead gate**: lower the sigmoid onset from 2.0 to 1.5 MAD units so confident trends get their lead compensation earlier, shaving lag_error without opening the gate to noise (1.5 MAD is still well above the robust noise scale).

Both changes are local, shape-safe, and preserve all existing degenerate-case guards.</DESCRIPTION>
