The current version (Gen 5) removed the trend-compensation lead and improved (0.63, lag_error 0.26). Prior evidence: adding lead with a raw gradient (Gen 3's np.gradient style) reintroduced ripple; but using the RTS-smoothed Kalman slope state as the lead source gave the best false-reversal behavior among lead variants. Recommendation: re-add the predictive lead at 0.4×sg_half, but compute it from `smoothed[:, 1]` (the denoised RTS slope state), and additionally apply a short SG polish to the slope state itself before the lead multiplication to remove any residual curvature ripple. This should reduce lag_error below 0.26 while keeping the 0.97 correlation and improving false reversals, since the lead is driven entirely by model-smoothed, low-frequency slope information rather than a raw gradient.

    sg_half = max(2, window_size // 4)
    lead = 0.4 * sg_half

            slopes,
