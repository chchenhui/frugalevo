<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the naive sliding-window averaging with an adaptive Savitzky-Golay / Kalman hybrid. A plain moving average induces phase lag proportional to window length, causing lag error and masked genuine trend reversals (false reversals). The edit computes a 2nd-order polynomial (Savitzky-Golay) fit over the sliding window, evaluating the endpoint to achieve near-zero group delay, then blends this with a constant-velocity Kalman estimate whose measurement noise is adapted to local volatility (MAD-based). The volatility-adaptive Kalman gain keeps responsiveness during high dynamics (minimizing lag) and smooths in quiet periods (minimizing slope changes). A hysteresis-based trend-sign detector with a confidence threshold suppresses spurious reversals, directly penalizing false reversals while preserving genuine ones. All operations are O(window) per sample with fixed cost, meeting the latency constraint.
</DESCRIPTION>
