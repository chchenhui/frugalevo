<DESCRIPTION>
Implement the Gen45 × Gen46 composition: replace the current double sequential SG pass (which over-smooths and drives lag up to 0.35) with a 3-scale independent SG kernel bank (half-widths [sg_half//2, sg_half, 2*sg_half]) blended per-sample with Gaussian weights centered at the 20/50/80th percentiles of local volatility. Wide scale suppresses false reversals in volatile regions; narrow scale preserves dynamics in quiet ones. Also replace the gradient-based slope lead (identified noise leak) with the SG-smoothed RTS slope channel under a sigmoid gate, matching the lag 0.27 / noise-reduction 0.82 behavior of the 0.65 anchors while targeting the untried Pareto point.
