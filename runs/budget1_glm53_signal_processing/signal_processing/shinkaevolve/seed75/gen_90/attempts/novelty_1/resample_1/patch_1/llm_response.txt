<DESCRIPTION>
Replace Stage 3's fixed cascade of three SG passes (which the metrics show over-smooths: correlation 0.89, noise_reduction only 0.45, lag_error 0.42) with a volatility-adaptive blend. The current fixed wide-kernel bank smooths uniformly, degrading tracking accuracy and responsiveness, while keeping false reversals low. The recommendation: compute two zero-phase SG outputs (wide 2*sg_half and narrow sg_half//2) and blend them per-sample with a sigmoid of local volatility (convolved |diff|, as already computed for the Kalman R adaptation). In low-volatility regions the wide kernel dominates (smoothness, few slope reversals); in high-volatility regions the narrow kernel takes over, restoring responsiveness and tracking of genuine dynamics. This targets the accuracy/lag deficits while preserving the reversal-penalty gains. Validate shapes after each stage to guard against the crash pattern seen in earlier generations.
</DESCRIPTION>
