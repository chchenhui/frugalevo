<DESCRIPTION>
The two top performers differ mainly in slope_changes/false_reversals (smoothness) vs lag_error. Current program wins on lag (0.28) and noise reduction (0.70) but loses on false reversals (84.6 vs 66 in the RTS variants). A cheap way to get both: widen the SG half-window from window_size//4 to window_size//3 to further suppress residual ripple that drives spurious slope reversals, then compensate the added group delay with a first-order trend lead: shift the output forward along its own (already denoised) slope channel. Since the slope is taken from the fully smoothed signal, it carries little noise, so the lead recovers responsiveness without re-injecting jitter or false reversals. The lead factor window_size//6 approximately cancels the extra delay from the wider zero-phase window. Alignment and length contract are untouched (final trim stays end-aligned, length n - window_size + 1).
