conservative_slope_lead_on_rts_state</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a conservative, low-risk lag-compensation stage between the multi-scale SG polish and the output trim. The RTS smoother already produces a denoised slope state (`smoothed[:, 1]` before it is overwritten), which is a far cleaner trend estimate than numerically differencing the final output (the approach that failed in Gen 41 with 85.4 false reversals). We (1) capture the RTS slope state before the SG stage reuses the variable, (2) apply one short 5-tap SG pass to strip any residual ripple from the slope channel, and (3) advance the output by a conservative `lead = 0.2 * sg_half * slope_smoothed` samples-worth of level. This shifts the level forward along its own denoised trend direction, directly reducing lag_error (currently 0.27) with minimal added distortion, since the slope channel is already heavily denoised by both the RTS smoother and the extra 5-tap pass. Fewer spurious reversals should also result because the lead is proportional to a clean slope rather than a noisy one, and the small coefficient (0.2·sg_half) keeps the extrapolation local.
