slope_sg_smooth_and_conservative_lead</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Apply a short Savitzky-Golay smoothing pass to the RTS-smoothed slope state (already heavily denoised, unlike prior failed lead attempts on noisy slopes), then add a small conservative slope-lead to the output level. The lead is a fraction (0.2) of the SG-smoothed slope scaled by the SG half-window (proxy for effective delay). Because the slope channel is pre-smoothed, the injected lead is nearly noise-free, so false reversals should not increase while lag error drops.
</DESCRIPTION>
