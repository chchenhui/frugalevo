<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation identifies the one structural lever never tested: pass ordering. Currently the bilateral damper and all polish act only *after* both RTS passes (terminal), where Gen 42/45 showed damper stacking caps out with zero metric movement. Moving the bilateral one-pole damper *between* the two RTS passes (alpha ≈ 0.70) lets the Q/4 refinement pass re-sharpen genuine corners that the damper rounds, while the ripple the damper removed stays removed — the refinement pass sees a cleaner signal and its minimum-variance estimate tightens without re-introducing noise. Since the inter-pass damper now carries the ripple-suppression load, the terminal damper is lightened (alpha floor raised from 0.55 to 0.80) to protect lag_error, and the Whittaker escalation polish is retained as the final guarded step. Inter-pass alpha 0.70 sits in the recommended 0.65–0.75 band; if lag_error worsens, the alpha-eff fallback path in the guarded damper raises terminal alpha toward 0.85+ as recommended.</DESCRIPTION>
