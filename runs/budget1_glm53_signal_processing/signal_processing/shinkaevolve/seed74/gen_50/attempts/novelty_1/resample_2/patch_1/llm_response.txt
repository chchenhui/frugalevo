<DESCRIPTION>
Add a dynamics-blended third RTS pass. Gen 35's full-replacement third pass over-smoothed genuine turns; instead, run a third forward+RTS cycle with q3 = 0.0625·q2 (continuing the validated 4× geometric decay), then blend y ← y2 + w(k)·(y3 − y2) where w(k) = tanh-squashed ratio of local |second difference of y2| to σ_r. In noise-dominated segments w→1 (accept the extra smoothing), near genuine turns w→0 (preserve dynamics). A convergence gate skips the pass when the second pass already converged, protecting the 0.05s runtime budget. This should reduce slope_changes and false_reversals further (toward Gen 35's smoothness) while retaining the responsiveness/lag of the current generation.
