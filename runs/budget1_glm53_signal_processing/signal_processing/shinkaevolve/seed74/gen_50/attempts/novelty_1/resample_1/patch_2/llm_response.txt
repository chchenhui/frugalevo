<DESCRIPTION>
Add a gated, dynamics-blended third forward/backward RTS pass. The third pass is skipped when the first two passes have already converged, uses a reduced process-noise term, and blends the third-pass result back into the second-pass output with a local-curvature weight so genuine turns are preserved while noise-dominated segments receive extra smoothing.
