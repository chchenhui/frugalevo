fused_velocity_deadband

<DESCRIPTION>
Rather than adding a separate slope-stabilizer stage (which Gen 8 showed degrades performance by adding lag and stacked complexity), this edit embeds a lightweight deadband directly into the fused velocity computation. The forward (vel_f) and backward (vel_b) velocity estimates are summed, and if the magnitude of the fused velocity falls below a small threshold (≈ 0.2 × std of the first difference of the input), the velocity is zeroed out (deadband) and the fused level is held flat. This suppresses spurious slope reversals caused by noise without introducing pipeline latency, directly targeting the false-reversal penalty while preserving responsiveness for genuine large-magnitude trend changes.
</DESCRIPTION>

            # Fuse forward and backward velocity estimates with a
            # lightweight deadband to suppress small spurious reversals.
            # Instead of an extra slope-stabilizer stage (which adds lag),
            # shrink small fused velocities toward zero so noise-induced
            # sign flips are eliminated locally.
            vel_raw = 0.5 * (vel_f + vel_b)
            vel_thresh = 0.2 * vel_noise_std
            if abs(vel_raw) <= vel_thresh:
                # Deadband active: hold slope at zero, keep level flat
                vel = 0.0
            else:
                # Shrink by the deadband amount to avoid a step discontinuity
                vel = math.copysign(abs(vel_raw) - vel_thresh, vel_raw)
            fused[k] = fused[k - 1] + vel * dt
