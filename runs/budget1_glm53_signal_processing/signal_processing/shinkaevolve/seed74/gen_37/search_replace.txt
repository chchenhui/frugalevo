<DESCRIPTION>
The third RTS refinement pass (q3 = qa/16) hurt tracking: correlation fell from 0.89 to 0.86 and noise_reduction from 0.55 to 0.39 while only marginally improving smoothness — classic over-smoothing bias. The fix: remove the third pass entirely and instead make the *second* pass adaptive via a dynamics-aware blend between the pass-1 and pass-2 outputs. When the estimated signal dynamics (sigma_acc) are large relative to measurement noise, the aggressive Q/4 refinement over-flattens genuine trends, so we weight the more responsive pass-1 output back in; when the signal is quiet (mostly noise), we trust the full refinement. This recovers 0.72-level accuracy while retaining most of the smoothness gain and eliminating the extra O(n) pass.
