<DESCRIPTION>
The single-point random moves get trapped in local optima because moving one point at a time cannot restructure the whole configuration. I add a global "shake all points" phase: random displacement of all points at once with a moderate sigma, accept if min_area improves (or via annealing). Repeating this between local refinement rounds lets the search hop between basins. Also add a final alternating pattern polish: for each point, try a small set of axis-aligned and diagonal moves greedily, which reliably squeezes out extra area after the stochastic phases.
