<DESCRIPTION>
The current multi-start keeps the best across only 6 seeds, and if all refinements are weak (or degenerate) it silently returns a low-scoring configuration. Two targeted fixes: (1) increase the number of multi-start seeds to explore more of the landscape (the known best Heilbronn 11-point configurations are hard to reach from a lattice start, so more restarts with varied perturbation scales helps); (2) add an explicit non-degeneracy floor: any candidate whose min area is below 0.01 is rejected, and if no candidate passes the floor, the function retries with fresh seeds instead of returning a degenerate/weak result. This guarantees the returned configuration always has a meaningful nonzero minimum triangle area while improving expected quality.
