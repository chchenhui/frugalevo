<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation says single-point polish can't escape pair-traps where a cluster of 3-4 points simultaneously binds the minimum. I extend `_polish` with an extended candidate set: when a proposed single-point move fails, try simultaneous moves of the top-2 bottleneck points (by near-min triplet membership count) along a fixed 12-direction star and along same/opposite directions, accepting the first strictly-improving candidate. This keeps the incremental update structure (two points' triplet sets recomputed) while adding 24+ coordinated pair moves per sweep that can lift a jointly-binding cluster, which provably no single-point move can improve.
