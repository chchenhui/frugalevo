# Optimization Insights Analysis

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns
- **Current best (Gen 40, score 0.86, min_area 0.03, 1.13s): softmin-Adam → escape burst → low-temp Adam → adaptive polish.** The four-stage pipeline per seed is the new champion, beating Gen 30's 0.84. Key details: 700-step temperature-annealed log-sum-exp softmin Adam (τ0=0.02→5e-4, decaying lr), then a short 700-iter bottleneck-targeted EscapeAnnealer (T0=0.008→5e-5, membership-weighted multi-point perturbations), then a fine 350-step Adam at low temperature (τ=2e-3→2e-4, lr=0.003), then adaptive polish whose tight-triangle threshold adapts per step scale (multiplier 3.0→0.6 across the schedule).
- **Diversified seed bank with fixed corners remains decisive**: 9 seeds (lattice, mid-edge pattern, jittered variants, golden-ratio low-discrepancy, uniform random). Gen 46 (0.81, min_area 0.03, 4.75s) independently confirmed that a "portfolio of diverse seeds combined with softmin-gradient Adam is the main driver of quality."
- **min_area 0.03 is the score threshold**: every program reaching 0.03 this round scored ≥0.81 (Gens 40: 0.86, 46: 0.81), while all programs stuck at 0.01–0.02 scored ≤0.60.
- **Incremental triplet updates are a proven efficiency lever when polish-heavy**: Gen 45's recompute-only-~45-affected-triplets polish achieved 0.60 in 12.25s, and Gen 46's mask-based local updates delivered 0.81 in 4.75s — but the current best shows pure vectorized softmin gradients over all 165 triplets with `np.add.at` can be even faster (1.13s) while scoring higher.

## Ineffective Approaches
- **Heavy annealing pipelines without a smooth surrogate stay trapped at min_area 0.02**: Gen 39 (multi-start SA + basin-hopping + 12-direction pair polish, 18.01s → 0.54), Gen 43 (8 starts + pair polish in 24 directions, 18.73s → 0.60), and Gen 45 (6000-iter anneal + 4000-iter greedy, 12.25s → 0.60) all burn 12–19s for sub-0.65 scores. Feedback consistently cites poor local optima and initialization limits.
- **Softmax ascent with weak projection/convergence underperforms its potential**: Gen 42 (softmax-projected ascent, 5 seeds, 0.39s → 0.22, min_area 0.01) — efficient but stuck; feedback suggests the clip-based projection and insufficient exploration, with the fallback possibly masking the real result.
- **Silent/ambiguous failures still produce 0.00 scores**: Gen 44 (ring-layout SA + pair polish, 3.40s → 0.00 despite passing validation — likely min_area definition mismatch) and Gen 50 (Adam softmin reheat → 0.00 in 0.01s, exception path returning degenerate config). - **Smooth-then-greedy without sufficient surrogate quality plateaus low**: Gen 48 (log-sum-exp Adam + jitter greedy, 5 seeds, 0.87s → 0.42, min_area 0.02) — the softmin objective alone with poor conditioning near-degenerate triangles is insufficient; it needs the low-temperature re-optimization and adaptive polish stages the best program has.

## Implementation Insights
- **The best program's stage sequencing is the differentiator**: coarse softmin Adam (broad basin) → targeted annealed escape (basin escape via membership-counted point selection with per-triangle membership precomputation via boolean matrix) → low-τ fine Adam (sharpens the surrogate near the hard min) → adaptive polish (coarse-to-fine directional moves). Gen 48's single-pass softmin + greedy (0.42) shows the multi-τ refinement is what converts a softmin basin into a true sharp minimum.
- **Exact analytic gradients via scatter-accumulate over precomputed triplet indices**: `_surrogate_grad` computes per-triangle gradients including the sign term `s = sign(cross)` (handled at |cross|≤1e-18) and chains through the barycentric→xy map analytically; `np.add.at` accumulates over I/J/K — no Python loops over 165 triangles.
- **Cheap projection after every update keeps feasibility free**: `Geometry.project` clips then renormalizes over-simplex points with a 1e-9 safety margin — called after every Adam step and every polish/anneal candidate.
- **Guarded fallback pattern works but must be validated**: Gen 40 keeps a fixed corner-anchored fallback and compares `best_val < fb_val` before returning — but Gen 50's and Gen 44's 0.00 results show the fallback must itself be a valid, score-evaluable configuration, and metric computation must match the normalized-area definition.
