adaptive_bottleneck_threshold</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Widen the tight-triangle threshold at high temperatures (3.0x) and narrow it at low temperatures (1.2x) during annealing, per the recommendation. Early iterations move points involved in near-minimal triangles broadly (exploration); late iterations focus only exact binding constraints (fine polish). The `tight = a <= thresh` computation is already vectorized; only the multiplier becomes a function of annealing progress. Keep the polish threshold fixed (or slightly tighter) for fine refinement.</DESCRIPTION>
