topk_softmin_objective</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the uniform 165-triple softmin with a top-k (k=20) softmin in both the objective and its Jacobian. Currently the softmax weights w spread gradient mass over all 165 triplets, most of which are far from binding — this dilutes the optimization signal and biases SLSQP toward configurations where merely "average" triangles are comfortable rather than pushing up the binding worst-case ones. By taking the 20 smallest areas and applying the temperature-annealed log-sum-exp over only those, the gradient concentrates on the constraints that actually determine the objective, which is the smooth analogue of the discrete worst-triplet targeting that produced the biggest historical gains. The `m = ar.min()` shift is retained for numerical safety. Changes are confined to `obj` and `jac` inside `_optimize`; the rest of the pipeline (starts, polish, hybrid loop) is untouched.

            K_TOP = 20  # top-k softmin: focus gradient on binding constraints

            def _topk(ar):
                """Return (idx, values) of the K_TOP smallest areas."""
                if ar.shape[0] <= K_TOP:
                    return np.arange(ar.shape[0]), ar
                part = np.argpartition(ar, K_TOP)[:K_TOP]
                return part, ar[part]
