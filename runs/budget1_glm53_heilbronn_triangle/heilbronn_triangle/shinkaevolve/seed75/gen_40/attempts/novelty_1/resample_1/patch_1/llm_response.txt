mirror_symmetric_starts</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The known near-optimal Heilbronn configurations for n=11 in an equilateral triangle exhibit strong reflection symmetry about the vertical median (x = 0.5). Random starts almost never have this structure, so SLSQP and polish must fight an asymmetric basin. Adding deterministic mirror-symmetric starts — one centroid point plus 5 mirrored pairs — dramatically broadens coverage of high-quality basins at negligible cost (they are cheap to construct and deterministic). Each pair is a point plus its reflection (x, y) -> (1-x, y), guaranteeing the reflection symmetry that optimal layouts exploit. This directly implements the recommendation to diversify mirror-symmetric starts. The clip projection preserves symmetry since the triangle is symmetric about x=0.5, so these starts stay feasible after projection. This should raise min_area_normalized, and combined_score, without materially increasing eval_time (SLSQP converges quickly from symmetric starts since gradients w.r.t. mirrored coordinates cancel).
</DESCRIPTION>
