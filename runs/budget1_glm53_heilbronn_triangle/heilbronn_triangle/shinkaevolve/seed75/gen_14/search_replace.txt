<DESCRIPTION>
The prior proven pattern (smooth-softmin multi-start local optimization) reached 0.76 of benchmark in ~16s for only 4 starts. The single lowest-risk improvement is best-of-N over many more restarts wrapped in an anytime time budget: keep the softmin objective but anneal temperatures with fewer Powell iterations per pass (Powell's cost is dominated by line searches, so trimming maxiter lets many more starts fit), generate more deterministic starts (perturbations of the symmetric base plus seeded random ones), and loop `while time.time() - t0 < 25`, always keeping the best configuration found. Since the min-area objective is highly multimodal, best-of-N over independent starts is the most reliable way to push combined_score above 1.0. Determinism is preserved via a fixed seed; feasibility is guaranteed by the softmax barycentric parametrization; a hardcoded fallback remains if optimization fails.</DESCRIPTION>
