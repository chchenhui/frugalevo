# Optimization Insights: Heilbronn Triangle Problem (n=11)

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns
- **Softmax-parametrized smooth optimization is the new best approach.** The current best (Gen 26, score 0.82, min_area 0.03, 24.53s) parametrizes points as convex combinations of triangle vertices via per-point softmax weights (guaranteeing feasibility by construction) and minimizes a log-sum-exp softmin over annealed temperatures (1e-2→1e-4 × triangle area) with Powell — this outperforms all prior discrete local searches (previous best Gen 12: 0.77) and all SLSQP softmin variants (Gens 18, 24: 0.46).
- **Softmin annealing with smooth gradient-friendly objectives beats hard min targeting.** Gen 26's temperature annealing (coarse-to-fine) lets the optimizer consider many near-worst triangles simultaneously, avoiding the local-optima trapping seen in greedy worst-triplet approaches (Gens 25: 0.30, 23: 0.54).
- **Random-restart discrete local search remains the runner-up paradigm.** Gen 22 (score 0.71, 20.09s) used best-of-K batched perturbations of the worst triplet with geometric decay and structured warm starts — the strongest non-softmax result, confirming the pattern from Gen 12 (0.77).
- **Mirror-symmetric restarts and structured/symmetric seeds broaden basin coverage.** Gen 26 exploits the triangle's reflection symmetry (pairing points via barycentric weight swap) and adds a symmetric barycentric start; Gen 30's ring seeds (centroid + staggered 5-point rings) plus 25 restarts scored 0.46 in only 0.35s — fast but plateaued.

## Ineffective Approaches
- **SLSQP with softmin objectives consistently underperforms** (Gens 18, 24, 28: scores 0.46–0.49, min_area 0.02, ~0.4s). Feedback repeatedly notes softmin SLSQP converges to poor local optima dominated by near-degenerate configurations, and the subsequent single-point polish phases get trapped.
- **Silently-swallowed exceptions into degenerate fallbacks remain the dominant failure mode.** Gen 21 scored 0.00 in 0.01s — its main search crashed into a 4×4-lattice fallback (only 15 unique points, degenerate triangles), masked by a bizarre `_valid(np.delete(cand, vi, axis=0) * 0 + cand)` no-op check and broad exception handling. This confirms the prior insight that near-instant eval time (0.01s) diagnoses total failure.
- **Greedy single-worst-triangle focus with rapid step decay limits refinement.** Gen 23 (0.54) moved only the worst triangle's vertices with step factor 0.98 floored at 1e-5; Gen 25's coordinate ascent (0.30, 0.13s) with perpendicular moves was fastest but most trapped. Both plateau far from known-best configurations (~0.033–0.055 normalized).
- **Expensive per-iteration O(n³) rescans burn budget without score gains.** Gen 27 (0.51, 17.67s) repeated the full 165-triple scan inside polish loops, dominating runtime and limiting iteration budget — worse score than Gen 26 at comparable total time.

## Implementation Insights
- **Guaranteed-feasible parametrization eliminates the repair/clipping machinery entirely.** Gen 26's softmax weights (W = softmax(theta), points = W @ V) make every candidate feasible without barycentric clipping — the source of prior exceptions (Gen 15's out-of-triangle fallback point) — while unpack stays smooth and differentiable.
- **Annealed softmin lower bound (`temp*log(sum(exp(-(a-a.min())/temp))) - a.min()`) with precomputed 165 triples** gives Powell a smooth, well-scaled objective across temperature stages; the shift by a.min() prevents exp under/overflow — a numerically robust refinement of the raw softmin used by SLSQP variants.
- **Strictly-positive min-area final guard with a rescue restart** (Gen 26) is the correct defensive pattern: unlike Gen 21's zero-scoring fallback, it attempts one more optimization pass before returning a non-degenerate-by-construction fallback (Gen 26's `base` layout has min_area > 0).
- **Vectorized triple indexing `pts[triples]` with cross-product area computation** is universal across all working programs (Gens 12, 19, 22, 26) and enables cheap full-scan minimum/argmin; Gen 30's restart-basin skipping (discard starts below 30% of best) shows a viable throughput optimization (0.35s at 0.46).

## Performance Analysis
- **Score tiers**: crash/fallback cases (Gen 21: 0.00), fast-but-trapped greedy searches (Gens 25: 0.30, 18/24/30/28: 0.46–0.49, Gen 27: 0.51, Gen 23: 0.54), discrete restart search (Gen 22: 0.71, Gen 12: 0.77), and smooth softmax optimization (Gen 26: 0.82). The best program exceeds the strongest discrete search by ~15% and the SLSQP family by ~78%.
- **min_area_normalized 0.03 remains the hard ceiling** across all 10 newly evaluated programs plus all prior ones — no approach (more restarts, longer budgets, annealing, polish phases) has broken past it. Gen 26 achieved 0.03 but with the best combined score, suggesting its advantage comes from score components beyond raw min_area (e.g., consistency, validation robustness) or a better min_area realization within the 0.03 rounding band.
- **Runtime vs. score tradeoff**: the top two scorers are the two slowest runs (Gen 26: 24.53s at 0.82; Gen 22: 20.09s at 0.71), while sub-second programs cluster at 0.30–0.54 — with the exception that *too fast* (0.01s) means total failure (Gen 21). Investing the full time budget in optimization correlates with higher scores.
- **Smooth global optimization (Powell on softmax-softmin) finally outperformed discrete local search**, contradicting the earlier hypothesis that pure worst-triplet discrete search was the winning paradigm — the key differentiator was combining smoothness (multi-triangle awareness via softmin) with guaranteed feasibility (softmax), rather than the optimizer's gradient usage.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

# Recommendations for Future Mutations

1. **Increase restart count and diversify mirror-symmetric starts.** The current best uses only 9 starts (1 structured + 3 random + 3 mirror-symmetric), and Gen 26 needed 24.53s — close to the budget — but Powell iterations per start are the bottleneck. Reallocate: reduce `maxiter` from 400 to ~200 at the coarse temperature (where precision is irrelevant) and increase starts to 15–20, adding more symmetric pairings (e.g., 4 mirror pairs + centroid) since symmetric starts were specifically credited in the insights as broadening basin coverage.

2. **Add a fine-grained fourth annealing stage below 1e-4 × area_T.** Gen 26's annealing ladder stops at temp = 1e-4 × area_T ≈ 4.3e-4, at which the softmin still blends many near-worst triangles rather than sharpening onto the true minimum. Append a final stage at temp = 1e-5 or 1e-6 × area_T (with the same a.min()-shifted, overflow-safe formulation) followed by one exact-argmin polish: perturb only the argmin triplet's points via softmax logits, keeping the proven guarded-feasibility machinery intact.

3. **Interleave a discrete worst-triplet refinement between annealing stages.** After each Powell stage, extract the current pts, compute the argmin triplet via the existing vectorized cross-product scan, and run ~20 cheap Powell evaluations on only the 9 logit-coordinates of that triplet's points (fixing the rest). This fuses the runner-up paradigm's strength (Gen 12/22's targeted worst-triplet refinement) with smooth optimization, and since the objective remains the softmin, acceptance is still globally gated.

4. **Batch the triple-awareness with a k-worst softmin weighting.** Replace the uniform softmin over all 165 triples with a top-k softmin: compute `a`, take the k=20 smallest areas, and apply the same temperature-annealed log-sum-exp over only those. This focuses the gradient signal on the binding constraints (avoiding dilution from 145 irrelevant triples), is a two-line change to `softmin_obj`, and retains the numerically robust `a.min()` shift.

5. **Add a final "point-spread" guard against clustered-point local optima.** Near-degenerate optima in this family come from points crowding together (Gen 18/24 feedback). Before the final return, compute pairwise minimum distances; if any pair is below ~0.02, split them by moving one point's logits a fixed step along the direction away from its nearest neighbor, then re-run the last annealing stage. Keep the strictly-positive min-area rescue guard as-is — this extends it to catch the "feasible but degenerate" cases the current guard cannot detect.
