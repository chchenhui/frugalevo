# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

# Optimization Insights: Heilbronn Triangle Problem (n=11)

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns
- **Softmax-parametrized Powell softmin remains the winning paradigm — the best program (Gen 37's family / current best, 0.82, 24.53s) confirms it.** Per-point softmax barycentric weights guarantee feasibility by construction, and a numerically-stabilized log-sum-exp softmin (`temp*log(sum(exp(-(a - a.min())/temp))) - a.min()`) with temperature annealing at 1e-2→1e-4 × area_T, warm-restarting Powell from each stage's solution, is the core winning formula. The full 10-program batch (Gens 37, 41–49) failed to beat it despite many elaborations.
- **Best challengers are structured multi-start + rescue-guard refinements of the same family.** Gen 44 (0.81, 32.47s) used bottom-40 active-set softmin annealing over 4 temperature stages with ~15 multi-starts including mirror-symmetric seeds; Gen 48 (0.80, 12.32s) used top-K=20 softmin Powell with 5 starts plus greedy perpendicular polish. Both confirm the pattern: **structured seeds (mirror-symmetric, lattice, edge-heavy) + annealed Powell softmin ≈ 0.79–0.82.**
- **Mirror-symmetry exploitation in starts is a consistent top-tier feature.** The current best program explicitly pairs points via barycentric coordinate swaps (indices [1,0,2]) to reflect across x=0.5 — retained across Gen 44/45/48 (0.79–0.81), suggesting Heilbronn-optimal layouts respect the triangle's reflection symmetry.
- **Pure discrete local search is the strongest non-Powell family.** Gen 41 (0.71, ~10s) used best-of-32 random perturbation of the worst triplet with structured warm starts and perpendicular polish; Gen 29/39 (0.74–0.76 previously) remain the best discrete results. Incremental area updates via precomputed per-point triple lists (Gen 42) keep these efficient.

## Ineffective Approaches
- **SLSQP softmin variants remain stuck at the bottom tier.** Gen 46 (0.53, 0.90s) and its predecessors (Gen 32/40, also 0.53) show top-k softmin SLSQP with only 120/80 iterations, linear containment constraints, and heavy `except Exception` swallowing gets trapped at min_area 0.02 — escape/polish machinery layered on a bad basin yields no gain. Powell > L-BFGS-B > SLSQP ordering is confirmed again (Gen 44/45 Powell ≈0.80; SLSQP ≈0.53).
- **More stages ≠ better results.** Gen 43 (0.21!) ran 5 temperature stages × 4 starts with escape polishing, stochastic hill climbing, and argmin polish, yet barely beat its hardcoded fallback (min_area 0.01). Gen 37's own feedback notes its softmin final stage is "safe but underwhelming" — polishing stages attached to a converged basin add runtime without score.
- **Overly-broad active sets dilute the objective.** Gen 44's bottom-40 restriction (0.81) underperformed Gen 48's K=20 (0.80 at 2.5× less runtime) and the current best's full-triple softmin (0.82), and Gen 49's low-temperature tau=0.02 gradient ascent (0.64, min_area 0.02) suffered vanishing gradients — temperature and active-set size are sensitive, fragile knobs.
- **The 0.03 min_area ceiling is universal.** All ~30 evaluated programs plateau at min_area_normalized 0.03 or below; 0.02–0.03 outputs score anywhere from 0.21 to 0.81, proving combined-score variance at the plateau comes from other components, not from breaking the area barrier. No amount of restarts, annealing, or polish has exceeded it.

## Implementation Insights
- **The current best program's projection-free softmax parametrization eliminates the highest-frequency failure class.** `unpack(theta)` computes per-row stabilized softmax weights and multiplies by vertex matrix V — no barycentric repair, no clipping. In this batch, programs with repair code (Gen 37's barycentric repair, Gen 45's repair projection) all worked, but prior crash failures (Gens 33–35) occurred in projection/start-construction code, validating the best design.
- **Safe seeding via approximate inverse-softmax with clipping works**: `np.log(np.clip(bary, 1e-6, 1.0))` converts a hand-crafted barycentric layout to logits, guaranteeing valid initialization — a pattern Gen 43 also used successfully but from a weaker fallback seed (0.21 shows the seed matters).
- **Incremental area computation is the only way discrete searches stay fast.** Gen 42 (0.65, 10.43s) and Gen 41 (~10s) used precomputed per-point triple lists for best-of-K batched moves; Gen 37's O(n³) min-area enumeration at every trial step dominated its 17.68s runtime and "capped refinement quality" per its feedback. Gen 46's sub-1s runtime shows SLSQP variants simply run too few iterations to converge.
- **Defensive guards must verify positivity, not just finiteness.** The current best's final check `min_area(best_pts) <= 0.0` triggers a rescue restart rather than silently returning degeneracy — directly addressing Gen 38's failure mode (broad `except` masking a min_area=0.00 result).

## Performance Analysis
- **Score tiers (updated with this batch)**: crash bugs (prior Gens 33–35: 0.00) → degenerate-despite-valid (Gen 38: 0.00; Gen 43: 0.21 at min_area 0.01) → SLSQP/polish hybrids (Gen 46: 0.53, 0.90s) → gradient softmin variants (Gen 49: 0.64; Gen 42: 0.65) → discrete local search (Gen 41: 0.71; Gen 37: 0.72; prior Gens 29/39: 0.74–0.76) → smooth softmin variants (Gen 47: 0.75; Gen 48: 0.80; Gen 44: 0.81) → **current best softmax-Powell: 0.82**.
- **Runtime pays off only with the right optimizer.** The best spent 24.53s → 0.82; Gen 44's 32.47s → 0.81 (diminishing); Gen 46's 0.90s → 0.53 (starved). Powell-based pipelines clustering 12–32s score 0.80–0.82, while sub-second and ~9–10s gradient/discrete runs cap at 0.53–0.72. Time budget alone doesn't correlate with score beyond the optimizer/parametrization choice.
- **Diversity of starts beats depth of polish within a basin.** Every program ≥0.75 in this batch (Gens 44, 45, 48, best) used 5–15 structured multi-starts with symmetric/mirror/lattice seeds; programs relying on a single clever seed or heavy polishing of one basin (Gen 43: 0.21) scored lowest despite comparable sophistication.
- **Convergence plateau at 0.03 area across ~30 programs** indicates the remaining combined-score headroom lies in score components other than min_area, or in finding a fundamentally different structural configuration — no incremental modification of the softmin-Powell family (annealing granularity, active-set size, polish stages) has moved the needle in two full evaluation batches.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

# Recommendations for Future Mutations

1. **Add a fourth annealing stage at temp = 1e-5 × area_T followed by an exact-argmin triplet polish.** The current ladder stops at ~4.3e-4, where the softmin still blends many near-worst triangles rather than sharpening onto the true minimum — a plausible cause of the universal 0.03 area plateau. Append a final stage with the same `a.min()`-shifted formulation, then run a small Powell pass on only the 9 logit-coordinates of the argmin triplet (all other logits frozen), keeping the softmax parametrization intact.

2. **Replace the full 165-triple softmin with a bottom-k softmin (k≈20) plus an adaptive k decay across stages.** Gen 48 showed K=20 matches top-tier scores at 2.5× less runtime, and Gen 44's bottom-40 slightly underperformed the full softmin — suggesting k should *shrink* as temperature drops (e.g., k=60→20→10 across stages), concentrating gradient on binding constraints while avoiding Gen 49's vanishing-gradient failure at low temperature. Retain the `a.min()` shift for numerical safety.

3. **Reallocate Powell budget toward more diverse starts: reduce maxiter from 400 to ~150–200 at the coarse temperature and grow the start pool from 7 to 15.** Every program ≥0.75 in this batch used 5–15 structured multi-starts, and the current best spends 24.53s near the runtime budget. Emphasize mirror-symmetric seeds (via the `[1,0,2]` barycentric-swap pairing already in the code) plus lattice and edge-heavy barycentric layouts converted through the safe `np.log(np.clip(bary, 1e-6, 1.0))` inverse-softmax.

4. **Intersperse discrete worst-triplet refinement between annealing stages.** After each Powell stage, unpack points, identify the argmin triplet via the existing vectorized cross-product scan, and run ~20 cheap Powell evaluations optimizing only that triplet's 9 logits against the softmin objective, accepting only if global min_area improves. This fuses the discrete local-search family (0.71–0.76, Gens 29/39/41) into the smooth pipeline — the only cross-family hybrid not yet tested against the plateau.

5. **Add a point-spread degeneracy guard (pairwise min distance > ~0.02) checked on every candidate *before* acceptance, not just on the final return.** Gen 38's failure was a clustered-point local optimum that the `min_area > 0` check misses; the current program only guards the final result. Compute pairwise minimum distances per candidate, and when a near-collision is detected, split the offending point's logits away from its neighbor and re-run the last annealing stage — also raising the rescue-restart acceptance threshold from `> 0.0` to `> 0.02` since all successful programs cluster at 0.02–0.03.
