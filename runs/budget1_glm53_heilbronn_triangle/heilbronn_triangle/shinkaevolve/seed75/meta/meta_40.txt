# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

# Optimization Insights: Heilbronn Triangle Problem (n=11)

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns
- **Softmax-parametrized smooth softmin (the current best program) remains the strongest paradigm.** Gen 26 (score 0.82, min_area 0.03, 24.53s) uses per-point softmax barycentric weights (feasibility by construction) minimizing an annealed log-sum-exp softmin over all 165 triples with Powell at temperatures 1e-2→1e-4 × area_T. None of the 10 newly evaluated programs beat it; the closest were incremental/structured discrete searches at 0.76 and 0.74.
- **Incremental local search with structured warm starts + merge polish is the strongest challenger.** Gen 29 (0.76, 5.31s) batched best-of-24 moves on the worst triangle with precomputed per-point triple lists for incremental area updates, plus jittered lattice/edge-heavy warm starts and a top-3 crossover/merge phase; Gen 39 (0.74, 5.53s) added perpendicular guided moves and lattice/symmetric/ring/edge seeds with the same merge pattern. Both spent mid-range runtime efficiently (5–6s vs. the best's 24.5s).
- **Powell on smooth objectives outperforms SLSQP and L-BFGS-B on the same softmin family.** Gen 36 (Powell softmax top-k softmin, 0.63) vs. Gen 31 (L-BFGS-B active-set softmin, 0.69 close but slower-diversity-limited) vs. SLSQP variants Gen 32/40 (0.53 each). Powell's derivative-free robustness on the softmax-parametrized landscape is a consistent edge.
- **Structured multi-start seeds (lattices, rings, mirror-symmetric pairs, edge-heavy layouts) broaden basin coverage and appear in every top-4 scorer** (Gens 26, 29, 39). Mirror-symmetry exploitation (barycentric weight swaps for reflected point pairs) is retained in the best program's starts.

## Ineffective Approaches
- **Trivial initialization/indexing bugs still zero out otherwise-sound programs.** Gen 33 (`best_val` used before initialization), Gen 34 (`_project` assumes 2D but receives (K,n,2) batches), Gen 35 (ring index 30 out of bounds for a 30-element array) all scored 0.00 from single-line-scale defects — reinforcing that batch-shape handling and start-construction indexing are the highest-frequency failure points.
- **Hard clipping/projection plus overly-smooth softmin causes degenerate collapse.** Gen 38 (CMA-ES, 0.00) passed validation but returned min_area_normalized 0.00 — its p=60 softmin was too smooth to resist point coalescing and the final hard clip collapsed points onto edges; broad `except Exception` blocks silently masked the failure.
- **Escape/polish machinery attached to SLSQP softmin doesn't fix local-optima trapping.** Gen 32 and Gen 40 (identical 0.53, 0.02, 0.89s) show teleport/perpendicular-escape polish layered on a stuck SLSQP start yields no gain — the polish cannot recover from a bad basin, confirming the prior insight that global structure (starts, smoothness) matters more than local escape heuristics.
- **Fine-grained step decay and small K starve discrete searches.** Gen 29's feedback explicitly attributes its 0.03 plateau (vs. potential) to step decay ×0.65 on stall and K=24 sampling; greedy worst-triplet moves with rapid annealing keep converging prematurely.

## Implementation Insights
- **Guaranteed-feasible softmax parametrization (Gen 26) eliminates projection/repair code entirely** — and notably, both new programs that crashed did so inside projection helpers (Gen 34's `_project`) or start construction (Gen 35's ring), validating the best program's projection-free design. Gen 31's clip+renormalize simplex projection worked but adds complexity without score benefit.
- **Numerically robust softmin objective is the best program's key formula**: `temp*log(sum(exp(-(a - a.min())/temp))) - a.min()` with temperature annealing (1e-2→1e-4 × area_T) and per-stage Powell restarts from the previous stage's solution — this coarse-to-fine continuation is what Gens 31/36/38 replicate with less success (L-BFGS-B/CMA-ES optimizers).
- **Incremental area updates matter at scale.** Gen 29/39 precompute per-point triple lists so batched moves update only affected triangle areas — this is why they reach 0.74–0.76 in ~5s while full O(n³) rescan loops (prior Gen 27) burned 17s for 0.51.
- **Defensive final guard with rescue restart (Gen 26 pattern) remains correct** — but Gen 38's broad `except Exception` blocks show the guard must not silently swallow optimization failure into a degenerate result; always check min_area > 0 after optimization, not just before return.

## Performance Analysis
- **Score tiers confirmed and extended**: crash/bug programs (Gens 33/34/35: 0.00), degenerate-despite-valid programs (Gen 38: 0.00 with min_area 0.00), fast-but-trapped SLSQP/polish hybrids (Gens 32/40: 0.53), smooth softmax variants (Gens 36: 0.63, 31: 0.69), structured discrete search (Gens 39: 0.74, 29: 0.76), and the softmax-Powell best (Gen 26: 0.82).
- **min_area_normalized 0.03 is a hard ceiling across now ~20 evaluated programs** — no method (more restarts, annealing, polish, crossover, CMA-ES) has exceeded it. Gen 26's 0.82 lead over Gen 29's 0.76 at the same 0.03 area implies its combined-score advantage comes from other score components (consistency, validation robustness), and both are plateaued on the area metric.
- **Runtime–score correlation holds but with diminishing returns**: the best (24.53s → 0.82) and the 5–6s structured searches (0.74–0.76) cluster high, sub-second SLSQP hybrids stall at 0.53, and instant 0.01s runs signal total failure. However Gen 36's 10.6s bought only 0.63 — spending time isn't sufficient; the optimizer + parametrization choice governs the payoff.
- **Powell > L-BFGS-B > SLSQP on this landscape** (0.82 vs. 0.69 vs. 0.53 at comparable pipeline designs), and discrete incremental search (0.74–0.76) beats all gradient-based softmin variants except the Powell-softmax combination.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

# Recommendations for Future Mutations

1. **Add a fourth annealing stage at temp = 1e-5 × area_T followed by an exact-argmin polish.** Gen 26's ladder stops at ~4.3e-4, where the softmin still blends many near-worst triangles instead of sharpening onto the true minimum — likely a cause of the 0.03 area plateau. Append a final stage with the same `a.min()`-shifted overflow-safe formulation, then run a small Powell pass on only the 9 logit-coordinates of the argmin triplet (all other logits frozen), keeping the guarded-feasibility softmax parametrization intact.

2. **Reallocate Powell budget toward more diverse starts.** Reduce `maxiter` from 400 to ~150–200 at the coarse temperature (where precision is irrelevant) and grow the start pool from 7 to 15–20, emphasizing mirror-symmetric pairings (e.g., 4–5 mirror pairs plus centroid) since symmetry exploitation is credited in every top scorer. This addresses the 24.53s near-budget runtime while broadening basin coverage, which insights show matters more than per-start polish.

3. **Replace the uniform 165-triple softmin with a top-k softmin (k≈20).** Compute all areas, take the k smallest, and apply the temperature-annealed log-sum-exp over only those — a two-line change to `softmin_obj` that concentrates the gradient signal on binding constraints instead of diluting it across 145 irrelevant triples. Retain the `a.min()` shift for numerical safety. This is the direct analogue of Gen 29/39's worst-triplet targeting, but fused into the smooth Powell pipeline rather than a discrete search.

4. **Intersperse discrete worst-triplet refinement between annealing stages.** After each Powell stage, unpack the current points, identify the argmin triplet via the existing vectorized cross-product scan, and run ~20 cheap evaluations optimizing only that triplet's 9 logits against the softmin objective. This imports the runner-up paradigm's incremental, triple-aware efficiency (which reached 0.76 in 5s) while keeping global softmin-gated acceptance, potentially breaking the 0.03 plateau that neither pure approach has exceeded alone.

5. **Add a point-spread degeneracy guard before the final return, and make the rescue guard check *after* optimization, not just the fallback.** Compute pairwise minimum distances; if any pair is below ~0.02 (the Gen 38-style clustered-point local optimum that "feasible but degenerate" checks miss), split them by shifting one point's logits away from its nearest neighbor and re-run the last annealing stage. Critically, per the Gen 38 lesson, ensure the rescue restart verifies `min_area(cand) > 0.0` — and ideally `> 0.02` — on its *own* result rather than silently accepting a degenerate output, since broad `except Exception` handling has masked such failures before.
