A better strategy from Heilbronn optimization practice: (1) start from several non-degenerate, quasi-uniform structured layouts (triangular lattice perturbations) via deterministic multi-start; (2) make moves targeted — perturb one of the vertices of the currently-smallest triangle, which is far more effective than uniform random point selection at breaking collinearity bottlenecks; (3) use a proper geometric cooling schedule with a larger initial step size. All remains deterministic (fixed seeds per restart).
