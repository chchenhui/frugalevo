anneal_softmin_sharpness</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Apply the recommendation: anneal the soft-min sharpness k in Phase 1 from ~30 to ~120 instead of the fixed k=300 used in `_soft_obj`. A lower sharpness early makes the surrogate smoother (better exploration of the plateau-riddled Heilbronn landscape), while increasing sharpness late bridges the gap to the hard-min objective used in Phase 2, avoiding the abrupt objective switch that wastes Phase 2 iterations re-adapting. Keep frac at a smaller 0.05 (matching the winning pattern from earlier generations that used the smallest-triangle emphasis) and keep Metropolis-style acceptance unchanged in structure. Additionally, scale Phase 2's acceptance to be slightly tolerant of tiny decreases at high temperature is unnecessary; instead we simply pass the annealed k. We implement k annealing inline in Phase 1 by computing the surrogate with a k argument.
