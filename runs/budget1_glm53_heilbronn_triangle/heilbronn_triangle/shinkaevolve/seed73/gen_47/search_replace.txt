<DESCRIPTION>
Two targeted improvements:

1. **Multi-seed initialization.** The current heuristic hand-crafted seed can start the annealing in a poor basin. Known good Heilbronn configurations for n=11 place most points on the triangle boundary (vertices and edge subdivisions) with a few interior points. We add several structured candidate seeds (boundary-heavy layouts with different subdivisions and interior placements), evaluate each with the exact hard-min objective, and start the optimizer from the best one. This costs microseconds at module-call time but can shift the whole search into a better basin, directly raising min_area.

2. **Validation harness before return.** Following the recommendation from prior generations (Gen 35's syntax-error 0.00, Gen 7/27 exceptions), we add a lightweight self-check that the output has shape (11,2), finite values, distinct points, and lies within the triangle, falling back to a safe clipped configuration otherwise. Correctness dominates algorithmic gains; this guards the score against regressions.
