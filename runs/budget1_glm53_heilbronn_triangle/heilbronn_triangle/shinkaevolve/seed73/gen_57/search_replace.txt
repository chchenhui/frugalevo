diverse_seeds_with_screening

<DESCRIPTION>
The uniform feedback across the 0.03-tier programs is that all runs converge into local basins near triangular-lattice-seeded configurations. Two changes address this: (1) add two qualitatively different seeds — a "hex-ring" seed built from nested equilateral rings rotated 30° relative to the outer triangle (vertex directions + edge-midpoint directions at half radius), and a boundary-heavy seed with ~9 points on the triangle's edges; (2) replace the naive "pick the seed with the best raw min-area" selection with a two-stage screening: each seed gets a short 20-iteration soft-min anneal, seeds are ranked by a combined soft-screening score (softmin plus a small weight on the hard min), and only the best basin is promoted to the full expensive pipeline. This diversifies initialization while keeping the offline budget bounded and deterministic (fixed per-seed RNG streams).
