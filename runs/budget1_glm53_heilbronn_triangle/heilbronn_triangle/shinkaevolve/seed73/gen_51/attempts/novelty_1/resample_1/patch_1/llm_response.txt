Per the recommendation: expand multi-start to 10–13 seeds with a two-stage filter. Stage 1 runs only 20 `_soft_search` iterations per seed to cheaply rank them, then only the top 3 seeds proceed to the full pipeline (soft + hard refine). Adds perturbed variants of the winning-layout hex seed with noise σ ∈ {0.01, 0.05, 0.1} and a Dirichlet(2.0) barycentric sample. Runtime stays controlled because stage-1 screening is much cheaper than the full refine per seed, and eval_time currently reports 0.00s so headroom exists.
