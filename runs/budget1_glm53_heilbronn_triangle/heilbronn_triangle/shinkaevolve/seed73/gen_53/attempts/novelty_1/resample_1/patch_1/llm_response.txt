<DESCRIPTION>
Add a deterministic directed polish phase after Phase 4. It sweeps the ~10 smallest triangles in ascending area order; for each binding vertex it probes moves along the outward normal of the opposite edge (analytically: rotate the edge vector by 90°, oriented away from the vertex), with step-halving on failure. This targets exactly the binding constraints, complementing the existing stochastic polish (which Gen 31 showed fails by moving only within the minimal triangle). Random kicks (20% budget) retain exploration. For the smallest triangle {i,j,k}, moving vertex i along the outward normal of edge (j,k) increases that triangle's area at the maximal local rate (gradient direction of |cross|), so a halving search from step 0.01 converges quickly to any available improvement. Repeat sweeps until no sweep improves.

    # Sweep the k smallest triangles in ascending area order; for each
    # binding vertex, probe moves along the outward normal of the opposite

        improved = True
        sweep = 0

                for v_idx, o_a, o_b in ((i0, i1, i2), (i1, i0, i2), (i2, i0, i1)):
                    base = best.copy()
                    base_val = _min_area(base)

                        cand[v_idx] += rng_dir.normal(0.0, 0.002, size=2)
                        cand = _clip_to_triangle(cand)

                            break
                        step *= 0.5

        pass
