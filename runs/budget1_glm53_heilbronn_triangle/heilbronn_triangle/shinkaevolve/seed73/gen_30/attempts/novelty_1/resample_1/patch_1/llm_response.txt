<DESCRIPTION>
The current optimizer plateaus around a local optimum of the hard-min objective. Two targeted fixes, following the recommendation:

1. **Annealed soft-min**: The soft objective currently uses a fixed sharpness k=200 and frac=0.15. Sharpening the surrogate progressively (k annealed from 150 to 600) and widening the fraction to 0.20 lets Phase 1 lift more near-minimal triangles simultaneously and smoothly transition toward the hard-min Phase 2, rather than abruptly switching objectives.

2. **Kick-and-repolish**: After greedy polish converges to a hard-min local optimum, small random "kicks" (deterministic seed) applied to the points of the minimal triangle followed by re-polish escape shallow local optima. This is a well-known basin-hopping technique for the Heilbronn problem, since the objective is piecewise (the identity of the minimal triangle changes discretely).

def _soft_objective(areas, frac=0.20, k=300.0):
    # soft-min over the smallest `frac` of triangle areas (maximize)
    m = max(4, int(len(areas) * frac))
    smallest = np.partition(areas, m - 1)[:m]
    # log-sum-exp soft minimum with tunable sharpness k:
    # val = max + log(mean(exp(-k*(a-max))))/k  -> smooth lower bound on min
    mx = smallest.max()
    return mx + np.log(np.mean(np.exp(-k * (smallest - mx)))) / k

def _kick_repolish(pts, triples, rng, kicks=12, kick_size=0.01):
    """Basin hopping: perturb the minimal triangle's points, re-polish,
    keep the best hard-min configuration found."""
    pts, best = _greedy_polish(pts, triples)
    for _ in range(kicks):
        cand = pts.copy()
        _, areas = _min_triangle_area(cand, triples)
        kmin = int(np.argmin(areas))
        tri = triples[kmin]
        for j in tri:
            cand[j] += rng.normal(0.0, kick_size, size=2)
        cand = _project_inside(cand)
        cand, v = _greedy_polish(cand, triples)
        if v > best + 1e-14:
            pts, best = cand, v
    return pts, best
