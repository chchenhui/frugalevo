<DESCRIPTION>
Single-point perturbations cannot escape degenerate configurations where two near-minimal triangles share vertices (a "hinge"): improving one worsens the other. Following the recommendation, I extend the soft phase with a ~10% probability two-triangle group move that perturbs all vertices of the two smallest triangles simultaneously with independent per-vertex noise, plus a post-polish hinge kick that jointly displaces those vertices and re-polishes. All code is self-contained (triplet indices built inline) so no helper is needed and searches match the current file exactly.</DESCRIPTION>
