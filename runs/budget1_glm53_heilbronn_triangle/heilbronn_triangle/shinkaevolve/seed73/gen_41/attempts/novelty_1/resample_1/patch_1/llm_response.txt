<DESCRIPTION>
Two targeted improvements: (1) add more diverse seeds (Dirichlet sample and concentric-rings layout) so the multi-start layer explores more basins — the recommendation notes multi-start+Gen-32 combination is untested; (2) replace the single-point random moves in Phase 2 with a mix of single-point and group (2-3 point) moves, which historically beat single-point-only search (Gen 32 > Gen 28 via group moves); (3) polish ALL seed results with the greedy coordinate polish rather than only the best, since the polish can lift a suboptimal seed past the current best. Budget: adding 2 seeds raises cost ~60%, still within ~8.4s headroom because _all_areas is vectorized; we trim Phase 1 iterations slightly to compensate.
