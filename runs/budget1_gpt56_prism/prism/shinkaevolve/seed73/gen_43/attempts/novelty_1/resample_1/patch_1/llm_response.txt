<DESCRIPTION>
Add a targeted three-GPU ejection chain after all existing relocation, swap, and two-GPU exchange neighborhoods fail. A bottleneck model that cannot fit on a lower-pressure target may become feasible by moving one model from that target to a third GPU. The full chain is evaluated atomically, including all three changed KVPR values, and is accepted only for a strict maximum-KVPR improvement. This expands the search only in the constrained case where simpler neighborhoods are exhausted, preserving the current algorithm's low runtime while escaping memory-fragmentation local minima.
</DESCRIPTION>
