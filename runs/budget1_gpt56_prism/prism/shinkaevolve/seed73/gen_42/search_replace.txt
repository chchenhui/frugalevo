<DESCRIPTION>
Broaden the bounded swap and asymmetric-exchange neighborhoods with a few of the largest resident models in addition to the models with the highest individual KV-pressure contribution. The current ranking is effective for relieving demand-heavy bottlenecks, but it can miss a large, low-demand model that is necessary to exchange memory capacity and unlock a better feasible pressure balance. Keeping most pressure-ranked models preserves the existing objective focus and runtime bound, while reserving several candidate slots for large models improves fragmentation repair.
</DESCRIPTION>
