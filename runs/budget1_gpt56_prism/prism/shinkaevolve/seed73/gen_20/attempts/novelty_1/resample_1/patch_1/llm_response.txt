<DESCRIPTION>
Add memory-feasible two-model relocations to local search. Single moves and swaps cannot improve placements when a GPU needs to release a combined pressure/memory bundle before another GPU can accept it. Testing pairs from the currently most pressured GPUs keeps the additional search focused on actions that can reduce the minimax objective, while the existing lexicographic objective guarantees every accepted action is non-worsening for maximum KVPR and strictly improves the complete pressure vector.
</DESCRIPTION>
