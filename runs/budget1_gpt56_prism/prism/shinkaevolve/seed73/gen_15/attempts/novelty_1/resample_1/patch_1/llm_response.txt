<DESCRIPTION>
Add a small, controlled plateau-escape phase to the local search. When no strictly improving move or swap exists, the algorithm may perform up to two feasible actions that keep the maximum KVPR unchanged while strictly reducing KVPR on a current bottleneck GPU. This redistributes memory and load to escape fragmentation/local-minimum configurations, after which strict minimax improvement resumes. The plateau budget prevents cycles and keeps runtime bounded.
</DESCRIPTION>
