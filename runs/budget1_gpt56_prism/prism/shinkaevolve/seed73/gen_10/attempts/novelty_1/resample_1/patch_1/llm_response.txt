<DESCRIPTION>
Add memory-aware greedy starting orders based on each model's standalone KVPR contribution, `pressure / (GPU_MEM_SIZE - model_size)`. A model that is both large and high request-pressure is substantially harder to place late because its remaining KV cache capacity is small. The existing pressure-first and size-first starts do not directly capture this interaction. Trying these additional deterministic orders improves the chance that local search begins in a better feasible basin, while retaining the existing placement and improvement logic.
</DESCRIPTION>
