<DESCRIPTION>
Improve the greedy seed placements by comparing the complete sorted KVPR vector rather than only the projected maximum KVPR. When two candidate GPUs have the same projected maximum, reducing the next-highest pressures creates a more balanced state and gives local search more room to lower the true bottleneck later. This is especially helpful for tied bottlenecks, while preserving the existing memory packing/spreading tie policy only after the complete pressure objective is equal.
