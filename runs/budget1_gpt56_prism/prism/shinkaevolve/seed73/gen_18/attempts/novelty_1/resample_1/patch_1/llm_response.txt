<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bottleneck-focused two-model relocation neighborhood after ordinary moves and swaps have been considered. A minimax KVPR local optimum can require moving two models away from the most pressured GPU together: neither individual move may reduce the global maximum, while their combined removal can. Restricting this richer search to GPUs currently at the maximum KVPR keeps runtime low and directly targets the objective bottleneck. The operation remains memory-safe and is accepted only on a strict objective improvement, so it cannot cycle.
