<DESCRIPTION>
Extend the existing relocation local search with pairwise model swaps. A placement can be locally optimal for single-model moves because no destination has enough free memory, while exchanging a large low-pressure model with a smaller high-pressure model can rebalance both the KV demand and remaining memory. The search evaluates both feasible moves and feasible swaps, applies the best strict improvement to the maximum KVPR, and repeats until no such improvement remains. This preserves memory feasibility and directly optimizes the requested minimax objective.
</DESCRIPTION>
