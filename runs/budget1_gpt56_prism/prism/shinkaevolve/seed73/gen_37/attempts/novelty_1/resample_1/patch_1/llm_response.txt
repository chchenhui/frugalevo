pressure_aware_exchange_ranking
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Rank models considered for bounded swaps and asymmetric exchanges by their individual KVPR contribution after placement, rather than only raw demand. A large model with moderate demand can be essential to exchange because it sharply reduces remaining memory and therefore creates high pressure. The previous demand-first ranking can exclude such models from the limited exchange neighborhoods, preventing improvements that require trading memory-heavy models. This preserves the existing runtime bounds while making local search target the objective more directly.
</DESCRIPTION>
