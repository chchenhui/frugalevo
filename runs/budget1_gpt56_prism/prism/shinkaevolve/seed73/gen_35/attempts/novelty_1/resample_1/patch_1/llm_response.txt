<DESCRIPTION>
Add a strict two-model relocation neighborhood before swaps. A bottleneck GPU can sometimes be improved only by moving two complementary models together: neither individual move sufficiently reduces the maximum KVPR, while the pair produces a better globally sorted pressure vector. This preserves capacity constraints and accepts only lexicographically strict improvements, so it cannot cycle or worsen the maximum KVPR.
</DESCRIPTION>
