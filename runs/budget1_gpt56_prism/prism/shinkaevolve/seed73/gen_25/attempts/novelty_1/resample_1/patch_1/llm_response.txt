<DESCRIPTION>
Add the reverse bounded exchange neighborhood: replace one model on a bottleneck GPU with two models from another GPU. The existing two-for-one exchange mainly helps when a crowded GPU can export two models and import one. Its reverse explores complementary memory/load tradeoffs, especially when the bottleneck GPU has one high-demand model that should be moved away while receiving two lower-demand models. Both post-exchange memory capacities are checked exactly, and acceptance continues to require a strict lexicographic KVPR-profile improvement.
</DESCRIPTION>
