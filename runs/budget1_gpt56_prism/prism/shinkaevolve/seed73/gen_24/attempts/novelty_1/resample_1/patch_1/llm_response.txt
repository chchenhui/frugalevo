<DESCRIPTION>
Use the full sorted KVPR vector as the local-search objective rather than only the worst GPU KVPR. This preserves the primary minimax objective while accepting changes that keep the maximum KVPR unchanged but reduce secondary bottlenecks. Such layouts have better balance and can enable later moves that reduce the actual maximum. Strict lexicographic improvement prevents cycles.
</DESCRIPTION>
