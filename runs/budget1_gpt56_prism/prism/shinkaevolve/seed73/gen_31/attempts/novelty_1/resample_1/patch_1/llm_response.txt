<DESCRIPTION>
Add a targeted three-GPU ejection-chain neighborhood after direct moves, swaps, and two-for-one exchanges fail. A bottleneck model may be unable to move to a lower-pressure GPU solely because that GPU lacks sufficient free memory. This edit tries ejecting one of the smallest models from that target to a feasible third GPU, then atomically moves the bottleneck model into the capacity released on the target.

The candidate is evaluated using the existing lexicographic KVPR vector, so it is accepted only when it strictly improves the current placement. Restricting sources to bottleneck GPUs, targets to low-pressure GPUs, victims to the smallest few models, and third GPUs to low-pressure candidates keeps the additional search inexpensive.
</DESCRIPTION>
