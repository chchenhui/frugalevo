<DESCRIPTION>
Add objective-aware construction orders and a memory-feasible one-for-two exchange neighborhood. The existing greedy seeds can miss placements where a large high-pressure model must trade places with two smaller models; neither a single move nor a one-for-one swap can cross this capacity barrier. Evaluating such exchanges only after moves and swaps preserves the inexpensive successful search path while expanding its ability to escape fragmentation-driven local optima. The added seed orderings prioritize models whose individual placement leaves little KV-cache capacity, improving the starting basins considered by local search.
</DESCRIPTION>
