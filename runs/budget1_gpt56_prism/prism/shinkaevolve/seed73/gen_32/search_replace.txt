lexicographic_bottleneck_search
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace scalar-only local search with a lexicographic vector of all GPU KVPR values. This allows moves that reduce one of several tied bottleneck GPUs even when the maximum KVPR is initially unchanged, enabling subsequent moves to reduce the true maximum. Restricting searches to pairs involving a bottleneck also concentrates work on actions relevant to the objective.
</DESCRIPTION>
