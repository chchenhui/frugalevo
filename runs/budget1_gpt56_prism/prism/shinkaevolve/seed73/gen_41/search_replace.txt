<DESCRIPTION>
Add a narrowly scoped three-GPU eviction chain after the existing move and swap neighborhoods are exhausted. A model on a bottleneck GPU may be unable to fit on a lower-pressure GPU because of insufficient free memory. The new step tests evicting one model from that target GPU to a feasible third GPU, then placing the bottleneck model into the freed space. The entire chain is evaluated atomically against the maximum KVPR and is accepted only for a strict improvement. This preserves the inexpensive existing local search as the primary strategy while resolving common memory-fragmentation local minima.
</DESCRIPTION>
