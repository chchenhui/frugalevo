<DESCRIPTION>
Add a two-model relocation neighborhood when single moves and swaps cannot improve the maximum KVPR. A high-pressure GPU can often only be relieved by moving a pair together into available memory on another GPU; neither individual move may improve the objective because it creates an imbalanced intermediate placement. This is simpler and less expensive than broad multi-model exchanges, while directly targeting the maximum-KVPR bottleneck.
</DESCRIPTION>
