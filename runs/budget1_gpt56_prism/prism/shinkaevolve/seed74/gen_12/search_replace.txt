<DESCRIPTION>
The current local search can only improve the greedy basin it starts in, so the quality of its initial feasible packing is important. Add the two pressure-aware orderings that prioritize models with high isolated KVPR and high size/load pressure tradeoff. Also add deterministic rotated tie-breaking for each ordering. Rotations change which otherwise equivalent GPU receives early models, creating additional feasible basins without randomness or substantial runtime cost. The existing move/swap descent then selects the best resulting layout.
</DESCRIPTION>
