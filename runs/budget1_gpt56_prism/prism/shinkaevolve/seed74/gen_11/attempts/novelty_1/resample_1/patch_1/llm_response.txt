<DESCRIPTION>
Add a pairwise model-swap local search after relocation. Single-model relocation cannot improve placements constrained by fragmented GPU memory: moving a useful model may not fit anywhere, while exchanging it with a smaller or lower-pressure model can reduce the bottleneck KVPR and remain memory-feasible. The search accepts only strictly improving swaps, so it terminates and never worsens the returned placement.
