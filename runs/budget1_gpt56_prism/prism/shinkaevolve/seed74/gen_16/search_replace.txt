<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single intrinsic-pressure ordering with several complementary feasible packing seeds and retain the one with the lowest actual maximum KVPR before refinement. KVPR placement has two competing constraints: high-load models should be balanced, while large models must preserve memory for later placements. A single ordering can make an early irreversible fragmentation decision. The new seeds prioritize intrinsic pressure, model size, raw load, and transformed load-to-memory pressure, while every placement choice directly minimizes the projected global KVPR. The existing move, swap, and pair-move local search then refines a substantially stronger initial solution without changing its logic.
</DESCRIPTION>
