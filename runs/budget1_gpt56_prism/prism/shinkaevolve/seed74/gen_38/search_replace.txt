tighten_pressure_bound
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Use the actual maximum KVPR of every successful transformed packing to tighten the binary-search upper bound. The transformed threshold is only a sufficient capacity target, and a greedy packing can have substantial slack below it. Repacking at the measured KVPR gives the objective-aligned threshold another opportunity to construct a better layout. When that tighter packing succeeds, it becomes the search upper bound and is retained when it improves the verified placement score.
</DESCRIPTION>
