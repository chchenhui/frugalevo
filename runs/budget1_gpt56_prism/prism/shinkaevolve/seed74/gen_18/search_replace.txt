<DESCRIPTION>
Use the full sorted vector of GPU KVPR values as a secondary objective, rather than considering only the single worst GPU. This preserves the primary goal of minimizing maximum KVPR, while allowing local search to make moves or swaps that keep the maximum unchanged but reduce the next-highest pressures. Such moves improve balance and can expose later moves that reduce the bottleneck KVPR, avoiding local minima without accepting any degradation of the maximum pressure.
</DESCRIPTION>
