<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the threshold-feasibility packing portfolio with deterministic anti-fragmentation orderings and evaluate both first-fit and best-fit for every ordering. The effective-size ordering is the most directly aligned with a target KVPR, but equal or near-equal transformed sizes can still produce substantially different memory fragmentation. Raw size, load density, and intrinsic pressure orderings provide complementary constructions, while first-fit and best-fit explore opposite fragmentation policies. The best actual KVPR placement is retained, so these additional constructions cannot worsen the selected candidate.
</DESCRIPTION>
