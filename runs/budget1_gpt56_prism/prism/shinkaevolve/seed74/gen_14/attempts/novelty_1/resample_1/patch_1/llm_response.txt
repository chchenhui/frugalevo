<DESCRIPTION>
Use several inexpensive greedy construction orders before the existing relocation/swap improvement phase. The current construction prioritizes request pressure only, which can prematurely consume the spacious GPUs needed by large models. A size-first seed preserves packing flexibility, while a pressure-per-memory seed targets models most likely to create bottleneck KVPR. Each feasible seed is scored by its actual maximum KVPR, and the best is passed to the existing local search. This retains memory-feasibility safeguards and adds only a small constant amount of construction work.
</DESCRIPTION>
