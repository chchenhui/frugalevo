<DESCRIPTION>
Add a final local-search phase that relocates two models together from a bottleneck GPU. The existing single-model relocation rejects moves whose intermediate placement does not lower the global maximum KVPR, even when moving two models together would substantially reduce the bottleneck. Evaluating pair relocations preserves memory feasibility and accepts only strict reductions in the actual maximum KVPR, so it cannot degrade the result or cycle.
