<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded two-model relocation repair when ordinary single moves and swaps cannot improve the current placement. A local optimum can require moving two models off the bottleneck GPU simultaneously: neither model may fit or improve alone, while their combined removal frees enough memory pressure to lower the global maximum KVPR. The repair only examines pairs from GPUs currently attaining the bottleneck and evaluates feasible target combinations, preserving the existing fast greedy and steepest-descent structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
