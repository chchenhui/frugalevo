<DESCRIPTION>
Add transformed-pressure model orderings to the initial seed search. At the global lower-bound pressure estimate, a model consumes both request-pressure capacity and memory capacity; sorting by `load + pressure * size` prioritizes models that are hardest to place under the KVPR objective. This improves initial placements before relocation, swaps, and pair moves without increasing search complexity substantially.
</DESCRIPTION>
