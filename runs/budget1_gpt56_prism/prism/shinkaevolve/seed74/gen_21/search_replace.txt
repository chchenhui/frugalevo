diverse_pressure_aware_orders
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the transformed bin-packing seeds with a bounded set of deterministic pressure-aware and anti-fragmentation orderings. The existing transformed-cost, size, and load orders are retained. The additional rankings prioritize models that are difficult to combine with later models: high transformed cost relative to leftover GPU capacity, large models with high load density, and high load density generally. This improves the likelihood that `try_pack` finds a feasible assignment at a lower binary-searched pressure bound, without changing the feasibility condition or adding meaningful runtime.
</DESCRIPTION>
