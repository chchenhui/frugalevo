kvpr_aware_orderings
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add greedy initialization orders based on each model's standalone KVPR contribution, `weight / (GPU_MEM_SIZE - size)`. Unlike request-weight or weight-per-size ordering, this directly captures the nonlinear pressure caused by consuming model memory. Models that leave little KV-cache capacity are therefore assigned early, when the placement has the most freedom. Additional complementary orders emphasize large high-pressure models and improve the chance that the existing local search starts from a better feasible basin without changing feasibility behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
