<DESCRIPTION>
Evaluate local improvement from every feasible greedy construction rather than choosing only the best pre-improvement construction. A placement with a slightly worse greedy peak can lie in a much better relocation/swap neighborhood. Also add a small set of pressure-aware and anti-fragmentation ordering variants: model pressure relative to its residual memory, size-first density ordering, and load-density ordering. This improves robustness on instances where memory fragmentation dominates simple load balancing, while retaining deterministic behavior and small runtime.
</DESCRIPTION>
