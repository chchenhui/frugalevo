<DESCRIPTION>
Add a paired-model relocation neighborhood to the local search. Single moves and swaps cannot improve solutions where neither model can individually reduce the maximum KVPR enough, but moving two models together off a congested GPU can. The new operation is memory-feasible, evaluated against the same exact maximum-KVPR objective, and is applied only when it strictly improves the placement.
</DESCRIPTION>
