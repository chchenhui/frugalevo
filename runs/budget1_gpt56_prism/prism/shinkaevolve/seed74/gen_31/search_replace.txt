<DESCRIPTION>
Use the actual maximum KVPR of every successful effective-size packing to tighten the binary-search upper bound. A placement found at threshold `middle` is itself a proof that its measured peak KVPR is feasible, and that value can be substantially lower than `middle` because greedy bin packing leaves slack. Repacking at this tighter verified target can produce a better seed for local search. The best seed is retained according to the actual lexicographic pressure key instead of simply replacing it with the most recent binary-search candidate.
</DESCRIPTION>
