<DESCRIPTION>
Improve local-search decisions by minimizing the full sorted KVPR profile rather than only the current maximum KVPR. This preserves the primary minimax objective while breaking ties by reducing the next-highest pressured GPUs, which creates better states for later moves and swaps. It is deterministic, memory-feasible, and adds only small tuple construction overhead.
</DESCRIPTION>
