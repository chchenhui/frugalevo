<DESCRIPTION>
Add two complementary deterministic construction orders so the search explores more packing patterns before selecting the best initial KVPR profile. This is especially useful when high-pressure models and memory-heavy models must be interleaved differently.

Also add a strict-improvement two-step relocation chain after moves and swaps are exhausted. A chain can move one model out of an intermediate GPU to create room for a hotspot model, then place that hotspot model into the newly created space. This repairs memory-blocked placements that neither a single move nor a direct swap can improve, while preserving the requirement that every accepted change improves the complete sorted KVPR profile.
</DESCRIPTION>
