enable_best_relocations
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The relocation local-search loop computes and records an improved candidate profile, but never records the corresponding move in `best_move`. Consequently, every relocation round behaves as if no improving relocation exists and skips directly to swaps. Recording the move when its profile is better enables the intended best-improvement relocation phase, which can reduce the maximum KVPR without violating memory constraints before considering more expensive swaps.
</DESCRIPTION>
