<DESCRIPTION>
Add a two-for-one exchange neighborhood before the three-GPU chain search. A KVPR bottleneck can be caused by several high-demand small models sharing a GPU; relocating or swapping just one of them may not improve the maximum pressure. Exchanging two such models for one lower-pressure model can simultaneously rebalance demand and residual memory. The neighborhood is restricted to current bottleneck GPUs and small instances, preserving the existing fast execution behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
