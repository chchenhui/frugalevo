<DESCRIPTION>
Add a threshold-feasibility construction based on the KVPR inequality. For a target pressure `p`, a GPU is valid exactly when its assigned models satisfy `sum(weight + p * model_size) <= p * GPU_MEM_SIZE`. This transforms the nonlinear pressure constraint into a bin-capacity constraint.

The new construction binary-searches for a low feasible pressure threshold and packs transformed model sizes with several deterministic orders. Candidate GPU selection uses the requested stronger tie-break: projected maximum pressure, projected second-highest pressure, transformed remaining capacity, and physical remaining memory. The previous greedy and local-search candidates remain available, so this only adds stronger placements rather than replacing existing successful behavior.
