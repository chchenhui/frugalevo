<DESCRIPTION>
Add deterministic construction orders that explicitly prioritize models which consume a large fraction of a GPU’s remaining KV-cache capacity. A model near 80 GB sharply constrains future feasible placements, and placing it early avoids fragmentation that pressure-only and size-only orders can leave behind. The additional demand-per-residual-memory order also exposes placements that better balance KVPR for models whose pressure becomes expensive after their model memory is reserved.
</DESCRIPTION>
