<DESCRIPTION>
Add a two-step relocation search when no direct move or swap improves the bottleneck. A direct move can be blocked because the target GPU lacks memory, even though one of its models can be moved to a third GPU. This chained move unlocks target memory while evaluating the exact resulting global maximum KVPR, allowing the local optimizer to escape memory-fragmentation local minima without changing the initial placement strategy.
