two_for_one_exchange

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded asymmetric exchange neighborhood after relocations and one-for-one swaps fail. A bottleneck GPU may need to send two models away while receiving one smaller/lower-demand model; neither individual relocation nor a simple swap can represent this capacity-safe improvement. The search is restricted to currently maximal-pressure GPUs and modest model counts, so it directly targets the maximum KVPR without materially increasing runtime on larger instances.
</DESCRIPTION>
