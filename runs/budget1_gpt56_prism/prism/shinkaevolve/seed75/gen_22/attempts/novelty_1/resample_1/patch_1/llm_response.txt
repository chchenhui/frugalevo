lexicographic_local_search

<DESCRIPTION>
Use the complete sorted KVPR profile as the local-search objective instead of considering only the largest KVPR. A move, swap, or chain that keeps the current maximum unchanged but reduces the next-largest pressure is accepted. These plateau improvements can rebalance GPUs sufficiently to unlock a later move that lowers the actual maximum KVPR, while strict lexicographic improvement prevents cycling.
</DESCRIPTION>
