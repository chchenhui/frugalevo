add_pair_exchange_refinement
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Extend local refinement with a two-for-one exchange when no improving single move or single swap exists. Memory-constrained placements commonly reach local optima where a high-pressure model cannot move to another GPU unless it replaces two smaller models together. This exchange preserves memory feasibility while allowing the bottleneck GPU’s KVPR to decrease substantially. It is only evaluated after cheaper single-model operations fail, retaining the existing fast path and limiting extra search cost.
</DESCRIPTION>
