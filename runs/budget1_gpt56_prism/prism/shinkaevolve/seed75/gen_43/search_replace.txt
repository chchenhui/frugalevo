<DESCRIPTION>
Add a construction order derived from the KVPR feasibility transformation. For a candidate maximum pressure T, each GPU must satisfy `load + T * model_memory <= T * GPU_MEM_SIZE`; therefore a model’s effective packing weight is `demand + T * size`. Using the global lower bound on T as the estimate prioritizes models that are difficult in both request pressure and memory, reducing fragmentation that can prevent the greedy and local-search phases from reaching a lower bottleneck. This is an additional deterministic start, so existing successful starts and feasibility fallback remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
