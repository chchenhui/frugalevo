<DESCRIPTION>
Add one deterministic plateau-diversification step to the strict local search. When no improving move exists, a capacity-safe swap that preserves the maximum KVPR (within floating-point tolerance) can move the placement into a different local-search basin. Strict descent resumes immediately afterward, and only one such perturbation is allowed, preventing cycles or substantial runtime growth. Since the perturbation never increases the maximum KVPR beyond numerical tolerance, it preserves the primary optimization objective while enabling improvements unreachable through strictly improving one- and two-model exchanges.
</DESCRIPTION>
