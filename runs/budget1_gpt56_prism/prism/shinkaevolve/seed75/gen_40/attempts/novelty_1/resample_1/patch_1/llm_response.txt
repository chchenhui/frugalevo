residual_aware_orders
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add two deterministic model-arrival orders that explicitly account for residual-memory fragmentation. Large models become disproportionately difficult to place once any GPU has been partially filled, especially models whose size consumes a large fraction of the 80 GB GPU. The new orders prioritize this risk using size relative to residual capacity and a large-item-first key.

The existing candidate GPU selection and threshold feasibility logic remain unchanged, so every selected assignment still obeys both physical memory and transformed KVPR capacity. These additional starts only broaden the packing constructions considered at each threshold, improving the chance of finding a feasible packing at a lower maximum KVPR threshold.
</DESCRIPTION>
