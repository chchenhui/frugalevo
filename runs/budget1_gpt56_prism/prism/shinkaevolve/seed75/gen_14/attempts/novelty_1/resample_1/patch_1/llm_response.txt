<DESCRIPTION>
Extend local refinement with reverse two-for-one exchanges. A single relocation cannot improve placements where a bottleneck GPU must give up two smaller models in exchange for one larger model from another GPU to rebalance both memory and request pressure. The new neighborhood examines pairs only on current bottleneck GPUs, verifies both resulting memory capacities, and accepts only a strict lexicographic improvement of the sorted KVPR profile. This guarantees that the maximum KVPR never increases while allowing improvements that are unreachable through single-model moves.
