<DESCRIPTION>
Extend the bottleneck-reduction local search with model swaps. A single relocation cannot improve placements where each GPU lacks enough free memory for the beneficial incoming model, even though exchanging differently sized models would reduce the maximum KVPR. The added swap search considers exchanges involving the current bottleneck GPU, preserves strict positive remaining memory, and accepts only swaps that lower the global maximum KVPR.
</DESCRIPTION>

        best_max = ratios[source]
        best_action = None
