<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded two-step evacuation move when no improving relocation or swap exists. A bottleneck model can then move to a GPU that is currently full by first moving one of that target GPU's models to a third GPU. The complete three-GPU state is evaluated before accepting the action, so every accepted chain is memory-feasible and strictly lowers the global maximum KVPR.
</DESCRIPTION>
