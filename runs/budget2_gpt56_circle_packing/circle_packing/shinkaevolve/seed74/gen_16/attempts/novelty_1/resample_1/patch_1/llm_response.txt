<DESCRIPTION>
Polish every SLSQP center placement with the exact fixed-center linear program for radii. Once centers are fixed, all containment and non-overlap conditions are linear in the radii: each radius is bounded by its wall clearance and every pair satisfies r_i + r_j <= distance(i,j). SLSQP can terminate with radii below this LP optimum even when its centers are strong. Solving this small LP preserves the nonlinear center arrangement while recovering radius allocation lost through joint nonlinear optimization termination. The existing final global scaling is retained as a numerical safety certification.
</DESCRIPTION>
