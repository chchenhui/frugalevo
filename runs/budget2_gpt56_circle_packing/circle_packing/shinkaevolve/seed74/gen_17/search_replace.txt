<DESCRIPTION>
Use a continuation strategy rather than trusting one SLSQP pass from each grid perturbation.  Variable-radius packings have many nearly degenerate contact graphs: after the first joint center/radius solve, resolving the exact fixed-center LP exposes the preferred allocation of radius among contacts.  Restarting SLSQP from those improved centers with a larger iteration budget can release contacts that were prematurely frozen in the first solve.  The original seed and first-pass center arrangement are retained as candidates, so an unsuccessful nonlinear pass cannot discard a stronger LP arrangement.  A few extra asymmetric starts explore the important larger perturbation regimes while remaining far below the timeout.
</DESCRIPTION>

    # A range of perturbation sizes is useful because the desired solution
    # has an asymmetric boundary contact graph rather than a rigid grid.
    starts = [
        (0, 0.000),
        (1, 0.010),
        (6, 0.014),
        (11, 0.018),
        (14, 0.022),
        (9, 0.028),
        (3, 0.034),
        (12, 0.040),
        (7, 0.048),
        (15, 0.056),
    ]

    for seed, jitter in starts:
        centers0 = _initial_centers(seed, jitter)

        # Keep every continuation level as a candidate.  In particular, this
        # prevents an iteration-limited nonlinear solve from losing a good
        # fixed-center LP packing.
        first = _nonlinear_improve(centers0, maxiter=600)

        # Re-entering SLSQP after the first contact graph has formed is a
        # useful active-set continuation: the fresh LP sizing inside the
        # routine gives previously under-sized circles room to change which
        # neighbours they touch.
        second = _nonlinear_improve(first, maxiter=850)

        for centers in (centers0, first, second):
            radii = _safe_radii(centers, _radius_lp(centers))
            score = float(np.sum(radii))

            if score > best_score:
                best_score = score
                best_centers = centers.copy()
                best_radii = radii
