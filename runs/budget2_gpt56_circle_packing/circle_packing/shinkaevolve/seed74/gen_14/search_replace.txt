<DESCRIPTION>
Use the fixed-center LP solution as a warm start for one additional joint center/radius SLSQP refinement. The first nonlinear solve discovers a contact topology, while the LP extracts the best unequal radii supported by those centers. Reintroducing those LP radii into SLSQP lets the centers respond to the improved boundary and contact pressures, often opening small gaps that the original equal-small-radius initialization cannot exploit. A final LP then certifies the best radius allocation for the polished centers. The extra refinement is applied only to already strong candidates, limiting runtime.
</DESCRIPTION>
