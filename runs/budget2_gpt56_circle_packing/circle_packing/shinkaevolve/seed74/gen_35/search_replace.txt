<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the second-stage restart portfolio with moderate deterministic perturbations. The existing 0.004 displacement is primarily a numerical restart and usually retains the same active contact graph. For 26 disks, improved unequal-radius arrangements often require a boundary disk to exchange contacts with an adjacent staggered row; displacements around 0.01--0.02 are large enough to cross those basin boundaries while still preserving the successful layered topology. Every resulting candidate is jointly optimized and LP-polished, so unsuccessful perturbations cannot degrade the returned incumbent.
</DESCRIPTION>
