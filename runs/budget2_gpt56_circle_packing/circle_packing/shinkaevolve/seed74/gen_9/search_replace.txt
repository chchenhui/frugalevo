Use several deterministic feasible initial packings instead of relying on one square-lattice basin.  In particular, add staggered five-layer hexagonal seeds and small asymmetric perturbations of the square/interstice seed. Select the best feasible SLSQP result and polish it once more; the existing common radius scaling still guarantees strict final validity.
