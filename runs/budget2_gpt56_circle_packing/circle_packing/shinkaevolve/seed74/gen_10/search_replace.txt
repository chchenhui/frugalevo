<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the deterministic multistart portfolio beyond tiny lattice perturbations. The previous ±0.018 perturbations largely remain in the same local contact topology, which limits improvement from the staggered hexagonal seed. Add moderate and broad feasible perturbations so SLSQP can discover asymmetric boundary layers and alternative neighbour graphs.

For each perturbed center set, compute a uniform feasible starting radius from both wall clearance and the closest pair distance. This preserves a strictly feasible initial point even for broad perturbations, avoiding the numerical difficulty of beginning SLSQP with overlapping circles. The original seed and small perturbations are retained, while additional starts explore configurations capable of escaping the approximately 2.59 local optimum.
</DESCRIPTION>
