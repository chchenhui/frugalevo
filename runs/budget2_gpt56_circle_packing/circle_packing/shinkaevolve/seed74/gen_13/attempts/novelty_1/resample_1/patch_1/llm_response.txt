<DESCRIPTION>
Use a broader deterministic multi-start search with both the original rectangular-layer seed and a genuinely staggered hexagonal seed. The staggered seed better reflects the locally triangular contact graph expected in dense circle packings, while retaining the original seed preserves its successful boundary-adapted basin. Larger perturbations in later trials help escape nearly symmetric local optima. Feasible SLSQP iterates are retained even when SLSQP reports a non-success termination code, since the subsequent global radius scaling certifies strict feasibility.
</DESCRIPTION>
