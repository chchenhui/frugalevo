<DESCRIPTION>
Add an exact fixed-center LP radius polish to every nonlinear result, so selection is based on the true best radius allocation for each discovered contact geometry rather than the possibly suboptimal radius vector at SLSQP termination. Also diversify the first three deterministic starts with staggered and asymmetric layered variants, and increase the restart budget moderately. This preserves the joint nonlinear optimization while exploiting the fact that radii are a linear optimization problem once centers are fixed.

    def lp_polish(centers, fallback):
        """Maximize total radius exactly after center coordinates are fixed."""
        wall_limit = np.minimum.reduce((
            centers[:, 0],
            centers[:, 1],
            1.0 - centers[:, 0],
            1.0 - centers[:, 1],
        ))
        distances = np.sqrt(np.sum(
            (centers[pair_i] - centers[pair_j]) ** 2, axis=1
        ))
        polished = linprog(
            c=-np.ones(n),
            A_ub=pair_matrix,
            b_ub=distances,
            bounds=[(0.0, max(0.0, float(w))) for w in wall_limit],
            method="highs",
        )
        if polished.success and polished.x is not None:
            return np.maximum(polished.x, 0.0)
