<DESCRIPTION>
Replace squared-distance separation constraints with true Euclidean clearance constraints and their analytic Jacobian.  The objective is naturally expressed in radii, so constraints of the form `distance - r_i - r_j >= 0` have substantially better scaling near contact than squared constraints: center derivatives remain unit-sized rather than shrinking with inter-center distance, and radius derivatives are exactly `-1`.  This improves SLSQP active-set identification for the many nearly tangent contacts in a dense 26-circle packing, while preserving the same feasible set and public interface.  The Jacobian is vectorized over all pairs, avoiding the Python loop previously used to construct pair rows.
</DESCRIPTION>
