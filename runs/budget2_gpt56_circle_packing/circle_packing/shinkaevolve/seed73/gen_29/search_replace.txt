<DESCRIPTION>
Retain the strongest distinct first-pass solutions and re-optimize them from a microscopically deflated feasible state using substantially tighter tolerances. Different perturbations can converge to different contact graphs with nearly equal initial scores; a graph that is marginally worse after the broad pass can improve beyond the incumbent when its tangencies are settled accurately. Deflating radii before refinement avoids retaining tiny SLSQP constraint violations and lets the optimizer re-establish the active boundary and circle contacts cleanly.
</DESCRIPTION>
