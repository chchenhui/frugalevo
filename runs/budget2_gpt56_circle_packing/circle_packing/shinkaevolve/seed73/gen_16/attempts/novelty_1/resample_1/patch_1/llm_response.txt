<DESCRIPTION>
Use a continuation strategy before the fully unequal-radius solve.  The early stages impose a modest common lower bound on every radius, preventing SLSQP from immediately creating tiny sacrificial circles while it determines a well-spread contact graph.  Subsequent stages relax that floor and allow the unequal boundary/interior radii needed for the best total.

Also add several reproducible lattice perturbations.  Different small perturbations lead to different active-contact graphs, which is particularly important near the current 2.62 local optimum.  The final unrestricted stage retains the best result from every trajectory, so the added exploration cannot discard a stronger candidate found in an earlier run.
</DESCRIPTION>
