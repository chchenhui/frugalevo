<DESCRIPTION>
Use a broader set of asymmetric five-row topologies, including the structurally distinct layouts suggested by the row-population portfolio, and seed them with small unequal radii. Unequal initial radii help SLSQP leave the overly rigid equal-radius contact graph. Rank optimizer outputs by their strictly feasible common-radius-scaled objective rather than their possibly slightly infeasible reported objective; this prevents selecting a nominally larger result which loses more during the final validity correction.
</DESCRIPTION>

    def feasible_score(z):
        """Objective after the same common scaling used for final validation."""
        c = z[:2 * n].reshape(n, 2)
        r = np.maximum(z[2 * n:], 0.0)
        wall = np.minimum.reduce((c[:, 0], c[:, 1],
                                  1.0 - c[:, 0], 1.0 - c[:, 1]))
        scale = float(np.min(wall / np.maximum(r, 1e-15)))
        distances = np.hypot(c[pi, 0] - c[pj, 0], c[pi, 1] - c[pj, 1])
        scale = min(scale, float(np.min(

        return max(0.0, min(1.0, scale)) * float(np.sum(r))
