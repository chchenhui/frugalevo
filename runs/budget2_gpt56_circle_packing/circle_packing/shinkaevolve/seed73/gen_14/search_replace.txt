<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the staggered-start portfolio with structurally distinct five-row topologies, especially arrangements having adjacent six-circle rows.  The best 26-circle packings are sensitive to which layers carry the extra circles: moving a six-circle row changes the boundary contact graph and permits different unequal-radius defects near corners.  These seeds remain feasible at the existing radius 0.075, so the solver begins from valid dense configurations.  This is a targeted diversification of contact graphs rather than random perturbations, retaining the fast analytic constraint Jacobian and selecting the best feasible result.
</DESCRIPTION>
