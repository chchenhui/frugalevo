<NAME>
unequal_radius_multistarts
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace identical-radius starts by topology-aware unequal-radius seeds and increase the number of low-amplitude staggered perturbations. A uniform seed tends to preserve an artificial row symmetry during the early contact-graph formation. The new profiles give slightly more radius to the outer/corner-facing layer and slightly less to crowded interior-row circles, while remaining deliberately conservative and feasible. Independent deterministic perturbations also permit asymmetric boundary contact graphs, which are often superior for a finite square.
</DESCRIPTION>
