<DESCRIPTION>
Broaden the contact-graph search using deterministic geometric variants of every layered seed. Reflections are geometrically equivalent globally, but SLSQP follows coordinate- and constraint-order-dependent active-set trajectories, so reflected starts can reach different asymmetric unequal-radius optima. Alternating row displacements explicitly seed diagonal hexagonal contacts and boundary defects that a perfectly row-aligned layout may not discover. The displacement is only 0.01, leaving the small-radius common and direct starts safely feasible while providing a meaningful basin change.
</DESCRIPTION>
