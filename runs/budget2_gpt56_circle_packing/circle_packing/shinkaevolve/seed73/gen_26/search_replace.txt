<DESCRIPTION>
Add a final high-accuracy SLSQP polishing stage after the broad multi-start search. The existing starts efficiently identify a strong basin, but each is stopped at a moderate iteration budget. Re-solving only the winning feasible candidate with a substantially tighter tolerance can adjust the unequal boundary radii and near-tangent contact positions without multiplying the cost of every start. The feasibility check is retained, so an unfinished polishing iterate cannot replace the established packing.
