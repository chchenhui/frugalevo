<DESCRIPTION>
Retain the strongest three broad-search outcomes rather than only the single apparent winner, then re-solve each from a strictly feasible, slightly deflated version with substantially tighter SLSQP termination tolerance. Different initial perturbations can converge to nearly tied but distinct contact graphs; the broad pass identifies these cheaply, while the expensive precision pass is reserved for only the elite candidates. Deflating before the second solve removes any tiny numerical constraint violation and gives the active tangency graph freedom to settle to a higher-precision unequal-radius optimum.
</DESCRIPTION>
