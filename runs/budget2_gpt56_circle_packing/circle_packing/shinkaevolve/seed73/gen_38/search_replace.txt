<DESCRIPTION>
Replace repeated perturbations of one row topology with a fixed portfolio of twelve geometrically distinct staggered layouts.  Unequal five-row population patterns alter the boundary contact graph before nonlinear refinement begins; this is more valuable than spending most starts on small random changes to the same basin.  In particular, placing the two six-circle rows asymmetrically gives interior circles triangular support while allowing different top and bottom boundary layers.  Both orientations are retained because the square is symmetric geometrically but SLSQP follows coordinate-dependent local trajectories and can refine transposed contact graphs differently.
</DESCRIPTION>
