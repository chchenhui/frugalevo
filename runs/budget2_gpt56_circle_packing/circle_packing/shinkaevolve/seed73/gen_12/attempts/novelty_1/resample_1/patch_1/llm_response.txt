<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the radius of the staggered layered initialization from 0.035 to 0.075. The existing staggered rows have minimum center separation 0.17 in the six-circle rows, so equal radii of 0.075 are feasible with positive clearance. Starting from a dense feasible configuration gives SLSQP an active-contact structure immediately, rather than requiring it to grow 26 nearly zero circles through a highly nonconvex search. This layered seed better represents the locally hexagonal interior geometry expected in a strong 26-circle square packing while retaining asymmetric boundary slack for radius variation.
</DESCRIPTION>
