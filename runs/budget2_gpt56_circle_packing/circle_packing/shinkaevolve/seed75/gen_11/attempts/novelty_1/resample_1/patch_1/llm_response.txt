<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the deterministic multistart strategy rather than relying on four closely related sinusoidal perturbations of one five-row topology.  The sum-of-radii problem has many locally optimal contact graphs: the existing 5/6/5/6/4 layered seed is useful, but a six-row truncated triangular lattice can converge to a distinct boundary-contact pattern.  Retaining all original starts guarantees no regression from the prior search, while adding reproducible independent perturbations around both seeds gives SLSQP substantially more opportunities to discover an asymmetric packing near or above the 2.635 reference.  The number of variables and constraints remains small, and the added starts are well within the evaluator timeout.
