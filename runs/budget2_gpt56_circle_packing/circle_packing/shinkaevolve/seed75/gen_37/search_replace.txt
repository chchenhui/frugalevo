<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce independently asymmetric left and right row margins, then broaden the high-value five-row search family with defect partitions and their vertical reversals. Unequal-radius optima are sensitive to which circles acquire side-wall contacts; symmetric margins can repeatedly funnel restarts into reflection-equivalent contact graphs. The added layouts preserve 26 circles while testing wide-row and short-row defects in different boundary layers. A small deterministic phase displacement further distinguishes otherwise similar starts without endangering the conservative initial radii.
