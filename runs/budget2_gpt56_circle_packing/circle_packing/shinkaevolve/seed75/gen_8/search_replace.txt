<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a genuine staggered-row seed geometry and broaden the set of row-count topologies.  The previous seed used aligned x-coordinates despite describing itself as hexagonal; small alternating row shifts provide different contact graphs for the nonlinear optimizer, particularly near the vertical boundaries.  Additional reflected and six-row layouts explore boundary-layer configurations where smaller wall circles can permit larger interior circles.  This remains inexpensive because each optimization has only 78 variables and the existing analytic Jacobian is retained.
