<DESCRIPTION>
Add row-level translations and vertical band deformations to the layered seeds. Independent point jitter mainly perturbs an existing contact graph, whereas a coherent displacement of an entire row can change which circles touch neighboring rows and walls. The deformation preserves row order and remains small relative to the seed spacing, but explores asymmetric layered contact topologies that are especially relevant for unequal-radius optima near the square boundary.

    # nearly fixed vertically, since their eventual wall contacts are useful.
    row_phase = 0.73 * seed_number
    row_shift = rng.uniform(-0.012, 0.012, size=row_count)
    gap_shift = rng.uniform(-0.008, 0.008, size=row_count)
    if row_count > 1:
        edge_weight = np.sin(np.pi * np.arange(row_count) / (row_count - 1))
        gap_shift *= edge_weight

    for row, count in enumerate(row_sizes):
