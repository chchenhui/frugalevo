<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the nearly identical three local starts with a deterministic ten-start continuation search. The equal-radius hexagonal lattice is a strong basin, but the best known 26-circle configurations require unequal boundary circles and small asymmetric changes to the contact graph. Several moderately perturbed, reduced-radius starts give SLSQP room to alter that graph; later starts are seeded from the best solution already found, which efficiently refines promising asymmetric arrangements. Analytic Jacobians are retained, and the larger search remains comfortably below the timeout based on the prior optimized implementation.
