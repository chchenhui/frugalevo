<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a genuinely staggered, truncated-hexagonal initialization rather than aligned rows.  Alternating row offsets give the optimizer contact graphs closer to dense triangular-lattice packing while preserving generous initial feasibility margins.  The offset magnitude and jitter are varied deterministically across seeds, allowing both nearly regular and slightly asymmetric boundary configurations.

Expand the multistart set with all placements of the six-circle row among five rows, additional six-row layered topologies, and repeated high-quality topologies with independent perturbations.  Boundary effects are important for unequal-circle optima, so these alternatives expose SLSQP to distinct local contact structures while retaining the same public interface and inexpensive analytic optimization.
</DESCRIPTION>
