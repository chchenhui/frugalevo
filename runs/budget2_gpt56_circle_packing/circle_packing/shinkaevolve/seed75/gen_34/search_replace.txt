<DESCRIPTION>
Use count-dependent row margins to make four-, five-, and six-circle rows begin near their natural triangular-lattice horizontal spacing. This avoids initially spreading short rows unrealistically across the whole square, which can trap SLSQP in weaker boundary contact graphs.

Increase the restart budget modestly and replace uniform incumbent perturbations with deterministic contact-guided mutations. The restart identifies circles with the least clearance to a wall or another circle, then perturbs only those contact-critical circles in varied directions while shrinking the incumbent radii. This is intended to change a small number of active contacts without destroying the good bulk hexagonal structure already found.
</DESCRIPTION>
