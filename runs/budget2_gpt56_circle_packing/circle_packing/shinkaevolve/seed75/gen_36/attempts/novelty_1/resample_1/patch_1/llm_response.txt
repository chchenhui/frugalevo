<DESCRIPTION>
Retain several certified local optima from the inexpensive topology search, then perform a targeted high-precision restart phase from the strongest geometries.  Each restart begins with radii reduced by 1.5%, creating feasible slack while preserving the contact graph discovered by the first solve.  This is substantially more effective than adding more random seeds: near an unequal-circle optimum, SLSQP can use the slack to alter wall contacts and recover the small improvements that are otherwise hidden by its feasibility tolerance.  The pool is capped to keep runtime predictable.
</DESCRIPTION>
