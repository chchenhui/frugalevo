<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the deterministic multi-start set with cyclic boundary-defect variants. For 26 circles, five horizontal layers remain effective because they permit relatively large vertical spacing, while distributing the unavoidable extra circle as either one six-circle row or two six-circle rows plus one four-circle row changes which boundary circles can grow. These variants are not equivalent for SLSQP because the stagger offsets and active-contact graph differ. Increasing the iteration allowance slightly lets the promising additional starts settle into their local contact patterns while remaining far below the timeout.
</DESCRIPTION>
