<DESCRIPTION>
Expand the deterministic multistart family with additional row-count defects and reversals. For unequal-circle packings, the best local optimum is strongly affected by where the extra circle in a six-circle row, or the deficit in a four-circle row, is introduced relative to the square boundary. Although some row-count patterns are geometrically related by reflection in an ideal lattice, the deliberately asymmetric sinusoidal seed perturbations and phases make them distinct SLSQP basins. This preserves the existing explicit constructor and feasibility projection while increasing the chance of reaching the known approximately 2.635 configuration.
</DESCRIPTION>
