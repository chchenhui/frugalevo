<DESCRIPTION>
Add exact constraint derivatives and replace the final SLSQP radius allocation by the fixed-center linear-program optimum. The geometric optimization determines a contact graph, but its terminating radius vector is often not the best allocation on that graph: the LP can deliberately shrink a poorly placed boundary disk and distribute the released clearance to several neighbors. Analytic derivatives make the existing multistart optimization more reliable and leave ample time for a short second polish from that LP-optimal allocation. The strict existing common-scale polish remains the final validity certificate.
</DESCRIPTION>

        """Maximize radius sum exactly after the centers have been chosen."""
        wall = np.minimum.reduce((
            c[:, 0], c[:, 1], 1.0 - c[:, 0], 1.0 - c[:, 1],
        ))
        distances = np.linalg.norm(c[ii] - c[jj], axis=1)
        try:
            result = linprog(

                A_ub=pair_matrix,
                b_ub=distances,

                method="highs",
            )
            if result.success:
