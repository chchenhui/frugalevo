<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed final Adam polish with an adaptive late-stage continuation.  At this stage the objective is controlled by a small and changing set of closest/farthest contacts, so a fixed momentum and learning rate can oscillate after an active-pair switch.  The revised polish increases sharpness gradually, evaluates the true nonsmooth ratio every 25 iterations, and uses those checkpoints to damp momentum and reduce the step size after regressions.  Improvements retain the current scale, allowing productive motion to continue.  This directly optimizes the evaluator’s exact squared ratio while retaining the existing strong multi-start result as the initial configuration.
</DESCRIPTION>
