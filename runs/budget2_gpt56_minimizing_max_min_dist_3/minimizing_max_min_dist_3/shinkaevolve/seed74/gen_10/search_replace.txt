<DESCRIPTION>
Use the first restarts as independent basin discovery, then devote additional deterministic restarts to perturbing and re-optimizing the best configuration found so far.  This is effective for this nonsmooth packing problem because high-quality arrangements usually require fine adjustment within a promising basin rather than only more unrelated random initializations.

Also sharpen the final soft minimum/maximum temperature and reduce the late step size.  The broader early temperature still provides stable global movement, while the lower final temperature more accurately represents the actual active closest and farthest pairs used by the evaluator.
</DESCRIPTION>
