<DESCRIPTION>
Add a deterministic final polishing stage to the best annealed configuration. The existing search uses exact minimum-distance selection but its random single-point moves become inefficient near a contact-rich optimum. The new phase forms a smooth, increasingly sharp approximation to the shortest-pair and diameter-pair classes, differentiates their logarithmic ratio, and performs a small normalized line search using the evaluator’s exact squared-ratio score for acceptance. This preserves only genuine improvements while allowing coordinated motion of all active contacts.
