<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single annealing path with a deterministic portfolio of continuation schedules across restarts.  Maximin packing objectives have many basins whose useful contact graphs can emerge at different soft-extrema sharpness rates: the original schedule remains available, while other starts retain broader forces longer before concentrating on close/far contacts, or deliberately hold an intermediate sharpness before a final rapid refinement.  Since every result is still compared with `exact_ratio`, only genuinely improved candidates survive.  This changes no invariances or output contract and adds negligible evaluation cost.
</DESCRIPTION>
