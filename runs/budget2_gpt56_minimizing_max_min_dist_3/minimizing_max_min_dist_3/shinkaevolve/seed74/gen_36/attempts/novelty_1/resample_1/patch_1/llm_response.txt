<DESCRIPTION>
Blend the existing smooth-extrema gradient with a bounded exact active-contact subgradient once the continuation becomes sharp. The smooth objective is valuable while contact classes are changing, but at high beta it can still assign meaningful force to pairs that are no longer true minimum or diameter constraints. The added gradient averages all pairs within 1.5% of the exact minimum and maximum squared distances, making late polishing better aligned with the evaluator’s exact ratio while avoiding dependence on an arbitrary single tied contact. The blend is activated gradually as beta rises, retaining the current broad-basin behavior during early optimization.
</DESCRIPTION>
