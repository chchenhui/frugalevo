<DESCRIPTION>
Add an adaptive rollback mechanism to the refinement loop. The soft-extrema surrogate can continue moving after the true nonsmooth min/max ratio has peaked, especially in the late sharp phase where a small change in the active contact graph can cause a substantial evaluator regression. The existing code retains the best returned configuration, but it still spends subsequent iterations evolving inside a deteriorating basin.

The edit tracks consecutive meaningful regressions at exact-objective checkpoints. After two such regressions, it restores the last exact-best configuration, clears momentum, reduces the remaining step scale, and slightly reduces the effective sharpness. This preserves productive continuation while preventing accumulated momentum from carrying the search far from its best contact structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
