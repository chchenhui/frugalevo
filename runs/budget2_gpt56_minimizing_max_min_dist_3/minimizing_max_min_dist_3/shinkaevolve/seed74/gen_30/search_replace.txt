<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the uniform full-cost restart loop with a two-tier search. The existing code spends nearly identical effort on every perturbation of one antipodal seed, even though most starts do not enter a competitive basin. The new approach performs a cheaper continuation pass on a substantially larger and more diverse population, including both gentle symmetry-breaking perturbations and several broad perturbations. It records each candidate’s best exact ratio, selects only the strongest survivors, and gives those survivors a longer high-temperature/contact-focused continuation.

This reallocates approximately the same optimization budget toward basin discovery and survivor polishing. It also avoids ranking candidates solely by their final smooth-objective state: each candidate is ranked by the exact evaluator ratio encountered during its first stage.
</DESCRIPTION>
