<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the final isotropic random-kick hill climber with exact-ratio-guided batched proposals. The soft-extremum gradient identifies directions that simultaneously expand likely closest contacts and contract likely diameter contacts, while strict hard-ratio acceptance preserves the evaluator’s true nonsmooth objective. Each batch includes a deterministic force proposal, force-plus-tangent-noise proposals to permit contact-graph exchanges, and individual-point moves for localized contact corrections. This uses the smooth information retained from the continuation search instead of discarding it during the final polish.
