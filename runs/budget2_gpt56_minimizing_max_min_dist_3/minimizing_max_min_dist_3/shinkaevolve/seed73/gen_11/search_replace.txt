<DESCRIPTION>
Exploit the expected central symmetry of the strong 14-point diameter-packing candidate.  A centrally symmetric 14-point configuration is determined by seven directions and their negatives, while retaining the relevant optimum class for the known 7-line/projective packing formulation.  Projecting each iterate back onto this antipodal subspace removes asymmetric optimization noise and makes the closest-pair contact graph substantially easier to equalize.

The final softmin/softmax continuation is also sharpened.  Once the iterate is in the symmetric packing family, a higher final inverse temperature better approximates the evaluator's true nonsmooth min/max ratio and resolves small residual inequality between active contacts.
