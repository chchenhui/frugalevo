<DESCRIPTION>
Add a final constrained epigraph solve for the exact objective.  After the smooth and contact-set stages have identified a strong contact graph, rescale its closest distance to one and minimize an explicit squared-diameter variable subject to every pairwise squared distance being at least one and no greater than that variable.  This directly solves the evaluator’s squared ratio objective locally, avoids soft-contact approximation error, and retains the original candidate unless the returned configuration has a strictly better true score.
