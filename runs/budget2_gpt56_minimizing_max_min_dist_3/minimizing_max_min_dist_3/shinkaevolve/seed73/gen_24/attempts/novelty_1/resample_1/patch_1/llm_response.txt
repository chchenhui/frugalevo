<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the final softmax-only polishing stage with an exact contact-set method. At a near-optimal packing, the useful geometry is concentrated in several tied or nearly tied shortest and diameter pairs; a very sharp softmax can arbitrarily emphasize one of them. The replacement explicitly forms both active sets, pushes endpoints of short contacts apart, pulls endpoints of diameter contacts together, and accepts only strict improvements of the evaluator’s actual squared ratio. Small deterministic tangent perturbations are tested alongside the contact direction, allowing contact-graph changes that a single smooth gradient cannot initiate. Since every accepted candidate is compared against the exact score, this cannot discard the previously found packing.
</DESCRIPTION>
