<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the number of independently seeded contact-graph basins while reducing the long, redundant continuation tails spent on each basin. The diameter-packing objective has many symmetric and locally stable graphs; more independent starts improve the chance that the final SLSQP epigraph solve receives a strictly better feasible graph. The retained continuation still reaches the same broad and sharp beta ranges, and the final hard-constraint optimizer remains responsible for high-precision active-contact refinement.
</DESCRIPTION>
