<DESCRIPTION>
Add a final two-pass SLSQP diameter formulation after the existing smooth and exact-score searches.  The objective can be written with the minimum squared separation fixed to one: minimize the squared diameter `q` subject to every pair satisfying `1 <= d_ij^2 <= q`.  This directly matches the evaluator ratio, unlike the smooth contact surrogate.  Rescaling between passes reinitializes the active set at an exactly normalized minimum distance, and the final candidate is retained only when its measured exact ratio improves the incumbent.  Analytic constraint Jacobians keep the small constrained solve practical.
