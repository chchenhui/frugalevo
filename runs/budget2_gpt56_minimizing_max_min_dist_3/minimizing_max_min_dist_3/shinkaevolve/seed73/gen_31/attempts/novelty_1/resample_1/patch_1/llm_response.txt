<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the final softmax-only backtracking stage with an explicit active-contact method. The exact objective is nonsmooth: at a good packing, several shortest pairs and several diameter pairs are usually tied, so a softmax can dilute the useful direction or choose an unstable subset of ties. The replacement forms separate near-shortest and near-diameter contact sets, applies a balanced force that expands the short contacts while contracting the diameter contacts, and periodically proposes tangent random directions to permit contact-graph exchanges. Every proposal is still accepted only when the evaluator’s exact squared ratio strictly improves.

The contact bands cycle from narrow to wider tolerances. Narrow bands sharpen an established active graph; wider bands allow nearly active constraints to participate before they become limiting. Removing translation and radial-scale components from random proposals preserves the meaningful degrees of freedom.
</DESCRIPTION>
