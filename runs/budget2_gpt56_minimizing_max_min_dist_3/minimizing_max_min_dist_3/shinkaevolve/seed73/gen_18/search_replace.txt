<DESCRIPTION>
Augment the fixed-diameter repulsive relaxation with an explicit far-contact attraction term. The current force only increases the soft minimum distance, relying on renormalization to handle the diameter. Near the final high-beta regime, this can leave the active diameter pair poorly balanced against the nearest-neighbour contact graph.

The added soft-max weight identifies current and near-current diameter pairs. Attracting those pairs with a scale proportional to the current squared ratio supplies the missing derivative of a soft minimum-distance divided by soft diameter objective. This provides intermittent contact-graph balancing throughout the late continuation without changing the exact SLSQP formulation used for final certification and polishing.
</DESCRIPTION>

            z = -beta * dsq
            z -= z.max()
            w = np.exp(z)
            w /= w.sum()

            # The complementary soft maximum keeps the current diameter
            # contacts from expanding while closest contacts are repelled.
            # This is the gradient balance for a soft min-distance / diameter
            # ratio and becomes an active contact-graph refinement at high
            # inverse temperature.
            z_far = beta * dsq
            z_far -= z_far.max()
            w_far = np.exp(z_far)
            w_far /= w_far.sum()
