<DESCRIPTION>
Replace purely instantaneous soft-min/soft-max force averaging with persistent, proximity-weighted contact coefficients. The exact objective is governed by a small contact graph, but smooth weights can be diluted by pairs that briefly enter a soft band while the configuration is moving. This change keeps exponential proximity weighting while maintaining an exponential-memory contact signal for near and far pairs. Persistent limiting pairs therefore receive stronger force, particularly during the late narrow-temperature stages, while newly violated pairs still receive immediate weight through their proximity signal.
