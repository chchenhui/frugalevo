<DESCRIPTION>
Use shifted log-sum-exp evaluations for the soft minimum and soft maximum. The weights were already stabilized, but the scalar soft extrema were computed from unshifted exponentials. At late continuation temperatures, or from a dispersed random start, `exp(beta * dsq)` can overflow and make `soft_max` infinite, eliminating the useful diameter-gradient term. The shifted formulas are mathematically identical, remain finite, and preserve informative gradients throughout all starts and annealing iterations.
</DESCRIPTION>
