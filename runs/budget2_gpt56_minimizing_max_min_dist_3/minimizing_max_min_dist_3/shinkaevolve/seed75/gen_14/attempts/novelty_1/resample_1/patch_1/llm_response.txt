<DESCRIPTION>
Exploit the strong likelihood that the best diameter packing is antipodal, since antipodal spherical codes naturally fix the diameter while distributing nearest-neighbor contacts uniformly. Replace unconstrained random starts with exact antipodal starts. Also reallocate the optimization budget using a short, diverse screening continuation followed by substantially sharper refinement only for the best survivors. This retains basin diversity while allowing the promising contact graphs to converge under higher soft-min/soft-max sharpness, rather than spending the full budget on clearly inferior starts.
</DESCRIPTION>

        seeds.append(np.vstack((directions, -directions)))
