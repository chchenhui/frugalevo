<DESCRIPTION>
Preserve strong intermediate contact graphs from the broad continuation and branch late refinements from them, rather than refining only the single globally best exact checkpoint. Finite best-packing configurations can pass near several asymmetric basins during annealing; a configuration which is slightly inferior at 65%, 80%, or 92% can improve more under a sharp high-beta continuation than the final state of its original trajectory.

The edit records exact-score checkpoints, selects the strongest ones after the global phase, and performs paired deterministic/noisy refinements from each. The final added beta stage concentrates force on the true limiting pairs, improving local equalization of minimum-distance and diameter contacts.
</DESCRIPTION>
