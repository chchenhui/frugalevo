<DESCRIPTION>
Replace equal full-budget optimization for every seed with staged survivor selection. The initial low and medium sharpness stages are sufficient to identify promising contact structures using the exact squared-distance ratio. Only the strongest four candidates are then given a substantially longer high-sharpness continuation, concentrating computation where it can improve the active minimum and maximum distance constraints. This preserves reproducible seed diversity while allowing sharper late optimization than the previous uniform schedule.
</DESCRIPTION>
