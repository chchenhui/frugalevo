<DESCRIPTION>
Increase global basin coverage while holding the main continuation-force budget essentially constant: the two Fibonacci seeds differ only by a rigid rotation, so additional genuinely random starts are more valuable than very long trajectories from a few basins. Record each trajectory's own strongest exact checkpoint, rather than only the globally best configuration. Then replace repeated perturbations of a single basin with survivor-based reannealing of the strongest independent basins. This better explores competing asymmetric contact graphs, while the final high-beta stages still provide accurate local refinement.
</DESCRIPTION>
