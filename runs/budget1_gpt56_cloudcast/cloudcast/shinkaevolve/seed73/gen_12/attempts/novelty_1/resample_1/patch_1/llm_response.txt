relay_aware_greedy_fallback
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the large-fan-out fallback with a relay-aware expansion heuristic. Instead of attaching only the nearest pending destination, this evaluates every reachable relay as a possible fan-out point and incrementally adds its cheapest downstream destination paths. The selected candidate minimizes newly introduced unique-edge cost per destination reached, so one paid ingress path can serve multiple destinations. This better models cloud broadcast transfers over parallel multi-network topologies while retaining a polynomial planning cost for workloads too large for exact Steiner DP.
</DESCRIPTION>
