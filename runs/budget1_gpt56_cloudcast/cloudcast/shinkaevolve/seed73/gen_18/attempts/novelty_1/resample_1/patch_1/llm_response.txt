<DESCRIPTION>
Construct the scalable shared-tree solution first and use its unique-link cost as an incumbent upper bound for the exact directed Steiner solver. Because all valid transfer costs are non-negative, no DP subtree whose cost already reaches the incumbent can contribute to a cheaper final broadcast tree. This safely prunes merge and reverse-relaxation states, allowing the exact solver to cover twelve destinations rather than eleven while retaining the greedy topology whenever exact search cannot improve it. This increases opportunities to find lower-cost shared relay trees on larger multi-cloud broadcasts without sacrificing a known feasible solution.
</DESCRIPTION>
