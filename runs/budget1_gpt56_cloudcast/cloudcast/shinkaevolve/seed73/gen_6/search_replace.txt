<DESCRIPTION>
Strengthen the large-fan-out fallback where exact Steiner dynamic programming is intentionally avoided. The previous greedy method connects only one destination at a time, so it can miss a cheap relay that accepts one ingress transfer and then fans out to several destinations. This edit precomputes low-cost relay-to-destination paths and, at every iteration, evaluates both single-destination and two-destination relay bundles.

Candidates are scored by incremental union-edge cost per newly served destination. Existing tree edges have zero marginal cost, so the heuristic explicitly prefers paths that overlap previously installed transfers. Pair bundles model parallel fan-out from a relay and avoid paying redundant ingress links separately for destinations that can share them. The resulting selected graph remains rooted at the source, and the existing output logic continues to assign the shared routes to every partition.
</DESCRIPTION>
