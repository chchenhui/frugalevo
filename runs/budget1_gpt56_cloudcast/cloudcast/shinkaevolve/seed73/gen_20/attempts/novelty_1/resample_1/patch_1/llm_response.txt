<DESCRIPTION>
Extend exact Steiner optimization to moderately larger destination sets by using the best greedy shared-tree candidate as an incumbent upper bound. The exact dynamic program can safely discard split and path-relaxation states whose accumulated union cost already exceeds a known feasible broadcast tree, because all link costs are non-negative. This preserves optimality whenever the exact search completes while reducing unnecessary work and improves plans that previously fell into the heuristic-only branch for 16 terminals.
</DESCRIPTION>
