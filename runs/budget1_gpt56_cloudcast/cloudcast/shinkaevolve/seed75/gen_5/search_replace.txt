<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the per-destination detour guard when growing the broadcast tree. The optimization target is the total cost of unique network edges used to distribute the input, not the individual unicast path length to each destination. Once a transfer reaches any tree node, extending from that node only incurs the cost of newly installed edges; rejecting a low incremental-cost branch because its end-to-end route is longer can discard the most economical multicast plan.

The source shortest path remains implicitly available as a candidate at every iteration, so all reachable destinations can still be attached. Selecting by incremental edge cost directly aligns the greedy expansion with minimizing redundant cross-cloud transfers.
</DESCRIPTION>
