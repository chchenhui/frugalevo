<DESCRIPTION>
Add a cost-preserving cleanup pass after the Steiner/greedy edge set is selected. Reconstruction from dynamic-programming merge states can retain alternate forwarding edges that are no longer needed after their paths are unioned. Removing expensive edges whenever every reachable destination remains source-reachable reduces the evaluator’s shared physical-link cost without changing delivery coverage, partition paths, or source-rooted topology semantics. Processing edges from highest to lowest cost maximizes savings first while retaining a valid broadcast route to every terminal.
