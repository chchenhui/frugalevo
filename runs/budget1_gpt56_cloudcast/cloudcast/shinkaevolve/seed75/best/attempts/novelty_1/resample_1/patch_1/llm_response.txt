<DESCRIPTION>
Add an exact directed-Steiner refinement for modest destination sets after the greedy shared-tree construction. The current seed-based heuristic reduces redundant links but can miss a globally cheaper relay/trunk arrangement when an initially expensive intermediate edge enables several low-cost branches. The Dreyfus-Wagner-style dynamic program computes the minimum-cost directed edge union from the source to all destinations, combining terminal subsets at relay clouds and relaxing those shared subtrees backwards over network links. It is bounded to nine destinations to preserve practical runtime, and only replaces the greedy result when it produces a lower-cost reachable topology.
