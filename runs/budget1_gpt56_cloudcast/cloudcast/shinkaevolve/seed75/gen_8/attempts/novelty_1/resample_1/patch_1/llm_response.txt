<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the exact directed-Steiner optimization range so more moderate-fanout broadcasts use the globally minimum shared multicast tree instead of the greedy fallback. The existing subset dynamic program explicitly minimizes unique inter-cloud transfer edges and is therefore substantially better at identifying inexpensive relay clouds and shared prefixes. Raising the threshold from 11 to 14 terminals keeps the exponential solver bounded while making it practical for common multi-region broadcast configurations, where the greedy attachment order can otherwise lock in unnecessarily expensive branches.
