<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the large-fanout greedy attachment fallback with a DP-guided clustering approach. Destinations whose independently cheapest routes share trunk edges are grouped together, then each group is solved with the exact directed Steiner dynamic program. This retains scalable runtime while allowing each correlated destination group to discover non-greedy shared relays and trunks. The resulting cluster subnetworks are unioned, preserving overlap between groups without repeatedly charging or transmitting along redundant source-side paths.
</DESCRIPTION>
