<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the exact directed-Steiner DP state space before solving it, then extend its terminal cutoff from ten to twelve receivers. Nodes which are not reachable from the source, or which cannot reach any requested reachable destination, can never belong to a useful broadcast tree and only inflate every subset-DP relaxation. Removing them preserves optimality while making the exact regime practical for larger destination sets. This replaces the weaker marginal-attachment fallback for more multi-cloud broadcast requests and can discover shared relay trunks that greedy attachment misses.
</DESCRIPTION>
