<DESCRIPTION>
Restrict the Steiner dynamic program to relay nodes that can both be reached from the source and can reach at least one requested destination. Nodes outside this intersection cannot participate in any valid broadcast arborescence, but previously consumed state-space, Dijkstra relaxation, and reconstruction work for every destination subset.

This preserves the optimal transfer cost exactly while substantially reducing the exponential solver’s effective graph size on multi-cloud topologies containing unrelated regions or one-way links. The reduced state space makes it more practical to solve larger destination sets exactly, rather than spending time evaluating routes that cannot contribute to a shared broadcast backbone.
</DESCRIPTION>
