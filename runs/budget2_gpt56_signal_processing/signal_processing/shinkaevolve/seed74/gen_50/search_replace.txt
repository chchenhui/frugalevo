<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single-sample reversal threshold with cumulative, normalized multi-scale evidence and apply the directional lock to the reported output rather than freezing the internal alpha-beta state.

The current tracker updates `previous_direction` as soon as velocity crosses its small deadband, which explains the very high slope-change and false-reversal counts despite excellent lag and point accuracy. The new gate requires counter-trend velocity, recent-slope confirmation, and a significant innovation in the same direction. Evidence accumulates for sustained turns, decays quickly for incoherent pullbacks, and confirms after sufficient normalized evidence or three coherent samples. Meanwhile, the internal position and velocity continue adapting, so the output can be released directly to the already-updated internal state once a genuine reversal is confirmed. This targets the dominant false-reversal penalty while retaining a bounded turn latency.
</DESCRIPTION>
