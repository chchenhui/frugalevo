<DESCRIPTION>
Stabilize the causal alpha-beta tracker with two complementary changes. First, bootstrap the robust innovation scale from recent measurement differences during the first few samples; this avoids the near-zero initial MAD causing excessive innovation clipping and delayed tracker acquisition. Second, replace the instantaneous slope sign crossing logic with significance-gated two-sample reversal confirmation. Small or isolated counter-trend innovations are damped rather than immediately changing the prediction direction, reducing slope changes and false reversals. Confirmed directional moves retain a stronger slope update to limit turning-point lag.
</DESCRIPTION>
