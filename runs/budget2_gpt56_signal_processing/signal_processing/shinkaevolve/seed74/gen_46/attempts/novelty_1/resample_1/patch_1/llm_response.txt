<DESCRIPTION>
Make counter-trend transitions hysteretic and dependent on the maturity of the established direction. The existing controller can admit a reversal after only two or three opposing windows, including when the established trend is very recent. This edit tracks directional maturity, requires a larger counter-trend displacement for a mature trend, and lowers that requirement only after persistent, slope-consistent opposing evidence. In-direction updates retain the original low threshold and gain behavior, preserving responsiveness while specifically reducing noise-driven slope changes and false reversals.
</DESCRIPTION>
