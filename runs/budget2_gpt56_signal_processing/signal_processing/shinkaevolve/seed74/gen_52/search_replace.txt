evidence_weighted_turn_gate
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the binary consecutive-window reversal counter with normalized cumulative counter-trend evidence. The existing gate treats every qualifying counter-trend sample equally, so two weak movements can reverse the visible trend while a strong, coherent turn is delayed unless it passes the separate strong-move condition. Accumulating scale-normalized displacement evidence makes the decision robust to alternating low-amplitude noise, directly reducing false reversals and slope changes.

The hard output lock is retained while a turn is pending, preserving smoothness. Evidence is accumulated only when the proposed visible displacement, fused slope, and recent slope are directionally consistent. A confirmed multi-scale turn therefore remains responsive, while weak or disagreement-driven pullbacks decay rather than becoming reversals.
</DESCRIPTION>
