<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the one-sample reversal attenuation with signed, normalized cumulative turn evidence. A reversal now requires agreement between the proposed velocity direction, local regression slope, and clipped innovation across persistent samples. While evidence is pending, a directional max/min output lock prevents small opposite innovations from creating visible false reversals. Decisive, coherent turns retain a fast bypass to limit lag on genuine sharp changes.
</DESCRIPTION>
