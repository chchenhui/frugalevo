<DESCRIPTION>
Tighten robust weighting only for the newest few residuals when they oppose both the local fitted slope and the already-established tracker velocity. The endpoint has disproportionate influence on a causal local-linear estimate, so isolated contrary impulses can otherwise create false reversals. Requiring slope/velocity agreement preserves responsiveness: sustained genuine turns rapidly alter the fitted slope and no longer meet the contradictory-sample condition.
