<DESCRIPTION>
Add a lightweight 5–7 sample endpoint slope that acts only as an internal confidence signal for the alpha-beta tracker. The full and recent robust fits remain the sole reported-measurement path, preserving their strong tracking accuracy and noise reduction. When the newest micro-scale slope agrees with the established recent/full/tracker direction, the filter modestly lowers velocity persistence and raises position gain for reduced lag. When it disagrees, it increases persistence and lowers alpha, preventing a few newest samples from inducing a false reversal. This supplies an additional temporal agreement test without introducing a third expensive robust regression.
</DESCRIPTION>
