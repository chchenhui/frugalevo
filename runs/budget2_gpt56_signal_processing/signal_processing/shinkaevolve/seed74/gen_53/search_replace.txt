<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the one-sample, innovation-only reversal test with a normalized cumulative counter-trend evidence gate. The existing tracker permits noisy velocity sign flips whenever the candidate velocity crosses zero with an innovation above a threshold, producing excessive slope changes and false reversals. The new gate retains the prior velocity direction as a hard output lock while an opposite turn is pending. Evidence is accumulated only when the fitted local slope, bounded innovation, and candidate velocity all agree on the counter-trend direction. Thus, alternating noise pullbacks reset/decay evidence, whereas a real sustained reversal is accepted after approximately 2.8 normalized noise units or three coherent samples. A sufficiently strong, coherent turn remains fast-path eligible, protecting lag and responsiveness. This directly targets the poor smoothness and false-reversal metrics without requiring expensive multi-scale processing.
</DESCRIPTION>
