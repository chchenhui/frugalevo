<DESCRIPTION>
Retune the endpoint tracker toward a slightly longer recency scale and less aggressive maximum state correction. The current tracker is highly responsive but residual false reversals remain, indicating that transient endpoint-fit errors can still produce overly large state changes. Increasing the exponential history from -2.5 to -2.85 improves local slope stability without introducing moving-average phase lag, because the fitted intercept remains endpoint aligned. Centering the residual MAD prevents a biased local line residual distribution from overstating or understating the adaptive noise scale. Finally, bounding the innovation used for both level and velocity updates limits isolated excursions while preserving strong responsiveness through activity-dependent gains. The revised maximum alpha gain is 0.68, within the recommended 0.65–0.72 range.
</DESCRIPTION>
