<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the endpoint linear measurement with a regularized, exponentially weighted local quadratic model. The quadratic endpoint estimate captures curvature near genuine peaks and troughs, reducing the lag caused by a linear trend extrapolation. Its derivative provides a less noisy velocity measurement than updating velocity from level innovation alone.

The tracker then uses robust residual-scale estimation, bounded innovations, and explicit two-observation reversal confirmation. This prevents an isolated noisy local fit from reversing the trend, while allowing large, persistent turns to switch quickly. The result targets lower slope-change and false-reversal counts without sacrificing endpoint alignment or responsiveness.
</DESCRIPTION>
