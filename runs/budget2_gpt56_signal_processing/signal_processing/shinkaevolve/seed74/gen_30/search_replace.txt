<DESCRIPTION>
Cap the measurement gain when the endpoint observation is inconsistent with both the established tracker velocity and the independently fitted local slope, or when the newest residual is an extreme outlier. The current adaptive alpha can approach 0.68 for any large innovation, allowing a single corrupted endpoint measurement to move the level enough to cause a subsequent false reversal. This gate retains high gain for coherent trend changes, because genuine turns typically produce innovation and local-slope agreement, while reducing isolated impulse sensitivity and slope-change count with negligible latency or computational cost.
</DESCRIPTION>
