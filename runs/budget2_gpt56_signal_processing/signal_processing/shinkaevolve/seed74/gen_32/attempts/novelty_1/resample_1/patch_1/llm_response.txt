<DESCRIPTION>
Add a short-horizon regression slope and use agreement between it and the full-window slope as a confidence measure for velocity updates. When both horizons support the same trend, a modest slope-based correction improves tracking and reduces accumulated lag. When they disagree, the tracker retains velocity persistence instead of reacting to a potentially noisy endpoint fit, reducing false reversals and slope jitter.
</DESCRIPTION>
