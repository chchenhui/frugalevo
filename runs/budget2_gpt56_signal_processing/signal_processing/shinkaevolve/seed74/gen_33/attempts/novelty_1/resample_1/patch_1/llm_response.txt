<DESCRIPTION>
Broaden the exponential regression kernel so the endpoint polynomial is estimated from a slightly longer effective history. The previous newest-to-oldest weighting ratio is aggressively concentrated at the newest observations, which allows short noise bursts to alter the fitted slope and subsequently create false reversals. Reducing the decay from 2.7 to 2.3 gives recent samples dominant influence while increasing support from adjacent history. The alpha-beta measurement gain is modestly capped at 0.60 to prevent the state position from immediately following residual endpoint noise; the endpoint-aligned quadratic fit and velocity prediction retain responsiveness without introducing moving-average phase delay.
</DESCRIPTION>
