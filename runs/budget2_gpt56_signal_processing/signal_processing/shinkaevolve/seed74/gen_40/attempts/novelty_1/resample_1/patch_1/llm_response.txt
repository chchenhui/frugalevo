<DESCRIPTION>
Use the existing independent short-horizon slope as confirmation evidence rather than allowing every local-slope update to influence the tracker equally. Agreement modestly increases alpha/beta for responsive tracking of coherent motion; disagreement reduces beta to preserve velocity. Add a two-sample reversal confirmation state, while permitting immediate reversals only when both slope horizons agree and the new velocity is strong. This targets the dominant slope-change and false-reversal penalties without adding latency, extra passes, or expensive processing.
</DESCRIPTION>
