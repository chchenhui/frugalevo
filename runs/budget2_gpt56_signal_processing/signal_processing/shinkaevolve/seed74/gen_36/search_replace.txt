Add a lightweight six-sample endpoint slope as independent short-horizon evidence. Agreement between the recent and full-window slopes raises alpha and beta modestly for coherent movement, reducing tracking lag. Disagreement suppresses beta-driven velocity changes, preserving velocity persistence and reducing noise-induced reversals without changing the stable endpoint measurement itself.
