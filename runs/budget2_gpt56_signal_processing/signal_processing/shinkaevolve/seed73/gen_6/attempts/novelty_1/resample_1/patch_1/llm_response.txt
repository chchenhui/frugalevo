<DESCRIPTION>
Add a confidence-gated hysteresis mechanism to the Kalman slope update. The level measurement update remains fully responsive, preserving low lag and tracking accuracy. However, a proposed slope reversal is only accepted after it is supported by consecutive, sufficiently strong slope estimates. This prevents a single noisy innovation from flipping the estimated trend direction, directly reducing slope changes and false reversals.

The threshold is scaled from the adaptive innovation variance and inversely related to the sliding-window duration, so it adapts to signal volatility rather than using a fixed amplitude threshold. Small slope estimates are softly damped near zero, further suppressing jitter without blocking genuine sustained trend changes.
</DESCRIPTION>
