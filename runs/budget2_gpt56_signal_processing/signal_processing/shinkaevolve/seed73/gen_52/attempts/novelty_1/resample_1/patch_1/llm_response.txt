hysteretic_gain_modes
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace continuously varying tracker gains with hysteretic flat and trend modes. The mode is entered only after two consecutive high-confidence slope windows and exited only after four low-confidence windows. This prevents local-noise changes in slope confidence from directly modulating the gain every sample, which is a likely source of output-direction jitter. Flat mode uses conservative level and velocity gains to reduce false reversals, while trend mode restores higher gain and anticipatory slope blending for responsive tracking of sustained movement.
</DESCRIPTION>
