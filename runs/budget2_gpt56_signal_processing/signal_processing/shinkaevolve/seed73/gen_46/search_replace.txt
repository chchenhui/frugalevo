hysteretic_trend_modes
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace sample-by-sample continuous fusion-gain variation with hysteretic discrete flat and trend modes. The mode is selected from local slope confidence relative to residual noise, requiring two consecutive confident windows to enter responsive trend tracking and four weak-confidence windows to return to the more conservative flat mode. This prevents gain jitter from becoming output-direction jitter, while still allowing a confirmed trend to use a higher local endpoint-regression gain. The reversal guard is made mode-aware: flat/noisy periods require broader evidence before changing direction, whereas confirmed trends retain a shorter confirmation path and therefore low lag.
</DESCRIPTION>
