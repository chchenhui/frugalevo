<DESCRIPTION>
Use the last accepted published output step, rather than the unconfirmed current level displacement, as the stable velocity reference. Fuse it with the robust recency-weighted local regression slope. The fusion gives more weight to the accepted step when the local trend is weak relative to residual noise, suppressing noise-driven reversals and slope jitter. As local trend strength grows, the direct regression contributes more strongly to reduce sustained-trend lag. A residual-noise-scaled acceleration limit prevents a contaminated window from abruptly changing the Kalman slope.
</DESCRIPTION>
