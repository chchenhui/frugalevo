<DESCRIPTION>
Add a low-latency output-domain reversal gate to the Kalman filter. The current filter has strong tracking accuracy but excessive slope changes and false reversals, indicating that small posterior level fluctuations are being emitted as trend changes. The gate holds a proposed direction reversal inside a volatility-scaled band until two consecutive local measurement-slope observations support it. This preserves genuine sustained reversals with only a short confirmation delay, while preventing isolated noise excursions from creating output direction changes. The Kalman state itself remains unconstrained, so responsiveness is retained once reversal evidence is established.
</DESCRIPTION>
