<DESCRIPTION>
Strengthen the existing reversal suppression with explicit, asymmetric evidence gating. The present logic updates `stable_velocity` as soon as a candidate velocity crosses a noise-scaled threshold, allowing alternating local-regression slopes to create avoidable reversals. This edit requires two consecutive, sufficiently strong opposing trend indications before a direction change is accepted.

A pending reversal is cancelled when the direct local slope returns to the established direction. During the first opposing indication, velocity is gently decayed toward zero rather than flipped. This retains the endpoint regression tracker’s low lag on sustained turns, while reducing noise-driven slope changes and false reversals—the current primary weakness—without adding another filtering pass or material latency.
</DESCRIPTION>
