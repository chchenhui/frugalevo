<DESCRIPTION>
Add a low-cost, exponentially weighted local-regression slope as an independent trend measurement and fuse it conservatively with the Kalman slope and the actual published-output step. The consensus update is acceleration-bounded by the robust innovation scale, so a local noisy reversal cannot abruptly flip tracker velocity. This supplies direct recent trend evidence to reduce lag on sustained motion, while the bounded update and existing output hysteresis reduce noise-driven directional reversals.
