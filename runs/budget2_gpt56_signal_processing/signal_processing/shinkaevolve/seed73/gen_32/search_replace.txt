<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single exponential-window reference with a precomputed bank of causal endpoint regressions. A gentle decay remains dominant in flat or noisy regions, while current and steep recency-weighted fits receive progressively more weight when the fitted slope is large relative to its residual scale. This supplies a low-lag, trend-aware consensus measurement without making the Kalman tracker itself more reactive. The existing predictive stage and reversal hysteresis remain in place, preserving their false-reversal protection while the improved local reference reduces lag during coherent motion.

    regression_design = np.column_stack((np.ones(window_size), regression_time))
