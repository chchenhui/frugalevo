<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single fixed recency-weighted endpoint regression with a confidence-adaptive blend of three causal, endpoint-aligned regressions. The gentler profile is favored when the local slope is weak relative to robust volatility, reducing measurement jitter and false reversals. The more recent profile is admitted only when all regression scales agree on a strong directional slope, reducing lag during genuine persistent trends without allowing a noisy short-scale fit to dominate. All fits are precomputed once per invocation and remain vectorized, so the real-time tracker loop and computational latency are preserved.
</DESCRIPTION>

    # Fit endpoint-aligned local lines at three causal time scales.  The
    # gentler fit is less sensitive to noise, while the recent fit reduces lag
    # during a directionally consistent, high-confidence movement.
    t = np.arange(window_size, dtype=float) - (window_size - 1)
    design = np.column_stack((np.ones(window_size), t))
    fit_profiles = []
    for decay in (1.6, 2.7, 4.5):
        weights = np.exp(np.linspace(-decay, 0.0, window_size))
        normal = design.T @ (weights[:, None] * design)
        fit_profiles.append(np.linalg.solve(normal, design.T * weights))

    endpoints = np.column_stack([clipped @ profile[0] for profile in fit_profiles])
    slopes = np.column_stack([clipped @ profile[1] for profile in fit_profiles])

    # Use the medium scale as the stable reference.  A recent measurement is
    # trusted only when all scales support its direction; otherwise blending
    # toward the gentler estimate prevents short-scale noise from producing
    # spurious turns.
