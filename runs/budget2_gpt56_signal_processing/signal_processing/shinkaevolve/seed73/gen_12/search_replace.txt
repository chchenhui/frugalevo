<DESCRIPTION>
Add a lightweight output-stage directional hysteresis to the adaptive Kalman filter. The state estimator remains responsive and continues tracking every measurement, but the published output requires a directional reversal to persist for two meaningful samples before accepting it. A robust, innovation-derived deadband ignores sub-noise-scale output movement. This specifically targets false reversals and excessive slope changes without adding a windowed phase delay or expensive processing. Sustained genuine reversals are accepted after one confirmation sample, while isolated noise excursions do not alter the reported trend direction.
</DESCRIPTION>
