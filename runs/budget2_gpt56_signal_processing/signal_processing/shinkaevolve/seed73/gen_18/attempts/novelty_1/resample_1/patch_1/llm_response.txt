<DESCRIPTION>
Add an output-domain hysteresis gate to the adaptive Kalman estimate. The existing filter has strong tracking accuracy and low lag, but its unconstrained level updates allow frequent one-sample direction changes. The gate maintains the accepted output trend direction, suppresses weak opposing moves within a volatility-scaled band, and requires repeated opposing evidence before releasing a reversal. Exceptionally large moves pass immediately so genuine sharp turns retain low latency. This specifically targets the high slope-change and false-reversal metrics while preserving the causal filter and its responsive trend tracking.
</DESCRIPTION>
