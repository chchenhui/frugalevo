<DESCRIPTION>
Add a lightweight hysteretic reporting stage after the predictive consensus filter. The Kalman/consensus states remain fully responsive and continue accumulating evidence for a real turn, but the emitted signal rejects a single weak movement opposite to its established direction. A reversal is released after two directionally consistent samples or immediately for a statistically large change. This specifically targets the currently high slope-change and false-reversal counts without adding window delay or altering the adaptive tracker’s ability to follow sustained transitions.
