<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the average absolute-difference scale used by the regression fusion with a post-fit residual MAD scale from the balanced endpoint regression. Absolute differences include genuine ramp velocity and therefore overestimate noise during coherent trends, weakening slope evidence and increasing lag. Residual MAD measures only the portion not explained by the local endpoint line, allowing sustained trends to receive stronger fast-regression and predictive-consensus weighting while retaining robust resistance to impulsive residuals. This scale is already window-aligned and is computed vectorially once before the real-time update loop.
