<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a persistent two-state flat/trend controller driven by normalized local-slope evidence. The controller enters trend mode only after sustained high evidence and exits only after sustained weak evidence, preventing gain chattering near the noise threshold. Flat mode uses a lower level gain and stronger velocity damping to suppress false reversals; trend mode restores responsiveness. A direction-confirmation gate further prevents a single noisy fitted slope from reversing the velocity state.
</DESCRIPTION>
