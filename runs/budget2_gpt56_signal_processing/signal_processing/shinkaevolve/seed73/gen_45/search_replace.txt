<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the three-scale endpoint consensus with two closely related endpoint regressions: the validated exp(-3) profile and a slightly more recent exp(-4.5) profile. The recent profile is not used merely because an instantaneous slope is large; it is admitted only after both profiles agree on direction and the trend confidence has persisted across consecutive complete windows. This removes conflicting multi-scale slope estimates that can produce direction chatter, while preserving the low endpoint lag of local regression on clean, sustained trends. The residual-based confidence also makes the blend naturally conservative during volatile or noisy intervals.
