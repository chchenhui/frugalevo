<DESCRIPTION>
Strengthen the reporting-layer reversal gate using a wider innovation-normalized deadband, three consecutive directionally supported samples, and a higher emergency-turn threshold. The current controller treats very small opposite deltas as reversal evidence because the qualification threshold is only 20% of an already small band; this admits alternating Kalman/consensus jitter. Requiring persistent, velocity-confirmed opposition suppresses noise-induced slope changes and false reversals while retaining responsiveness for genuine turns through the strong-turn escape path. The adaptive band also scales with emitted velocity, so fast real movements are not reversed by a small counter-trend fluctuation.
</DESCRIPTION>
