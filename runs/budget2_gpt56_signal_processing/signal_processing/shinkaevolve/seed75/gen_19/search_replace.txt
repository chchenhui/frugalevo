<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the subtractive covariance update with the Joseph stabilized form. The current update can gradually lose positive-semidefiniteness through floating-point cancellation, especially when adaptive measurement and process variances change rapidly. Joseph form preserves covariance consistency, keeps Kalman gains stable, and permits the reversal gate to operate on a more reliable noise estimate. Explicit symmetrization and diagonal flooring prevent numerical degeneracy without changing the causal, low-latency architecture.

        # Joseph-form covariance correction is more numerically stable than the
        # subtractive form when adaptive variances and gains change over time.
        # With H = [1, 0], (I - K H) has the compact form below.
        correction_matrix = np.array(
            [[1.0 - gain[0], 0.0], [-gain[1], 1.0]],
            dtype=float,
        )
        covariance = (
            correction_matrix @ predicted_covariance @ correction_matrix.T
            + measurement_var * np.outer(gain, gain)
        )
        covariance = 0.5 * (covariance + covariance.T)
        covariance[0, 0] = max(covariance[0, 0], 1e-12)
        covariance[1, 1] = max(covariance[1, 1], 1e-12)
