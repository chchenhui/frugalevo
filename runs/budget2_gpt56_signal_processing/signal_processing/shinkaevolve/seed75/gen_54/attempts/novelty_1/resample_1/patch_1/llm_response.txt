<DESCRIPTION>
Gate pending reversal accumulation with directional slope evidence and decay weak evidence instead of treating every opposite endpoint step as confirmation. The current persistence branch can accept a reversal after two opposite candidate steps even when the fast/consensus trend models do not support that direction, which explains excess false reversals. This change retains the immediate low-lag path for strongly model-confirmed turns, while requiring persistent turns to show at least partial fast or consensus slope support. Weak or alternating endpoint noise now decays pending distance/count, preventing stale evidence from accumulating into a false directional change.
</DESCRIPTION>
