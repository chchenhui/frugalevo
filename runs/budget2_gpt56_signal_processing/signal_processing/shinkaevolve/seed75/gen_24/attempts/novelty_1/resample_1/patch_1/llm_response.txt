<DESCRIPTION>
Add a causal, asymmetric reversal hysteresis gate to the reported Kalman trajectory. The internal level/slope state remains fully adaptive, so sustained turns continue to update the predictor with minimal lag. However, a small counter-direction movement is not emitted unless it exceeds a noise-scaled threshold plus a fraction of the prior established output step. This specifically targets the current high slope-change and false-reversal counts while avoiding the broad smoothing and phase delay of a moving average or a second low-pass stage.
