adaptive_innovation_gating

<DESCRIPTION>
The current process noise is driven directly by the clipped innovation, so isolated measurement impulses can still raise the Kalman gain and produce many small output-direction changes. Replace the fixed clipping rule with an adaptive normalized-innovation gate and require evidence across consecutive samples before treating a large innovation as dynamic acceleration. A fast, bounded EWMA estimate of normalized innovation magnitude adapts the clipping threshold to changing noise regimes. In parallel, process-noise adaptation is based on persistent innovation energy rather than one sample. This should retain the current low lag on genuine transitions while materially reducing noise-induced slope changes and false reversals.
</DESCRIPTION>
