<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the discrete implicit treatment of fast/slow slope agreement with a continuous, magnitude-aware consensus confidence. The normalized slope alignment distinguishes strong agreement from weak or opposing derivatives, while the magnitude term prevents near-zero slopes from receiving high confidence merely because their signs match. This confidence softly modulates fast-slope influence and predictive lead: coherent active trends receive more responsive tracking, while ambiguous derivative states receive less lead and reduced susceptibility to noise-induced reversals. The modulation retains a nonzero fast-path contribution during genuine turns, avoiding a large lag increase while improving false-reversal resistance.
