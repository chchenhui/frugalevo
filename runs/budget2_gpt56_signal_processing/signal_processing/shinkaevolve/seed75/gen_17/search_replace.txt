Replace the subtractive covariance update with the Joseph stabilized form. The subtractive update can lose positive semidefiniteness under adaptive process and measurement variance changes, producing unstable gains that manifest as jitter and spurious reversals. The Joseph form preserves covariance consistency, while symmetric off-diagonal limiting guarantees the 2x2 covariance remains positive semidefinite with negligible real-time overhead.
