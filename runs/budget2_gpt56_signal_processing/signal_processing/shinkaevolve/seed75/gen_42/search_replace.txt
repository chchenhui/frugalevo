persistent_reversal_gate
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a causal output-level reversal confirmation gate after the existing fast Kalman update. The internal state remains responsive and continues tracking an emerging turn, but the emitted signal holds its established direction until an opposing move has persisted for at least two samples and accumulated meaningful noise-normalized displacement. Large turns bypass this confirmation immediately. This separates low-lag state tracking from conservative trend declaration, directly reducing noise-induced slope changes and false reversals without slowing sustained trends.
</DESCRIPTION>
