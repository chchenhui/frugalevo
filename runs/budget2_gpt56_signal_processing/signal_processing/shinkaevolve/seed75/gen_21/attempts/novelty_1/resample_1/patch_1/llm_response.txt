<DESCRIPTION>
Lower the steady-state acceleration variance to reduce noise-driven slope movement, while replacing the low fixed multiplier ceiling with a bounded quadratic change-response term. This preserves the causal two-state Kalman architecture and robust innovation clipping, but allows substantially faster adaptation when consecutive observations disagree with the predicted local trend. The explicit upper cap prevents a single large innovation from making the filter overly reactive, improving the trade-off between reversal suppression, lag, and trend tracking.
</DESCRIPTION>
