<DESCRIPTION>
Increase alpha-beta tracker responsiveness only when the incoming innovation continues a direction supported by both fast and slow derivative models. The current filter substantially reduced false reversals, but its conservative state gain increased lag error and average tracking error. This edit restores tracking gain for persistent, model-confirmed motion while retaining the existing impulse reliability reduction when an innovation contradicts the derivative consensus.

Requiring both derivative scales to agree prevents a noisy one-sample fast-slope fluctuation from increasing gain. The stronger state update is therefore applied primarily to genuine sustained trends, improving correlation and lag without weakening the downstream directional hysteresis and reversal confirmation logic.
