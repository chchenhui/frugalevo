<DESCRIPTION>
Make reversal acceptance depend on the reliability of the estimated directional state, not solely on two endpoint displacements. The current gate treats two opposite output candidates as sufficient even when the Kalman slope still indicates the previous trend, allowing short-lived noisy excursions to create false reversals. This edit accepts a large reversal promptly only when both the accumulated endpoint displacement and internal slope agree. Weak or slope-inconsistent reversals require one additional persistent sample. This should reduce slope changes and false reversals while retaining low lag for decisive genuine turns.
</DESCRIPTION>
