<DESCRIPTION>
Add a two-sample directional confirmation gate to the causal Kalman tracker and reduce innovation-driven process-noise inflation. The current tracker is highly responsive but its process covariance grows sharply after individual noisy residuals, causing the estimated slope to change sign frequently (284 slope changes and 228 false reversals). The proposed gate preserves immediate response for substantial, coherent trend changes while holding only small first-sample opposite moves. A second consistent opposite increment, or an unusually large move, releases the gate. This hysteretic turn logic directly targets false reversals without adding a fixed moving-average delay. Reducing the adaptive process-noise coefficient also prevents isolated innovations from temporarily making the state estimate overly reactive.
</DESCRIPTION>
