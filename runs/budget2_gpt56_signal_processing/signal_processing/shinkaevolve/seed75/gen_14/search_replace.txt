<DESCRIPTION>
Retune the adaptive alpha-beta tracker toward stronger steady-state noise suppression while increasing its maximum change response. A lower baseline gain reduces small slope jitter, whereas higher capped gains retain rapid tracking during meaningful innovations. Strengthen output reversal hysteresis so ordinary noise-scale counter-moves require confirmation, but permit sufficiently large, likely genuine reversals immediately. This specifically targets the still-high slope-change and false-reversal counts without adding latency-heavy windowing or non-causal processing.
