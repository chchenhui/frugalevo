slope_confirmed_reversals
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Require the Kalman slope estimate to corroborate an opposite output movement before accumulating reversal evidence. The existing gate evaluates only displacement from the emitted level; a temporary noise-driven level excursion can therefore begin a reversal count even while the estimated local velocity still supports the established direction. Resetting pending evidence unless slope and displacement agree rejects these inconsistent excursions. A large displacement retains the existing immediate-turn path, preserving responsiveness to sharp genuine corners while reducing slope changes and false reversals.
</DESCRIPTION>
