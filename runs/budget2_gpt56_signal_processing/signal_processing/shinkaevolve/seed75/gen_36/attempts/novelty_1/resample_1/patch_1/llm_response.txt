<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single-sample directional deadband with a low-latency persistence gate. The current filter accepts many opposite-direction increments once they exceed a relatively small threshold, which explains the high slope-change and false-reversal counts. The new gate requires two consecutive opposing moves whose accumulated displacement is meaningful relative to the adaptive noise scale, while allowing a sufficiently strong turn to pass immediately. Unconfirmed opposing samples hold the output level and strongly attenuate slope, preserving the existing filter’s fast consensus dynamics while reducing noise-induced directional flicker. This targets false reversals and slope changes with only a one-sample confirmation cost for ordinary genuine reversals.
</DESCRIPTION>
