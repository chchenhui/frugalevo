<DESCRIPTION>
Add a causal, confidence-scaled reversal confirmation stage to the emitted output. The Kalman state remains responsive and continues to assimilate every robustly clipped observation, but a directional change in the published signal now requires two consecutive noise-significant estimates. This separates state tracking from trend declaration: isolated innovations and short-window contamination no longer immediately create an output turn, while sustained genuine reversals incur only one-sample confirmation latency. The threshold scales with the learned residual noise level, preserving sensitivity during quiet periods and avoiding excessive false-reversal suppression in volatile regions.
</DESCRIPTION>

        # Require two consecutive noise-significant opposite moves before
