<DESCRIPTION>
Make the fast directional model influential only when its recent difference-noise estimate and newest residual indicate that it is reliable. The current fixed 0.55 fast-slope consensus can allow a short noisy interval or endpoint impulse to affect the candidate lead and reversal confirmation. This edit records fast/slow robust scales, derives an impulse/recent-volatility reliability factor, and reduces fast-model influence unless there is meaningful directional activity with a stable short-horizon fit. The slow model remains dominant during contaminated intervals, reducing false reversals and slope changes, while the fast model still receives additional weight during clean, active turns to preserve low lag.
</DESCRIPTION>
