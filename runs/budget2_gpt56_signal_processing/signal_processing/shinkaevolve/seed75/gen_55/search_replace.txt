<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded, turn-aware predictive lead to the published level. The Kalman level is intentionally smoothed and consequently retains some residual lag during sustained coherent motion. A small extrapolation based on consensus between the instantaneous Kalman slope and the smoothed recent increment improves tracking responsiveness without changing the causal estimator.

The lead is disabled when the two slope estimates disagree, reduced during deceleration, and attenuated when the innovation persistently opposes the current trend. These conditions commonly precede genuine turns, so suppressing prediction there avoids overshoot and the subsequent noise-induced counter-reversal. The lead is also bounded relative to the adaptive noise scale, preserving robust behavior during volatile regimes.
</DESCRIPTION>

        # instantaneous and slow trend estimates agree.  Prediction is reduced
        # as the fast slope decelerates and is removed when persistent
        # innovations oppose the current trend, which prevents extrapolation
        # beyond an approaching corner.
        consensus_slope = 0.65 * slope + 0.35 * previous_increment
        same_direction = slope * previous_increment > 0.0
        slope_ratio = abs(slope) / max(abs(previous_increment), 1e-12)
        deceleration_weight = np.clip(slope_ratio, 0.0, 1.0)

            0.0,
            -persistent_innovation * consensus_slope
            / max(noise_scale * abs(consensus_slope), 1e-12),

        )
