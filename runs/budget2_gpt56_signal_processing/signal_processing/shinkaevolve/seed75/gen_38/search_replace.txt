<DESCRIPTION>
Require a reversal to have directional support from the local trend models before it bypasses the persistence gate. A candidate reversal is now accepted immediately when the fast and slow slopes agree with its direction, or when the fast/consensus trend has a sufficiently strong normalized directional slope. This makes genuine, model-supported turns responsive while preventing a large output step caused by a transient fit perturbation from creating a false reversal. Unsupported opposing movement retains the existing two-sample persistence behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
