<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the binary slope-agreement gate with a continuous, magnitude-aware directional consensus. The prior Boolean test grants the full fast-model and gain boost even when one or both slopes are nearly zero, which can make the response discontinuous and can either admit weak noise-driven motion or unnecessarily favor the slow model after a genuine turn.

The new confidence is signed cosine-style alignment multiplied by a joint slope-strength term. Strong, same-direction slopes increase the fast endpoint contribution and adaptive correction gain to reduce lag. Weak, opposing, or near-zero slopes smoothly reduce reactivity, preserving the existing robust hysteresis behavior without introducing abrupt gain changes.
</DESCRIPTION>
