<DESCRIPTION>
Add a causal, noise-scaled directional hysteresis stage after each Kalman update. The Kalman state remains responsible for rapid level tracking, but a candidate output reversal is suppressed when its magnitude is small relative to the continuously estimated observation-noise scale and the preceding output motion. This specifically targets noise-induced slope sign changes and false reversals without adding a fixed-window delay. The state level and slope are made consistent with a rejected reversal so that the next prediction continues the established trend rather than repeatedly oscillating around it. Genuine turns pass once their sustained displacement exceeds the adaptive threshold.
</DESCRIPTION>

        # dominated by measurement noise; accepting every sign change creates
        # many false reversals even when level error remains low.

            previous_step = previous_level - filtered[index - 2]
            candidate_step = candidate_level - previous_level

                0.18 * np.sqrt(measurement_var)

                previous_step * candidate_step < 0.0
                and abs(candidate_step) < reversal_margin

                # counter-move, while retaining enough slope to avoid a
