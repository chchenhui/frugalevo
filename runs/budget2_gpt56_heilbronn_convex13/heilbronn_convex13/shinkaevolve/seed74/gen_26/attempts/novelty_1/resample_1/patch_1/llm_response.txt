<DESCRIPTION>
Exploit affine invariance by fixing the convex hull to the unit square for every candidate, not merely a minority of initial candidates. This makes the raw triangle area exactly equal to the normalized objective throughout differential evolution, removing the major objective mismatch caused by variable hull areas. It also reduces the search dimension from 26 to 18 free coordinates and prevents the optimizer from spending effort perturbing hull corners. The lower-tail term is reduced so selection more directly maximizes the required worst-triangle criterion.
</DESCRIPTION>
