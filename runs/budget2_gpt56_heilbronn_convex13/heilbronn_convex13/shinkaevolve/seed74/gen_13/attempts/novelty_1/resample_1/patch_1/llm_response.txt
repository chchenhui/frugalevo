<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single perturbed-grid initialization with a deterministic family of lattice-based seeds at several perturbation scales. This gives the maximin search many initially well-spaced configurations while avoiding the exact collinearities that make a regular grid unusable.

Also replace the fixed, overly aggressive differential scale with a cooling differential-evolution proposal and an attraction toward the incumbent best configuration. A scale of 0.78 frequently clips points onto square edges and destroys promising triangle constraints; decreasing the scale over generations yields large basin-changing moves early and useful geometric refinements later.
</DESCRIPTION>
