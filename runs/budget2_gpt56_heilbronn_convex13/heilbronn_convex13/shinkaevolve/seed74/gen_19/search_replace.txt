staged_tail_surrogate
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the purely nonsmooth selection objective during population evolution with a staged lower-tail surrogate. Early generations reward the mean of the 12 smallest triangle areas in addition to the true minimum, encouraging configurations with many robust near-active constraints rather than accidental single-triangle improvements. The tail coefficient is annealed toward a small value late in the search, preserving focus on the actual maximin objective. The incumbent and final polishing are still evaluated and retained strictly by the true minimum area.
</DESCRIPTION>

    def triangle_statistics(interior: np.ndarray) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
        """Return minimum area and mean area of the twelve smallest triples."""
        count = interior.shape[0]
        layouts = np.empty((count, 13, 2), dtype=float)
        layouts[:, :4, :] = corners
        layouts[:, 4:, :] = interior
        u = layouts[:, triangles[:, 1]] - layouts[:, triangles[:, 0]]
        v = layouts[:, triangles[:, 2]] - layouts[:, triangles[:, 0]]
        areas = 0.5 * np.abs(u[..., 0] * v[..., 1] - u[..., 1] * v[..., 0])
        minimums = areas.min(axis=1)
        lower_tail = np.partition(areas, 11, axis=1)[:, :12].mean(axis=1)
        return minimums, lower_tail
