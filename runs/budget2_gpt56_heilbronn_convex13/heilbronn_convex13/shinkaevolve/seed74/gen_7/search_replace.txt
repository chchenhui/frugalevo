<DESCRIPTION>
Replace one of the three purely local Gaussian proposals with a differential-evolution trial. The original population rapidly becomes genetically similar because every proposal is a shrinking perturbation followed by attraction to the incumbent best layout. Differential mutations use the displacement between two independently selected layouts, preserving useful geometric scales and allowing coordinated movement of several points across minimum-area barriers. The existing greedy selection, deterministic RNG, and two local proposals are retained.
</DESCRIPTION>

bottleneck_local_polish
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a deterministic final multiscale maximin polish around the best population member. Heilbronn objectives are nonsmooth: the limiting triangle often changes after a small move, so population-level evolution commonly stops with several points not locally adjusted to the active bottleneck constraints. Batched sparse perturbations move one or a few points at once, retain only strict improvements to the true minimum area, and decay from structural to fine geometric scales. This adds only vectorized area evaluations and does not alter the fixed unit-square hull normalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
