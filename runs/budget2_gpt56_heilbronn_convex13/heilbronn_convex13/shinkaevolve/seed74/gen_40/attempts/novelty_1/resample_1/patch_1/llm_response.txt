<DESCRIPTION>
Replace part of the purely stochastic sparse polishing batch with a deterministic coordinate-pattern poll over every non-hull point. Maximin triangle objectives are piecewise linear and nonsmooth: near a good configuration, random multi-point moves frequently damage one of the active minimum-area triangles before finding an improving direction. Explicit positive/negative x/y polls for each of the ten movable points provide a reliable local direct-search mechanism, while the remaining candidates retain random multi-point perturbations for diagonal and basin-changing moves. This preserves determinism, convex feasibility through the existing barycentric projection, and adds no expensive objective evaluations because the batch size is unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
