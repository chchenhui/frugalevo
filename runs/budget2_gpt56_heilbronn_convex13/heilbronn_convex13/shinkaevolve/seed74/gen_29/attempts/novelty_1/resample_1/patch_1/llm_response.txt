<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed weak lower-tail surrogate with an annealed schedule. Early differential-evolution generations should protect several near-minimal triangles, because a purely bottleneck-driven population tends to create fragile candidates whose active constraint changes immediately. Near convergence, the tail coefficient decays to a small value so selection closely follows the exact maximin objective. This follows the recommended two-stage lower-tail protection strategy while retaining the existing exact-minimum finalist and polishing logic.
</DESCRIPTION>
