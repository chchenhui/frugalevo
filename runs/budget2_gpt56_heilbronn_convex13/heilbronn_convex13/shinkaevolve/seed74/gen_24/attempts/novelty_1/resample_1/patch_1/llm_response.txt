<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the uniform coordinate-only final polish with a bottleneck-aware strict-improvement search. At every scale it identifies the currently smallest triangle constraints, weights interior points by their participation in those constraints, and concentrates proposals on the points responsible for the current bottleneck. In addition to directed coordinate moves, it includes deterministic paired moves for coupled active constraints. Every proposal is evaluated with the exact minimum-area objective and accepted only when it strictly improves it, preserving the primary metric while escaping coordinate-wise local optima.
</DESCRIPTION>
