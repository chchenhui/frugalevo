<DESCRIPTION>
Seed half of the deterministic annealing restarts near perturbed 3-by-3 configurations, while retaining random starts for basin diversity. More importantly, cache the current triangle areas and preferentially perturb a non-corner vertex belonging to one of the currently tightest triangles. In a maximin objective, uniformly selecting among all nine movable points wastes most evaluations on points unrelated to the active constraints. The retained random-selection probability still permits coordinated escape moves and avoids overcommitting to a transient active triangle.
