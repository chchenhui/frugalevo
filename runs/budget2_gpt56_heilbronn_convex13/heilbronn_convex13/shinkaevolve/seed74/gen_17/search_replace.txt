<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed exponentially concentrated rank surrogate with a staged lower-tail objective. Early search should reward improvement across the smallest dozen triangles, preventing the local walk from sacrificing overall geometric regularity to improve a single accidental bottleneck. As iterations progress, anneal the tail weight toward the true minimum-area objective so the final moves concentrate on the active Heilbronn constraints. This implements a deterministic coarse-to-fine continuation without increasing the number of area evaluations.
</DESCRIPTION>
