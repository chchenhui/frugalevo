triangular_hull_schedule

<DESCRIPTION>
Constrain every evolutionary candidate to a common triangular hull with vertices `(0,0)`, `(1,0)`, and `(0,1)`. This is particularly appropriate for the affine-invariant objective: the hull area is fixed at `1/2`, so raw triangle-area ranking is exactly proportional to normalized ranking. It also removes arbitrary hull variation, preserves the classical three-fold/triangular Heilbronn geometry bias, and makes every DE generation optimize a consistent objective.

The former population mixed square-hull, variable-hull, and anchored candidates, so its fast DE score was often not aligned with the evaluated normalized score. The replacement uses folded stratified samples in the fixed simplex. It also changes the tail objective from strong early lower-tail protection to nearly exact maximin selection late in DE, then prevents polishing from moving the three hull vertices.
</DESCRIPTION>
