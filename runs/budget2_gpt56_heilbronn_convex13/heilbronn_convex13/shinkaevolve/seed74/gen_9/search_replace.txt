<DESCRIPTION>
Replace one purely Gaussian proposal with a differential-evolution proposal and add a sparse final local polish. The maximin triangle objective is highly nonsmooth: differential moves preserve useful relative point displacements and escape basins that independent noise cannot, while sparse late-stage moves improve active near-minimal triangles without disrupting the rest of the arrangement. This restores the successful coarse-global/fine-local strategy while retaining deterministic seeded behavior and the unit-square hull normalization.
