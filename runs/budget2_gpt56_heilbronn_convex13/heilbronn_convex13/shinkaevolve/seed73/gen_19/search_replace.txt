<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce occasional coupled two-point proposals drawn from the union of several tight triangles. The current search almost always changes one vertex of one active constraint, which can stall when improving that vertex merely transfers the bottleneck to a neighboring near-collinear triple. Coordinated moves permit the search to reorganize a small local cluster while retaining the existing lower-tail annealing objective. They use a reduced per-point step size to avoid destabilizing the configuration and remain deterministic under the existing seeded generator.
</DESCRIPTION>
