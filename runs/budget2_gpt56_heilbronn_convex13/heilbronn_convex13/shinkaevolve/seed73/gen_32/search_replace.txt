<DESCRIPTION>
Focus stochastic proposals on vertices participating in the lower tail of triangle areas, rather than choosing every moved point uniformly. This directly addresses the active maximin constraints while retaining a fraction of global moves for combinatorial exploration. Then add a deterministic active-set pattern-search polish: after annealing, test compass and diagonal displacements for vertices occurring in the tightest triangles, accept only strict improvements to the true minimum determinant, and reduce the probing scale when no improvement remains. This is inexpensive because it is performed only once and evaluates batched candidates.
