<DESCRIPTION>
Replace two of the equal-budget random restarts with longer, reheated continuations from the best configuration found during the initial global-search phase. The Heilbronn objective has many sign-pattern and active-triangle basins; random starts are useful for discovering promising basins, while additional search budget is usually more valuable when spent refining an already strong arrangement. Continuations use a smaller initial displacement and lower temperature than exploratory runs, preserving the established geometric structure while allowing active constraints to change and escape shallow local maxima. The total annealing iteration budget remains 84,000, so runtime should remain comparable.
</DESCRIPTION>
