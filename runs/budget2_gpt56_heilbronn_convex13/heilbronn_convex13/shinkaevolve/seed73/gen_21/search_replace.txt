<DESCRIPTION>
Replace equal-budget independent anneals with an adaptive two-stage allocation. First, run a broad set of inexpensive structured searches to identify promising orientation/sign patterns. Then retain only the strongest states and spend the remaining budget on substantially longer reheated refinements. This preserves deterministic behavior and approximately the existing evaluation budget, while concentrating expensive optimization on configurations that have already demonstrated a strong lower-tail triangle-area distribution. The refinement starts at a lower perturbation scale than a fresh anneal so it improves active bottlenecks rather than repeatedly destroying a good hull/interior arrangement.
</DESCRIPTION>

               initial_temperature: float) -> tuple[np.ndarray, float, int]:
