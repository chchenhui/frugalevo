<DESCRIPTION>
The search explicitly uses a nine-vertex fixed hull, but the polishing pass incorrectly clamps the inferred hull size to 8. This makes the ninth true boundary point movable during polishing and normalizes its determinant score using an octagonal hull area. Consequently, the final polish can optimize an inconsistent objective and may even return a configuration whose hull is no longer the intended fixed nonagon.

Allowing the inferred count to remain 9 restores consistent feasibility, preserves all nine boundary vertices, and uses the correct nonagon hull area and incircle radius. This is a zero-cost correctness improvement to the most refined stage of the optimization.
</DESCRIPTION>
