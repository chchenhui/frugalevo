<DESCRIPTION>
Replace pure minimum-area annealing with a lower-tail surrogate and occasional coordinated two-point moves. The min objective alone frequently exchanges one active nearly-collinear triangle for another; averaging the smallest 16 determinants rewards widening the entire active constraint set. Coordinated moves are drawn from vertices participating in the worst 16 triangles, allowing escape from bottlenecks that cannot be resolved by moving one vertex alone. Reducing iterations while using this more informative objective should also substantially improve evaluation time.
</DESCRIPTION>

    def lower_tail_quality(values: np.ndarray) -> float:
        """Weighted quality of the active lower tail of triangle constraints."""
        tail = np.partition(values, 15)[:16]
        weights = np.linspace(3.0, 1.0, 16)
        return float(np.dot(tail, weights) / weights.sum())
