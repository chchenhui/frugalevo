nine_hull_focus
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Focus the search budget on the empirically stronger circular-hull combinatorics: nine broadly spaced hull points and four interior points. For thirteen-point Heilbronn configurations, a dense near-circular boundary ring avoids the long exposed edges of seven-point hulls, while four interior roles provide enough freedom to resolve the active near-collinearity constraints. Replacing the weaker seven/eight-point layouts also reduces total runtime, allowing the same per-layout annealing depth to be spent on several distinct rotational incidence patterns of the nine-hull family.
</DESCRIPTION>
