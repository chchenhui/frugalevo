nine_hull_prior
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the weaker seven/eight-boundary alternatives with the empirically stronger nine-point circular hull prior. A nine-point hull leaves four interior roles, matching the useful Heilbronn pattern of a broad nearly circular boundary plus a small interior subsystem. The distinct phases remain useful because they are paired with independent deterministic random streams and hence produce different initial perturbation/incidence patterns.
</DESCRIPTION>

<NAME>
threefold_inner_seed
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
For the four free points of the nine-hull layout, initialize three as a perturbed threefold inner ring and the fourth as a displaced central point. This avoids imposing a square-like four-ring degeneracy and supplies the threefold interior structure commonly associated with strong triangle-maximin configurations. The subsequent annealing still freely changes every interior role.
</DESCRIPTION>
