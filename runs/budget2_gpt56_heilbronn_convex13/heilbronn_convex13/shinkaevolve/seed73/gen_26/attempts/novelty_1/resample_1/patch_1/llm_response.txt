<DESCRIPTION>
Replace uniform long anneals with a two-stage basin-selection strategy. Short initial searches explore distinct oriented-ring and hull sign patterns cheaply; only the strongest configurations are reheated and refined with a smaller move scale. This is particularly useful for the nonsmooth Heilbronn objective, where a single long run often commits early to a mediocre set of active near-degenerate triangles. The total iteration budget is also slightly reduced while concentrating refinement on promising basins.
</DESCRIPTION>

    def anneal(points: np.ndarray, score: float, worst: int,
               iterations: int, initial_step: float,
               initial_temperature: float) -> tuple[np.ndarray, float, int]:
        """Metropolis refinement at one exploration or polishing scale."""
        for iteration in range(iterations):
            fraction = iteration / max(1, iterations - 1)
            step = initial_step * (1.0 - fraction) ** 1.7 + 0.0020
            temperature = (initial_temperature * (1.0 - fraction) ** 2
                           + 0.000012)
