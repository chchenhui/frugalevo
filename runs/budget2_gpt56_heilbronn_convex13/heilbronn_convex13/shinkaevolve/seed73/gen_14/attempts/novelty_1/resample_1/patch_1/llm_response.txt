<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the variable-hull square search with a fixed reference simplex. Fixing three points at the vertices of a triangle removes irrelevant affine and hull-area degrees of freedom: every candidate has the same hull, every interior point is feasible by construction, and the normalized triangle area is directly its determinant. This also eliminates repeated monotone-chain hull computations, allowing the existing annealing budget to spend its time on meaningful geometric moves.

The initialization and mutation operators are changed to sample/project only the ten movable points into the reference triangle. Keeping a small margin from the boundary prevents accidental duplicate vertex/boundary placements while retaining essentially the full feasible region.
</DESCRIPTION>
