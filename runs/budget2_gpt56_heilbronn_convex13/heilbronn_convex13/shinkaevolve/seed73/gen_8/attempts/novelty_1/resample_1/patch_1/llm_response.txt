<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the biased initialization caused by sorting each pair of random coordinates. Sorting forces every initial interior x-coordinate into roughly the left half of the reference triangle, severely reducing spatial coverage and making near-degenerate triples much more likely. Reflection sampling produces points uniformly over the full reference triangle, retains determinism through the existing seeded generator, and gives every annealing restart a substantially better global starting distribution.
</DESCRIPTION>
