<DESCRIPTION>
Change the fixed containing hull from a triangle to the unit square and initialize the nine free points from a perturbed 3-by-3 lattice. The square supplies four well-separated hull vertices and has fixed area one, while the lattice initialization avoids the frequent nearly-collinear triples produced by uniform random starts. The existing determinant objective remains suitable because maximizing double-area is equivalent to maximizing area under a fixed hull. Projection is changed to square clipping, and all free-point indexing is updated for the four fixed corners.
</DESCRIPTION>
