<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the forced four-corner square hull with a fixed equilateral triangular hull. For an affine-area-normalized Heilbronn objective, a three-vertex hull removes an unnecessary boundary point and permits ten independently optimized interior points. The equilateral hull also aligns with the common three-fold structure of strong triangle-packing configurations. Candidate points are projected into this hull, keeping its area fixed and making raw minimum-area optimization equivalent to normalized optimization. Additional deterministic restarts compensate for the changed, less grid-like search landscape.
</DESCRIPTION>

    def project_to_hull(candidates: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
        """Project candidate coordinates into the fixed equilateral hull."""
        projected = np.asarray(candidates, dtype=float).copy()
        projected[:, 0] = np.clip(projected[:, 0], 0.002, 0.998)
        projected[:, 1] = np.maximum(projected[:, 1], 0.002)
        ceiling = np.sqrt(3.0) * np.minimum(
            projected[:, 0], 1.0 - projected[:, 0]
        )
        projected[:, 1] = np.minimum(projected[:, 1], ceiling - 0.002)
        return projected
