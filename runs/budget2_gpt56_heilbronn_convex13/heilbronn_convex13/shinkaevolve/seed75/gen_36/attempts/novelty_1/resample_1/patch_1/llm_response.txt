<DESCRIPTION>
Augment each stochastic coordinate update with deterministic directions derived from the currently smallest triangles incident to the moving point. For an active triangle, moving its vertex perpendicular to the opposite edge changes that triangle’s altitude, and hence its area, most directly. The edit retains random directions for basin exploration while adding both normal signs for the four most restrictive incident triangles. This targets the true max-min bottlenecks rather than relying exclusively on isotropic random proposals.

</DESCRIPTION>
