<DESCRIPTION>
Add periodic coordinated two-point proposals focused on vertices appearing in the currently smallest triangles. The existing optimizer only accepts one-point moves, which can stall at nonsmooth max-min bottlenecks where moving either vertex alone reduces another tight triangle. Joint moves permit simultaneous escape directions while retaining strict max-min acceptance and lower-tail tie-breaking. Batched evaluation keeps the additional cost small relative to the existing coordinate search.
</DESCRIPTION>
