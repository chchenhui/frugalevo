<DESCRIPTION>
Augment the coordinate search with deterministic geometry-aware proposals aimed directly at the currently smallest triangles incident to the moving point. For a triangle, its area changes linearly with displacement of one vertex in the direction perpendicular to the opposite edge. The existing isotropic random proposals only occasionally discover these productive directions. Replacing four of the twelve random directions by both normal orientations for each of the two tightest incident triangles preserves exploratory diversity while systematically targeting the active max-min constraints. This is computationally negligible because the relevant incident triangle areas are already maintained.
</DESCRIPTION>
