<DESCRIPTION>
Replace four very long annealing trajectories with eight shorter independent trajectories while preserving essentially the same total number of determinant evaluations. The maximin Heilbronn landscape is strongly multimodal: once a trajectory reaches a coordinate-wise bottleneck arrangement, additional annealing iterations tend to produce diminishing returns. More deterministic restarts increase the probability of reaching a better critical-triangle combinatorial structure. The final strict polishing remains substantial, but is shortened proportionally so every candidate basin receives refinement within the previous runtime budget.
</DESCRIPTION>
