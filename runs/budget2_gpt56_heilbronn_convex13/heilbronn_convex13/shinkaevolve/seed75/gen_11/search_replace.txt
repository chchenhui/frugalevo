<DESCRIPTION>
The annealing loop changes the soft-min width at every iteration, but previously compared a candidate evaluated at the new width against an incumbent score retained from the preceding width. This makes acceptance decisions inconsistent: narrowing the soft-min alone changes the objective even when the point set has not changed. Re-evaluating the incumbent at the current width produces a valid deterministic annealing comparison and lets late iterations genuinely optimize the near-minimum triangle constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>

<NAME>
target_active_free_vertices
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Poor triangles should be repaired by moving one of their actual free vertices. The former fallback replaced a selected fixed-corner vertex with an unrelated random interior point, weakening the active-constraint targeting that is essential in a maximin problem. Every low-area triangle contains at least one non-corner point, since three square corners form a large triangle; selecting directly from its free vertices focuses moves on the relevant geometry.
</DESCRIPTION>
