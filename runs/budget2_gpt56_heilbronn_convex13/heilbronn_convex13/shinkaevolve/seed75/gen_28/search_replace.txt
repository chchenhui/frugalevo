<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the unproductive long 16-start portfolio with the historically stronger 10 fully converged starts, then introduce deterministic coupled moves during the main search. Coordinate-only max-min ascent is commonly trapped when two interior points participate in competing tight triangles: neither point can move independently without reducing another active constraint. Periodic full-configuration pair proposals select vertices from the current lower area tail and test both correlated translations and independent displacements. Since acceptance uses the exact global minimum triangle area, these moves are monotone and cannot degrade a candidate. This directly targets the nonsmooth active-constraint geometry characteristic of Heilbronn configurations while keeping runtime near the proven 10-restart implementation rather than the current costly 16-restart version.
</DESCRIPTION>
