<DESCRIPTION>
Replace several effectively equivalent jittered square-lattice restarts with deterministic staggered and interleaved/hexagonal-like seeds. The max-min triangle objective has many local basins, and all-jittered 3-by-3 starts tend to preserve similar horizontal/vertical near-collinearity structure. Staggered rows break those alignments from the outset, while retaining broad coverage and the fixed unit-square hull. The existing fixed RNG seed preserves reproducibility, and no additional optimization sweeps are introduced, so runtime remains essentially unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
