<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single perturbed square-lattice initialization with a deterministic portfolio of complementary structured seeds. The existing lattice remains present, while staggered hexagonal-like, skewed, and asymmetric patterns provide different initial combinatorial triangle structures and reduce the chance that all restarts converge to the same max-min basin. This directly follows the useful fixed-corner formulation while adding non-lattice geometry at negligible per-sweep cost. Two extra restarts exploit these new seed families; the search remains deterministic because the same seeded generator is retained.
</DESCRIPTION>
