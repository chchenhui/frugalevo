<DESCRIPTION>
Permit candidate points to reach the square boundary and explicitly test boundary projections during coordinate updates. The current search artificially excludes the boundary with a 0.035 margin, despite boundary points being especially valuable in Heilbronn configurations: they can enlarge triangles involving interior points while preserving the unit-area square hull. Testing all four orthogonal boundary projections gives a deterministic, geometry-aware mechanism for discovering useful edge placements without relying on a random proposal landing exactly on an edge.
