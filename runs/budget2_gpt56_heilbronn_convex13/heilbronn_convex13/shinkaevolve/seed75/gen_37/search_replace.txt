<DESCRIPTION>
Strengthen the coupled active-constraint repair phase. Near a max-min local optimum, improving a tight triangle often requires moving a pair of its vertices jointly; repeatedly selecting the first two vertices biases the existing repair toward one displacement mode. This edit cycles deterministically through every movable pair appearing in the current bottleneck triangle or lower-tail set, increases the number of repair attempts, and doubles the correlated/random candidate stencil. All candidates are still evaluated with the exact minimum-area objective and accepted only when non-decreasing, so this preserves deterministic feasibility while providing substantially better escape from coordinate-wise traps.
</DESCRIPTION>
