<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the unconditional, randomly directed pair search with a rare late-stage repair triggered only after the strict bottleneck has stalled. The repair selects the two movable vertices occurring most often among the six smallest triangles, then tests deterministic compass-direction joint perturbations at two scales. This concentrates full-configuration batch evaluations on coordinate-wise local optima, uses the active constraint structure rather than random pair selection, and accepts lexicographically by strict minimum area followed by the lower-tail sum.
