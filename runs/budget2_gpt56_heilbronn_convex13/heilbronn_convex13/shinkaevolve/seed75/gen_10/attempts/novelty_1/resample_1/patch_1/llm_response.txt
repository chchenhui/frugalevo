<DESCRIPTION>
Add a deterministic joint two-point refinement stage before the existing one-point polishing phase. The strict coordinate search can become trapped when several active minimum-area triangles require two interior points to move cooperatively: every individual move temporarily worsens one bottleneck, even though a coupled displacement improves the true max-min objective. The added critical-pair moves select two movable vertices from the currently smallest triangle, test a small batch of correlated and independent joint perturbations, and accept only strict non-decreasing improvements. This preserves determinism and feasibility in the unit square while providing an escape mechanism for coordinate-wise local optima.
</DESCRIPTION>
