<DESCRIPTION>
Broaden the deterministic restart portfolio while retaining the proven perturbed 3-by-3 starts. The max-min landscape has many isolated basins, and lattice-only initializations tend to repeatedly enter similar local optima. Six additional starts use staggered hexagonal and rotated-grid geometries, which better reflect the near-isotropic spacing and rotational structure commonly useful in Heilbronn configurations. Each receives small deterministic jitter to remove exact collinearities. This increases global-search diversity without changing the established coordinate optimization and final strict polishing stages.

             [0.22, 0.78], [0.50, 0.78], [0.78, 0.78]],
            dtype=float,
        )
        hex_base = np.array(
            [[0.25, 0.16], [0.50, 0.16], [0.75, 0.16],

             [0.25, 0.70], [0.50, 0.70], [0.75, 0.70]],

        if restart < 10:
