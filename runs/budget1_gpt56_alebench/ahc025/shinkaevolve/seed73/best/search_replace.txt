reserve_actual_refinement
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reserve part of the query budget for a final, weight-exact local improvement phase. The existing method spends every query on noisy numerical weight reconstruction, then optimizes only that reconstruction. Instead, after the estimated simulated annealing phase selects its best partition, use the remaining balance queries directly on the candidate partition.

For two groups A and B, after identifying A as heavier, moving item x from A to B improves the true squared-sum objective exactly when sum(A)-w(x) > sum(B). This can be tested legally by comparing A without x against B. Thus accepted moves are guaranteed true-objective improvements, independently of estimation errors. The final result is therefore refined using actual balance information rather than solely estimated weights.
</DESCRIPTION>
