<DESCRIPTION>
The annealer currently outputs its final state, even though simulated annealing intentionally accepts worsening moves. Near the time limit or after reheating, this can discard a substantially better partition found earlier. Track the lowest estimated-variance assignment throughout annealing and output that best-seen state instead. This is especially useful because the estimated-weight objective is the only optimization signal available after all balance queries have been consumed.
</DESCRIPTION>
