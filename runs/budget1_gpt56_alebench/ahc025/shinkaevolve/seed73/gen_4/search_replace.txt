retain_best_sa_state
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The simulated annealing phase currently outputs its final state, even though annealing deliberately accepts worsening moves. Its temperature is cooled extremely slowly, so the final state can be substantially worse than an earlier state found during the search. Preserve the best assignment according to the estimated-weight variance and output that state instead. This is a safe improvement: it never worsens the objective used by the local search, while retaining SA's ability to cross local minima.
</DESCRIPTION>
