<DESCRIPTION>
The simulated annealing phase accepts uphill moves by design, but the current implementation outputs its final state rather than the best state encountered. Consequently, a good greedy/local solution can be lost near the end of annealing. Track the assignment with the lowest estimated variance throughout SA and restore it after the loop. This has no query cost and guarantees the assignment phase never outputs an estimated solution worse than its best visited state.
</DESCRIPTION>
