<DESCRIPTION>
The simulated annealing phase deliberately accepts worsening moves, but the current program outputs its final state rather than the best state encountered. With the initial temperature proportional to the current variance, this can discard a substantially better greedy or intermediate partition before termination. Store the assignment whenever the estimated objective reaches a new minimum and output that best-known assignment. This is a zero-query change and preserves SA exploration while guaranteeing that annealing cannot make the returned estimated partition worse than its initial greedy state.
</DESCRIPTION>
