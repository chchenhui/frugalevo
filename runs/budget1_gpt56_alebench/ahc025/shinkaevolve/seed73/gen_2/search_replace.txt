<DESCRIPTION>
The simulated annealing phase intentionally accepts worsening moves, but the program currently outputs its final state rather than the best state encountered. This can discard the strong greedy solution or a better intermediate solution when the annealing loop ends at a worse configuration.

Track the best assignment under the estimated variance throughout SA and output that saved assignment. This is a zero-query improvement: it preserves exploration while guaranteeing that SA cannot worsen the estimated objective relative to the initial greedy assignment. Since the estimates are derived from all balance information, retaining the best estimated partition should improve robustness and expected true balance.
</DESCRIPTION>
