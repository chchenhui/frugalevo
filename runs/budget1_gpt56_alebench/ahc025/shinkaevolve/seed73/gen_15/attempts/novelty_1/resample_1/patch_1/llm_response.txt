retain_best_sa_partition
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current simulated annealing state is printed directly, even though SA deliberately accepts worsening moves to escape local minima. Consequently, good partitions found earlier can be lost during later exploration or reheating. The strongest prior program retained the minimum estimated-variance assignment seen throughout SA and achieved substantially better performance.

Track the best estimated partition immediately after greedy initialization and update it whenever an accepted relocation or swap produces a new best variance. Print that retained solution rather than the terminal annealing state. This is a low-risk correction: it does not change query behavior or search exploration, but guarantees the optimizer never discards its best discovered estimated solution.
</DESCRIPTION>
