<DESCRIPTION>
The current simulated annealing phase may deliberately accept worsening moves, but it outputs the final state rather than the best state visited. This can discard a high-quality greedy/early-SA partition during a late reheat or uphill transition. Track the minimum estimated variance partition throughout SA and output that retained partition. The assignment snapshot must be taken before mutating the live assignment array in accepted relocate and swap moves.
</DESCRIPTION>
