fix_bulge_boundary_delta
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Correct the incremental score update for bulge insertion. Fish on the old edge remain inside before and after the modification, while fish on the new bulge edge are inside after it. Therefore, only the strictly changed strip should be queried on the old-edge side. The current inclusive rectangle counts unchanged boundary fish and causes the simulated annealing state's cached score to drift from the actual polygon score, which can make it retain inferior solutions. This change preserves exact delta accounting for both vertical and horizontal bulges.
</DESCRIPTION>
