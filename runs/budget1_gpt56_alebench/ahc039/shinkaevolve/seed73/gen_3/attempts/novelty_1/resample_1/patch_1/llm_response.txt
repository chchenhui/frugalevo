fix_bulge_delta_boundaries
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Bulge moves currently query the entire closed rectangle between the original edge and the new parallel edge. This incorrectly counts fish on the original edge and/or new edge even though those boundary fish do not always change inclusion status.

A bulge has the same score-delta strip semantics as moving an edge:
- expanding right/up includes the new boundary but excludes the old boundary;
- shrinking left/down includes the old boundary but excludes the new boundary.

The existing inclusive query can corrupt `m_count` and `s_count`, causing SA to accept/reject based on inaccurate objectives and potentially retain a polygon whose tracked score is not its actual score. Restricting the queried strip fixes the incremental accounting while preserving the geometry and runtime profile.
</DESCRIPTION>
