<DESCRIPTION>
The incremental score update for an added bulge currently queries the whole closed rectangle between the old and new edge. This counts fish on the old edge even though that edge is unchanged when the bulge expands, and incorrectly handles which boundary is removed when the bulge cuts inward.

This is especially harmful because the search deliberately snaps edges to fish coordinates. The annealer can therefore accumulate artificial score gains from repeatedly counting fish that remain inside/on the boundary, causing it to retain polygons whose tracked score differs from their actual score.

Update the queried rectangle according to whether the bulge adds or removes area:
- When adding area, exclude the old edge and include the new edge.
- When removing area, include the old edge and exclude the new edge.

This keeps incremental counts consistent with the problem’s inclusive-boundary rule while retaining the existing fast KD-tree delta evaluation.
</DESCRIPTION>
