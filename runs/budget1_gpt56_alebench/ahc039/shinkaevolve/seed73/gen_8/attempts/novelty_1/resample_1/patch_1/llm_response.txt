fix_bulge_boundary_delta
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The incremental score update for an added bulge currently queries the whole strip including the original edge coordinate. Fish lying on the original edge remain inside both before and after inserting the bulge, so they must not contribute to the score delta. This can corrupt the tracked SA objective and cause the search to retain polygons whose actual score is worse than estimated.

Make bulge delta rectangles consistent with edge-move delta handling: exclude the removed/original edge line and include the newly created bulge line. This preserves the required “points on polygon edges are inside” semantics exactly.
</DESCRIPTION>
