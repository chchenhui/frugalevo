<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed separation heuristic for the edge-count strategy with a direct search over all valid graph sizes.  The score has a very strong nonlinear dependence on the probability of an error across 100 queries, while also dividing the result by N.  Therefore the best N is not generally characterized by one fixed K value.

For every candidate N, estimate the classification error of the actual equally spaced edge-count code.  Each boundary between adjacent codewords contributes a Gaussian tail probability based on its true integer edge-count spacing, the expected post-noise spacing, and the exact edge-count noise variance L*epsilon*(1-epsilon).  The estimated expected score multiplier is (1-0.1*p_error)^100 / N, matching the expectation of 0.9^E for 100 independent queries.  This also accounts for rounding of codeword edge counts, which is relevant for small N.
</DESCRIPTION>
