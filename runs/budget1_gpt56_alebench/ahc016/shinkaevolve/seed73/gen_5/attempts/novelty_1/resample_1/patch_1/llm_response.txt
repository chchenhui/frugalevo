optimize_edgecount_size
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed separation heuristic (`K_SEP = 2.5`) with a direct search over every valid vertex count for the edge-count channel.

For a graph with `L` possible edges, adjacent codewords spaced uniformly in edge count have an approximate decision-boundary z-score

`z = (1-2ε) * (L/(M-1)) / (2*sqrt(L*ε*(1-ε)))`.

Using the normal approximation, estimate the average query error probability from adjacent-boundary crossings, then maximize the expected log score proxy:

`100 * p_error * log(0.9) - log(N)`.

This directly models the contest objective’s strong exponential failure penalty together with its `1/N` graph-size penalty. The former fixed `K_SEP` method effectively selected a very small adjacent decision margin and can incur many classification errors, especially for moderate noise and smaller `M`.
</DESCRIPTION>
