<DESCRIPTION>
Improve operation 4 (targeted detour insertion) by always considering an insertion point at the temporal midpoint of the target cell's largest current revisit gap.

The dirt contribution of a cell is proportional to the sum of squared revisit intervals. When adding an extra visit to a target cell, placing it in the middle of its largest interval gives the largest direct reduction in that cell's dirt term. The existing implementation chooses only random route positions, so it often inserts a useful detour at a poor time in the cycle.

This change retains the random samples for route diversification, but adds one deterministic high-value candidate. It uses the visit-time buffer already computed by `calculate_score_full`, including correct handling of the cyclic interval between the last and first visit.
</DESCRIPTION>
