peak_gap_targeting
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Prioritize cells with a long worst revisit interval in addition to their existing total dirt contribution. The benefit of adding a visit is dominated by splitting a long gap: the dirt accumulated over a gap grows quadratically with its duration. The existing target score only stores the sum over all gaps, which can over-prioritize cells that are already visited often. Store each cell's maximum cyclic gap during scoring and blend its corresponding dirt term into target selection. This affects both detour insertion and target-based subpath replacement while retaining part of the original robust total-contribution signal.
</DESCRIPTION>
