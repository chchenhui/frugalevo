<DESCRIPTION>
Operation 4 selects a target square with high current dirt contribution, but the current implementation samples only 20 route positions when choosing where to insert the detour. Every target square is necessarily already visited by the valid route, so an exact occurrence always exists and permits a cost-2 local wiggle. Missing it causes unnecessarily long detours, consumes route length, and dilutes all visit frequencies.

Scan the route coordinates to find an occurrence of the target square, using reservoir sampling among occurrences to preserve phase diversity. This makes operation 4 consistently add the intended cheap extra visit to the important target.
</DESCRIPTION>
