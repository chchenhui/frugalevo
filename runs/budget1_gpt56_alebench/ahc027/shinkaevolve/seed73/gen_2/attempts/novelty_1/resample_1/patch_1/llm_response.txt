<DESCRIPTION>
Improve the high-dirt revisit operation by selecting the detour insertion point globally instead of from only 20 random route positions.

Operation 4 is intended to add visits to a currently expensive cell. Its effectiveness is dominated by detour length: sampling only 20 positions often chooses a location far from the target even when the route already passes adjacent to it elsewhere. Scanning the existing route for the minimum APSP distance finds the cheapest valid round-trip detour, allowing more frequent revisits to high-contribution cells within the length budget.

Also narrow Op4 target selection from the top 10% of cells to the top O(N) cells. Since Op4 now finds the cheapest insertion point exactly, concentrating on the most costly cells produces stronger score-improving insertions while retaining randomness among several candidates.
</DESCRIPTION>
