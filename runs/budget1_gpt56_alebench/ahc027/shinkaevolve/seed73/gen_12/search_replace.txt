copy_moves_only
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Avoid copying the large derived state (`coords`, visit flags, and per-cell dirt terms) for every proposed move. Every operation reads route geometry exclusively from `current_pd_obj`, modifies only `candidate_pd_obj.moves`, and then `calculate_score_full` completely reconstructs the candidate coordinates, flags, score, and dirt terms. Copying only the move sequence substantially increases the number of simulated-annealing iterations available within the time limit, especially after routes become long.
</DESCRIPTION>

<NAME>
partial_target_ranking
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Target-cell selection only needs a uniformly random cell among the highest-contribution K cells; it does not need those K cells to be fully sorted. Replace the full sort of up to 1600 cells on many iterations with `nth_element`, which partitions the desired top-K set in linear expected time. This reduces overhead in the dirt-focused operations and leaves more time for route evaluations and annealing transitions.
</DESCRIPTION>
