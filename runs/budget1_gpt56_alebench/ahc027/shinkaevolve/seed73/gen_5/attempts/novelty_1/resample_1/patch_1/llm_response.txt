<DESCRIPTION>
Improve two route-edit operations that currently spend substantial search effort on weak or invalid candidates.

1. Operation 4 selects a high-dirt target that is guaranteed to already occur somewhere in the route. The previous implementation samples only 20 route positions, usually missing that exact occurrence and adding a long out-and-back detour. By locating an existing visit to the target, the operation always adds a length-2 wiggle at that cell. This directly increases cleaning frequency for the selected problematic cell with the minimum possible route-length increase.

2. Operation 3 previously reverses and inverts an arbitrary segment. Such a transformed segment goes from the old segment endpoint back to its start, so it is generally invalid unless both endpoints are the same cell. Restricting it to closed subpaths makes every proposed reversal legal and turns a mostly wasted operation into a useful timing/order perturbation.
</DESCRIPTION>
