adaptive_operator_portfolio

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed 15/15/30/10/15/15 neighborhood distribution with an adaptive portfolio. The solver records evaluated attempts, accepted moves, strictly improving moves, and normalized improvement per operator. Future operator probabilities favor neighborhoods that have recently produced improvements while retaining a nonzero exploration floor.

The mix is additionally biased by route length: when the route becomes long, compression/removal operators receive more probability; when it is short, dirt-targeted insertion/rerouting receives more probability. This makes the search react both to its current route-size regime and to empirically successful neighborhoods instead of continually spending equal effort on ineffective mutations.
</DESCRIPTION>
