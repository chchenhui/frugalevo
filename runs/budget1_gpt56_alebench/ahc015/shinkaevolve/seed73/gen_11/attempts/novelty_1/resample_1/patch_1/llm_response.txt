<DESCRIPTION>
Replace independently randomized sampling phases with a deterministic phase based on simulated turn and depth. The same placement ranks are then compared for all candidate tilt directions, substantially reducing decision noise in the expectimax evaluation. Stratification is retained, while changing the phase across turn/depth avoids a permanently fixed board-order bias.
