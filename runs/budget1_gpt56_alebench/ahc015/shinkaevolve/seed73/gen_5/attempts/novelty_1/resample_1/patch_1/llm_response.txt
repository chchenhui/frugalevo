<DESCRIPTION>
The lookahead currently samples future empty-cell ranks with replacement. This wastes a nontrivial fraction of the fixed simulation budget by evaluating identical placements multiple times, especially when the number of empty cells becomes moderate. Sample future placements uniformly without replacement using a partial Fisher-Yates shuffle instead. This preserves an unbiased estimate over empty cells while increasing effective board coverage and reducing decision variance, with negligible overhead relative to copying, tilting, and evaluating game states.
</DESCRIPTION>
