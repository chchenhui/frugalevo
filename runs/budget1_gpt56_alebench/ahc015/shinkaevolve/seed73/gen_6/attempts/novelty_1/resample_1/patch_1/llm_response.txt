<DESCRIPTION>
Replace random without-replacement insertion-rank sampling with deterministic stratified sampling across all currently empty cells.

The lookahead estimates an expectation over uniformly random placements. The existing random samples remain noisy with only 18/7 samples, and—more importantly—each candidate root tilt consumes a different random sequence. This makes action selection partially depend on Monte Carlo noise rather than the actual difference between tilts.

Stratified ranks cover the entire empty-cell ordering evenly, while using a deterministic turn-dependent rotation so that the sampled portions vary across turns. Candidate directions at the same lookahead level use identical rank strata, acting as common random numbers and substantially reducing comparison variance. It also removes shuffle setup and RNG calls from the most frequently executed search path.
</DESCRIPTION>
