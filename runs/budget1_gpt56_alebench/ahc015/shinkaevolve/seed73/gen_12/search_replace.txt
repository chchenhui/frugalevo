<DESCRIPTION>
Sample future empty-cell placements without replacement at every chance node. The current Monte Carlo lookahead can evaluate the same future placement multiple times, wasting a substantial fraction of its limited simulation budget and increasing variance between directions. Distinct ranks provide a more representative estimate of the uniform placement expectation while preserving the same number of evaluated branches and all decision logic.
</DESCRIPTION>
