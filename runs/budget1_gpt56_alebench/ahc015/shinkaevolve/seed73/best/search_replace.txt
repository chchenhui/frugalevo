<DESCRIPTION>
Sample future insertion cells without replacement at every chance node.

The existing lookahead draws each sampled `p` independently. With 18 samples among up to 99 empty cells this creates duplicate placements fairly often, wasting expensive rollouts and increasing Monte-Carlo variance. A partial Fisher-Yates shuffle produces a uniformly random subset of distinct empty-cell indices, so every rollout contributes information about a different possible insertion location. This improves the quality and stability of direction comparisons without increasing the number of simulated states or requiring dynamic allocations.
</DESCRIPTION>
