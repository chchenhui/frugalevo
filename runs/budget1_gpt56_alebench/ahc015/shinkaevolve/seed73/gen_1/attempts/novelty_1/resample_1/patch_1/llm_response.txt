<DESCRIPTION>
Replace independent random placement samples with deterministic stratified samples over the empty-cell rank range.

The current lookahead draws samples with replacement, so the same hypothetical placement can be evaluated multiple times while other regions are missed. This substantially increases variance, especially with only 18 and 7 samples. Moreover, each candidate direction receives different random samples, making direction comparisons noisy.

Stratified sampling divides all currently empty-cell ranks into equal-sized intervals and takes one deterministic pseudo-random rank from each interval. This guarantees broad coverage without duplicates, remains representative of the uniform placement process, and uses common random ranks for comparable lookahead states. It should make decisions more stable and improve expected final clustering without increasing search cost.
</DESCRIPTION>
