cycle_aware_beam_score
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current beam evaluation heavily penalizes the number of connected components, but does not distinguish a clean forest from a graph containing cycles. This is problematic because the final required object is specifically a tree: a connected cyclic component is not useful for the objective, while a nearly complete forest is often a promising intermediate state.

This edit adds two structural measurements:
- `tree_vertices`: total number of vertices belonging to acyclic connected components.
- `cyclic_vertices`: total number of vertices trapped in cyclic components.

The beam score then rewards preserving tiles in acyclic components and strongly penalizes vertices in cycles. The component-count penalty is reduced substantially: disconnected forest components are normal and often necessary during construction, whereas cycles are fundamentally harmful. The original largest-tree size remains the dominant signal.

</DESCRIPTION>
