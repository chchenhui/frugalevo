<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single shuffled near-elite selection with controlled multi-bucket diversification. The previous policy fills every non-elite slot from one contiguous top-ranked range, which tends to collapse the beam into nearly identical boards. The new policy keeps the same 20% elites, then allocates the remaining slots across: 50% shuffled immediate near-elites, 30% shuffled middle-ranked candidates, and 20% candidates chosen to diversify empty-square position and previous move direction. A final shuffled fallback preserves beam width when a bucket is too small. This retains strong exploitation while increasing trajectory diversity and reducing premature beam convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
