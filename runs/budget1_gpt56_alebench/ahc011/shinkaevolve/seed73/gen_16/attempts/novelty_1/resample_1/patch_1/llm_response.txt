<DESCRIPTION>
Augment the evaluation with the total number of matched tile edges and the size of the largest connected component. The existing heuristic strongly rewards fewer components, but this can accidentally prefer closing cycles: a cyclic component reduces the component count without contributing to the official largest-tree objective.

The new score uses matched edges as a mild positive signal for assembling correct local adjacencies, while explicitly penalizing the cyclomatic excess `matched_edges - (V - components)`. This excess is zero for a forest and positive exactly when cycles exist. The largest connected-component term also supplies a useful tie-breaker among states with equal largest acyclic tree and component count, favoring states that are closer to globally connected without overpowering the primary objective.
</DESCRIPTION>
