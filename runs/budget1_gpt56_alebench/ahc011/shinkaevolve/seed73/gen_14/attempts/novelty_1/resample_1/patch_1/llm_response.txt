<DESCRIPTION>
Replace uniform random selection from the entire non-elite pool with controlled rank-bucket diversification. The best 20% remain deterministic elites. The remaining beam slots are sampled independently from the upper, middle, and lower portions of the bounded top-ranked candidate pool in a 50% / 30% / 20% allocation.

This retains exploitation through elites and near-elite candidates, while deliberately preserving some lower-ranked but structurally different trajectories. Expanding the retained ranked pool from approximately `elite + 2 * width` to `elite + 3 * width` gives the diversification bucket meaningful candidates without allowing arbitrary low-quality states into the beam.
</DESCRIPTION>
