<DESCRIPTION>
Replace uniform random survivor selection with tournament selection. The existing beam keeps 20% elites, but chooses the remaining 80% uniformly from the next `3 * beam_width` states; this often retains low-ranked states despite already having a useful evaluation function.

A random tournament of three candidates preserves stochastic diversity and avoids a fully greedy beam, while substantially biasing survivors toward better-ranked candidates. If the candidate pool is too small to fill the beam, all remaining candidates are retained as before.
</DESCRIPTION>
