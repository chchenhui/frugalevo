<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the current shortest-stack destination policy, which limits move cost, but improve its tie-breaking. When several eligible destination stacks have the same minimum height, place the moved segment onto the stack whose current top box has the largest label. This buries a box that is likely to be needed later, while preserving smaller, sooner-needed top boxes for earlier removal. Empty stacks are treated as having the largest possible top priority, retaining their natural desirability among equal-height candidates.
</DESCRIPTION>
