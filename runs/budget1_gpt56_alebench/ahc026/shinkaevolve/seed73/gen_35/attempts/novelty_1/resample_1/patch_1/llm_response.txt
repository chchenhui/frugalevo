<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the purely height-based destination choice with a priority-aware choice. A stack containing a small remaining label will need to be opened soon, so placing a blocking suffix on it is likely to create additional future relocation cost. Prefer the destination whose minimum box label is largest, meaning it is expected to remain untouched longest. Use stack height only as a tie-breaker to retain the balancing benefit of the original heuristic. This directly targets the relocation energy rather than only current height.
</DESCRIPTION>
