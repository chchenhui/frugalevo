<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the purely shortest-stack destination rule with a bounded deterministic rollout. For every possible destination, the code simulates the forced move and then simulates the next several target removals using the existing shortest-stack policy. It selects the destination with the lowest simulated future moving energy.

The immediate cost of moving the current suffix is identical for every destination, while the destination strongly affects which near-future boxes become buried. As a tie-breaker, an imminent-target penalty prefers not to place the suffix on top of boxes whose labels will be needed soon. This preserves the robust baseline behavior while making destination choices aware of the actual ascending removal order.
</DESCRIPTION>
