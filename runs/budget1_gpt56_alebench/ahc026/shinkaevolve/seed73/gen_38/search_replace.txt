lookahead_destination_rollout
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a small deterministic rollout when evaluating each possible destination stack. The current policy only estimates urgency from the minimum label currently in a destination stack. This misses whether that placement causes expensive relocations shortly afterward.

For selected additional policies, each candidate destination is temporarily applied and the next five removals are simulated using the same destination rule. The candidate is penalized by the relocation energy in this short future. This retains the fast policy search while making choices aware of near-term stack interactions. Existing zero-depth policies remain available unchanged, and several rollout weights are included so the final per-instance selection can choose whether and how strongly to trust the forecast.
</DESCRIPTION>
