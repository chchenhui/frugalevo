<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the arbitrary first-choice tie breaking among short destination stacks with bounded forward simulation. For every destination whose height is at most one above the current minimum, simulate the next 12 removals after placing the blocking suffix there. The simulation uses the same load-balancing rule for later relocations and estimates their energy expenditure. This keeps the robust shortest-stack behavior while selecting among near-equivalent destinations based on their effect on upcoming blocking patterns.
</DESCRIPTION>
