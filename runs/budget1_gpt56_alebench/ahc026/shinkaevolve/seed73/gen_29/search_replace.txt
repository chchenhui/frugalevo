<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the empirically effective shortest-stack rule as the primary criterion, so the immediate move cost behavior is unchanged. When several destination stacks have the same minimum height, select the one whose smallest remaining box label is largest.

Moving a suffix onto a destination buries every box currently in that destination. Therefore, among equally short destinations, burying the stack whose next required box is furthest in the future is preferable: it is less likely that the just-moved suffix must be moved away again soon. This adds future-blocking awareness only as a tie-breaker, avoiding the risk of sacrificing the strong minimum-height heuristic for a speculative long-term decision.
</DESCRIPTION>
