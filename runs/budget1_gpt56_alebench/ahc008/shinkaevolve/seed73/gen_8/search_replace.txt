<DESCRIPTION>
Replace Manhattan-distance target selection with wall-aware BFS distances. As partitions grow, Manhattan distance frequently selects a wall-building stand square that is geometrically near but separated by an already completed barrier, causing humans to waste turns or fail to reach their assigned boundary. Computing one BFS distance field per building human lets target evaluation use actual travel distance while respecting existing walls and walls scheduled by earlier humans on the same turn. This is a targeted correction to the wall construction planner and also avoids choosing unreachable targets.
</DESCRIPTION>
