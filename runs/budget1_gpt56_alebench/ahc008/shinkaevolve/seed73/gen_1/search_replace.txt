<DESCRIPTION>
Prioritize wall cells adjacent to already completed walls. The current planner mostly chooses by walking distance, which can scatter construction across a strip perimeter and leave many openings through which pets can move between intended safe regions. Giving a moderate bonus to cells connected to existing walls makes each person extend a contiguous barrier segment, allowing dividers and room boundaries to become effective earlier during the 300-turn horizon.

The bonus remains smaller than the large safe-side penalty, so humans still build walls from inside their assigned enclosure rather than taking unsafe exterior positions.
</DESCRIPTION>
