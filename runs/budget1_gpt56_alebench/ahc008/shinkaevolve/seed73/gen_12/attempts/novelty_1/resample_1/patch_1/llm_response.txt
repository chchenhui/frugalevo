<DESCRIPTION>
Humans are explicitly allowed to share squares, both before and after simultaneous actions. The current coordination code unnecessarily prevents two humans from moving to the same destination and prevents a wall builder from selecting a standing square that another human plans to move into.

Those restrictions can cause avoidable detours and idle turns while constructing the strip boundaries. Removing them is legal: only movement into a square that becomes impassable this turn must be prevented. Wall conflicts are still retained, so no human will move into a newly built partition.
</DESCRIPTION>
