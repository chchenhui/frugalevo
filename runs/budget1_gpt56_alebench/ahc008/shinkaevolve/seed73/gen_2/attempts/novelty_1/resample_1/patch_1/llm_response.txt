remove_redundant_boundary_walls
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current strategy intends to isolate the room into horizontal strips. However, it assigns each human many walls on the outer room boundary and on the left/right borders. These walls are redundant because squares outside the 30x30 room are already impassable, so adding walls along the perimeter cannot reduce pet reachability or improve the final connected components.

Those unnecessary assignments consume a large fraction of the 300 turns and delay completion of the only useful walls: the internal horizontal separators between strips. Assign only the two half-segments of each internal separator, preserving the existing split ownership between neighboring humans. This reduces total required walls from roughly 30(M+1)+60 to only 30(M-1), allowing partitions to close substantially earlier and giving pets less opportunity to cross between regions while construction is incomplete.
</DESCRIPTION>
