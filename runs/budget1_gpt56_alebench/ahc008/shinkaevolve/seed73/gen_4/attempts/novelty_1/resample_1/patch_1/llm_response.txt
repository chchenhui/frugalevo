<DESCRIPTION>
Replace Manhattan-distance wall targeting with true shortest-path distance on the current passable grid.

The existing wall-selection logic can choose a wall-side standing square which is geometrically close but separated by already completed partitions. `get_bfs_move_char` then fails to find a route and returns `.`, causing repeated wasted turns and potentially leaving partitions unfinished. Computing one BFS distance field per human decision makes unreachable candidates ineligible and selects the actually closest reachable wall-building position. The BFS also treats walls planned earlier in the same turn as blocked, consistently with simultaneous-action constraints.

</DESCRIPTION>
