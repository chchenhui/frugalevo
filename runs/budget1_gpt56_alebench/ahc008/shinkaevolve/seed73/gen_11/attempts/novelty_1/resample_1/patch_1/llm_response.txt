allow_human_overlap
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Humans are explicitly allowed to share squares, both initially after movement and while standing. The current planner incorrectly treats an already selected human move destination as forbidden for later humans, and later cancels such moves. This can unnecessarily stall people in narrow passages or near planned wall cells, delaying construction and occasionally leaving them on the wrong side of a partition.

Remove these artificial human-human movement conflicts while retaining the necessary check that a human may not move onto a square being made impassable in the same turn.
</DESCRIPTION>
