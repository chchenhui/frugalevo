<DESCRIPTION>
Assign horizontal strips to people according to their initial row order rather than input order. The previous implementation assigned strip `i` to person `i`, although input person IDs are random with respect to position. Consequently, many people spent a substantial fraction of the 300 turns walking across the board before building their assigned half-walls.

This change first reads every human position, sorts human indices by initial `(row, column)`, and assigns consecutive top-to-bottom strips according to that ordering. Actions still remain in original input order because `humans_global_state` is not reordered. This preserves protocol correctness while significantly reducing initial travel and allowing strip closures to complete earlier.
</DESCRIPTION>
