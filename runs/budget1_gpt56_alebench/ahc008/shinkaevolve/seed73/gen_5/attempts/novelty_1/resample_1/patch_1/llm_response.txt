<DESCRIPTION>
The current wall target selection treats every legal wall cell almost entirely by walking distance because both the adjacent-wall bonus and nearby-pet penalty are zero.

A partition is only useful once its boundary becomes connected. Prefer cells adjoining existing walls so that humans extend already-started boundary segments rather than scattering isolated wall cells. Also apply a modest penalty to cells close to pets (while still allowing them when necessary), since these cells are more likely to become temporarily illegal on following turns as pets move.

The weights are deliberately small compared with the 1000 own-strip-side preference, so workers still build their assigned enclosure and do not make excessive detours.
</DESCRIPTION>
