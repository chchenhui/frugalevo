<DESCRIPTION>
Bias simulated annealing proposals toward expanding the already-connected zero region. A valid zero region can generally only grow through its frontier, so uniformly selecting all 2500 cells wastes most iterations on proposals that immediately fail zero-connectivity validation. The focused proposal selects a random zero cell and attempts to erase one of its nonzero grid neighbors. The remaining iterations retain the original unrestricted local recoloring proposal, preserving the ability to rearrange wards and escape local minima.

This should substantially increase the number of meaningful positive-score proposals after the first zero tile has been created, while maintaining topology-changing exploration.
</DESCRIPTION>
