<DESCRIPTION>
Reserve the last part of the time limit for a monotone multi-pass erosion phase. Late simulated annealing spends many proposals attempting invalid moves or accepting/rejecting temporary regressions, while a repeated zero-conversion sweep can exploit the topology rearranged by SA and only improves the objective. Each successful deletion can make nearby cells removable in the next pass, so repeat shuffled sweeps until the global deadline or no further deletion is possible.
</DESCRIPTION>
