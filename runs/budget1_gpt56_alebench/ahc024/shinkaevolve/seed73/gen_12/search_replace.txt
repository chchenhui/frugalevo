<DESCRIPTION>
Bias simulated annealing proposals toward cells adjacent to the current outside region. Since the objective is the number of zero cells, random proposals spend many iterations attempting to create isolated zero components, which are invalid. Sampling a zero cell and then one of its non-zero neighbors produces directly useful erosion moves while retaining random local recoloring moves for escaping local optima and repairing topology.
</DESCRIPTION>
