<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed 55%-to-zero proposal bias with three explicitly represented move classes: deletion (`nonzero -> 0`), restoration (`0 -> neighboring nonzero`), and nonzero recoloring. The selection retains substantial base probability for every class, while periodically giving a modest bonus to classes whose recently proposed moves pass the strict topology/adjacency validator more often.

This is intended to preserve the stronger broad random-neighbor exploration of the better prior program, while avoiding spending most attempts on direct zero proposals when the current state has few deletable cells. Restoration remains available to rearrange bottlenecks, and nonzero recolors can change ward geometry without immediately sacrificing score.
</DESCRIPTION>
