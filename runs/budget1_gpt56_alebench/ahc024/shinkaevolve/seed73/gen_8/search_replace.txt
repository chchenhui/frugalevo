<DESCRIPTION>
Bias simulated annealing proposals toward expanding the already outside-connected zero region. A random deletion attempt has a low probability of being adjacent to color 0 and therefore usually fails the zero-connectivity check. Instead, selecting a random zero cell and one of its neighbors directly targets the zero frontier, where converting a neighboring ward cell to zero is structurally valid with respect to zero connectivity and is much more likely to increase the score.

A minority of the original generic local proposals is retained so that the annealing phase can still recolor zero cells and reshape wards when necessary to preserve or create required adjacencies.
</DESCRIPTION>
