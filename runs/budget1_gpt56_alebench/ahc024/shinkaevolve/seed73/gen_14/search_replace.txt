<DESCRIPTION>
Add periodic restart-from-best diversification to the annealing phase. The current annealing state can drift below the saved best map after accepted negative moves and then spend a large portion of the time budget exploring an inferior region. Rebuilding all mutable data structures after restoring `best_grid_state` is inexpensive on this fixed 50x50 board and guarantees consistency.

After a sufficiently long stagnation period, restore the best legal state and temporarily reheat the acceptance temperature. This preserves the strong incumbent while allowing several alternative reversible boundary changes to be explored from it, rather than continuing from a poor trajectory. The restart is disabled near the end so that the final phase remains a low-temperature improvement search.
</DESCRIPTION>
