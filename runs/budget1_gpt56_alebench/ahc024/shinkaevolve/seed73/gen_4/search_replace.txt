<DESCRIPTION>
The objective only improves when a non-zero cell is changed to color 0. The current simulated annealing proposal selects 0 with probability only 1/5, while the other 4/5 proposals are neighboring colors. This spends most iterations on neutral recolorings or on moves that reduce the zero count.

Bias proposals toward zero, while retaining a substantial fraction of local neighboring-color moves. The latter remains important because temporary recoloring can alter region shapes and enable later valid deletions. Using a 55% zero proposal probability gives considerably more direct attempts to expand the outside region without eliminating SA's ability to rearrange wards.
</DESCRIPTION>
