zero_frontier_bias

<DESCRIPTION>
Bias simulated-annealing proposals toward expanding the existing connected zero region. The current uniform-cell proposal spends most iterations on impossible conversions: changing a non-boundary, non-zero-frontier ward cell to zero immediately violates zero connectivity. Sampling a zero cell and one of its non-zero neighbors directly generates valid-connectivity candidates much more often, allowing substantially more useful removals within the same time budget.

The fallback preserves the original proposal distribution, including recoloring moves that can restructure ward boundaries and occasional zero contractions required to escape local optima.
</DESCRIPTION>
