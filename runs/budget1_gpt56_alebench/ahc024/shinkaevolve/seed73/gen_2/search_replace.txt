<DESCRIPTION>
Bias simulated annealing proposals toward expanding the existing zero region from its frontier. A valid new zero cell must remain connected to the outside-connected zero component, so uniformly selecting arbitrary cells wastes most attempts on impossible interior deletions. This change selects a random zero cell and a neighboring nonzero cell with increasing probability as annealing progresses, while retaining random recoloring moves early in the search to escape local configurations. The result is substantially more useful validation attempts during the late optimization phase.
</DESCRIPTION>
