<DESCRIPTION>
Repeat the initial monotone deletion sweep until its allotted time expires instead of examining each cell only once. A deletion that is invalid early in a shuffled pass can become valid after neighboring wards have been removed and the outside-zero component has expanded. This spends the greedy budget on guaranteed score-nondecreasing moves before simulated annealing begins, while preserving all existing validation logic.
</DESCRIPTION>
