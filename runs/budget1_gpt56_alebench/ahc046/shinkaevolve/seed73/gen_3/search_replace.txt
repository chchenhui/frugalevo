allow_slide_over_target
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Permit BFS pathfinding to slide across the current segment target when routing to a setup square. Passing over a target during a slide does not visit it according to the rules, so treating it as an impassable barrier unnecessarily eliminates many valid and potentially short slide setups. Moves onto the avoided target remain prohibited; only slide traversal is relaxed. This increases the feasible strategy space without changing the action model or producing illegal paths.
</DESCRIPTION>
