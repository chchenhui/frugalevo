<DESCRIPTION>
The greedy single-segment neighborhood currently keeps only a strictly cheaper strategy for the current segment. This discards strategies with the same immediate turn cost even when they leave a different player position or block layout, both of which can substantially improve subsequent segments.

Use reservoir sampling among equal-cost strategies in the sampled subset. The complete suffix is still evaluated afterwards, so a neutral local replacement is accepted only when its downstream effect is beneficial (or under the existing simulated-annealing rule). This increases exploration of useful block configurations without admitting locally worse candidates in this operator.
</DESCRIPTION>
