reverse_post_alter

<DESCRIPTION>
Extend the post-segment “move then alter” options to also permit altering the square that was just departed from. This includes placing a block back on the just-visited target square, which can create a useful stopping wall for subsequent slide-based segments. The existing code intentionally excludes this reverse direction, despite it being legal and often strategically valuable because targets need not remain unblocked after their visit.

This adds only four strategy choices per segment while allowing the simulated annealing search to construct substantially more useful local wall configurations.
</DESCRIPTION>
