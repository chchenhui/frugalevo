<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the least distinctive move-plus-alter layout: altering the square directly ahead after moving away from a target. The new layout permits moving to an adjacent square and then altering back onto the just-visited target square.

Previously visited targets are particularly useful block locations because they are distributed throughout the board and can become slide stoppers for later targets. This also preserves the existing 12-option strategy-space size and therefore avoids reducing SA iteration throughput. The action remains legal because the player first moves off the target before altering it.
</DESCRIPTION>
