<DESCRIPTION>
Treating an intermediate target as an obstacle during a slide is unnecessarily restrictive: sliding across it does not visit it according to the rules. Only landing on the avoided target must be prohibited. Allowing pass-through expands the routing graph for slide setup, especially for strategies that need to reach a square immediately before a target, while preserving target-order legality because the BFS state is only accepted at slide landing positions.
</DESCRIPTION>
