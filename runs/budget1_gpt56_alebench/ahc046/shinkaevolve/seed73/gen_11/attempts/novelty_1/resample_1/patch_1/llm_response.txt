<DESCRIPTION>
The greedy construction currently resolves equal-cost segment strategies solely by enumeration order. Equal immediate costs can leave different block layouts and player positions, with substantially different costs for the next target.

Keep the same immediate greedy objective, but collect all strategies tied for the minimum current-segment cost. For every tied option (except on the last segment), simulate it and measure the cheapest possible strategy for the following segment. Select the tied option with the lowest one-step lookahead cost. This never worsens the current greedy segment cost and gives the subsequent SA search a more promising feasible initial state, while only adding work when strategies actually tie.
</DESCRIPTION>
