# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. **Add Heilbronn-specific structured seeds and raise pool cap to 5.** The current 5 seeds are all ring/grid-based, and feedback across Gens 29–35 flags pool diversity as the weakest link. Add a parabola seed (`(t, t²)` for 13 evenly spaced t, rescaled to [0,1]², which minimizes collinear triples), a 13-point no-three-in-line lattice subset, and a golden-angle ellipse spacing; raise `max_pool` from 3 to 5 in `_pool_add` so the new geometric families aren't evicted by near-duplicates. Seeding is nearly free at 6.40s total runtime.

2. **Implement multi-vertex coordinated moves on the single worst triangle.** The 0.02 plateau across 10+ programs suggests single-vertex moves cannot escape a worst triangle requiring all three vertices to move together. In `_anneal`, with ~25% probability, select the argmin triangle and displace 2–3 of its vertices simultaneously (each with its own Gaussian step of size `step`), keeping the duplicate guard and axis-fallback structure. This is the cheapest structural escape consistent with the vectorized rescoring pipeline.

3. **Add a near-collinearity penalty to the annealing objective.** Feedback on Gens 33 and 35 explicitly diagnoses "near-collinear triples" as the plateau cause. In `_anneal`'s acceptance, use a surrogate `min_k_mean - λ * collinearity_term` where the penalty is e.g. the fraction of the bottom-8 triangles with area < 0.5× the bottom-K mean, or the reciprocal of the smallest area (`1/(min_area + ε)`) blended into the surrogate. Keep `_polish` strict on the true min so the final reported score is honest — this matches the validated smooth-surrogate/greedy-polish split from Gen 33 (0.76).

4. **Soften `_polish` acceptance for the first half of iterations.** Gen 32's strict greedy polish underperformed and feedback warned it over-constrains. Change `_polish` to accept moves with `s > best - 1e-4` plus a small Metropolis term at low temperature for the first 600 iterations, then switch to the current strict `s > best + 1e-12` greedy — the staged squeeze is preserved but greedy tunnel-vision in the final steps is avoided. This is a low-risk, localized mutation.

5. **Budget-rebalance: cut per-seed anneal to 1500 iterations and add a round-robin crossover reheat pass.** Runtime past ~6s doesn't pay (Gen 32 at 10.98s scored worse; Gen 35 at 2.42s shows budget flexibility), so reduce the per-seed anneal from 2200 to 1500 and spend the savings on a final pass over the (now 5-member) pool: for each ordered pair, reheat from `0.7*p_i + 0.3*p_j` with 400 iterations at `t0=0.002`. Gen 35 validated the interpolation mechanics (0.63) — pairing basin-mixing with Gen 30's axis-fallback annealer (the 0.77 differentiator) tests whether the two gains compose.
