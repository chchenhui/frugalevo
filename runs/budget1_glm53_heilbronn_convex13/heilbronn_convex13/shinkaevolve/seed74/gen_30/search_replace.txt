<DESCRIPTION>
Add coordinate-axis fallback moves when the Gaussian targeted move is rejected in `_anneal`, and richer axis-search candidates in `_polish`. The full rescoring via `_score` is already cheap, so trying ±x/±y variants of the same rejected step costs almost nothing but gives directional diversity that isotropic Gaussian rejection lacks — worst triangles in Heilbronn configurations are often collinear triples that need a perpendicular (axis-aligned) correction. Also include a perpendicular nudge variant (both axes) for near-collinear triples.
