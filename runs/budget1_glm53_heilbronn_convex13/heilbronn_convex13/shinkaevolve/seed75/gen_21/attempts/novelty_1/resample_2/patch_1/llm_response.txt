I add: (1) three new structurally diverse seeds built directly in the valid domain — a two-arc cluster configuration, a rounded-square boundary, and an Archimedean-spiral fill — to reach different optimization basins; (2) a stronger local search that perturbs two points at a time (helps break single-point move barriers typical in Heilbronn configurations where the binding triangle involves multiple points); (3) a "small-k" objective: maximize a soft-min over the bottom few triangles (softmin via -log-sum-exp), which gives smoother gradients and escapes flat plateaus of the pure min. Softmin shares the same _score argument structure so we can reuse tri_idx. All seeds are convex-valid by construction (points on curves / interior), no rescaling needed.
