<DESCRIPTION>
The Gen-14 program (score 0.72) used a single-basin polish + Adam-kick loop with 0.2s windows, while the current top-2 alternating scheme with 0.12s windows scored lower (0.71). Shorter windows mean the Adam re-anneals (which need ~20 steps/iteration to warm up) rarely converge before being cut off. I revert phase 2 to the proven 0.72 structure with longer 0.2s windows, and slightly extend the time budget to 3.0s to give both phase 1 and phase 2 more room, since eval_time is secondary and both top scorers (0.71 at 7.0s, 0.72 at 2.81s) confirm a few seconds is fine.</DESCRIPTION>
