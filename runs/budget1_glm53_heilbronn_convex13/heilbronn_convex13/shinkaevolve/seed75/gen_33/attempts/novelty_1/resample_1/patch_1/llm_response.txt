coordinated_triangle_moves</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Add coordinated 3-vertex moves of the current minimum triangle to the hill-climb, per the recommendation. With ~20% probability, all 3 vertices of the smallest-area triangle are perturbed simultaneously with correlated small sigma from the adaptive schedule. This enables basin transitions single-point moves cannot reach, while strict-improvement acceptance prevents degenerate drift. The pool-based bottleneck targeting is retained, and the pool is recomputed after any accepted move.</DESCRIPTION>
