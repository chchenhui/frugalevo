<DESCRIPTION>
History shows Gen 19's strict-acceptance hill climb with 50% bottleneck-vertex-targeted moves (0.60 → 0.63) beat both full bottleneck-only targeting (Gen 13–18) and the directed perpendicular-push variants. The current `_polish` uses 75% directed perpendicular moves, which are the historically underperforming family. Replace it with a plain 50/50 mix: with probability 0.5 perturb a randomly chosen vertex of the current minimum-area triangle (found via argmin over the 286 cross products) with a Gaussian step; otherwise perturb a uniformly random vertex. Keep strict improvement acceptance and the adaptive sigma schedule (grow on improvement, shrink otherwise). This should strictly dominate the previous best structure while retaining the multi-seed + Adam pre-optimization + restart framework of the current program.
