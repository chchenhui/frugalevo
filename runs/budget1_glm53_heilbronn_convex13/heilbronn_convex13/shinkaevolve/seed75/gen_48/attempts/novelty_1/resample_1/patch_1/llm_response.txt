tail_surrogate_and_speed_fix</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
Two targeted changes based on the observed plateau (all programs stuck at min_area=0.02 with combined scores 0.62–0.72, meaning the score rewards the geometry of the whole small-area tail, not just the single bottleneck triangle):

1. **Remove a wasteful redundant computation**: the inner loop calls `_hull_area(points=x) - _hull_area(x)` on the same array twice — a no-op comparison that costs O(n log n) per candidate move across hundreds of thousands of iterations. Deleting it frees substantial CPU for more search iterations per seed.

2. **Replace the pure "mean of k smallest" soft objective with a tail-weighted surrogate** `S = 0.5·min + 0.5·mean(bottom-5)`. The old soft term ignored the actual bottleneck (min), so uphill-escape moves could drift away from configurations whose bottleneck was large. The combined surrogate keeps the bottleneck heavily weighted while still smoothing over the 5 smallest triangles, so the annealing phase relaxes toward configurations where several near-degenerate triangles are simultaneously lifted — exactly the tail geometry the combined score rewards. This mirrors the recommended multi-term tail surrogate (softmin over all 286 + soft-min over the bottom-5) but implemented cheaply with `np.partition`, keeping the incremental-update machinery intact.
</DESCRIPTION>
