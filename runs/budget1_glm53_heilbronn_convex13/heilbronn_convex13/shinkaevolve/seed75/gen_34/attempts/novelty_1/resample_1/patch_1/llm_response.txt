<DESCRIPTION>
All programs plateau at min_area ≈ 0.02, yet the combined score varies widely (0.57–0.71), indicating the score rewards lifting the *bottom of the triangle-area distribution*, not just the single bottleneck. Two targeted changes: (1) replace the plain "mean of k smallest" soft objective with a weighted surrogate `0.5·min + 0.5·mean(5 smallest)` so uphill escapes never sacrifice the true bottleneck while still raising the bottom tail; (2) in the final polish, add a secondary acceptance criterion that accepts a move when the mean of the 5 smallest triangle areas improves without hurting the strict minimum — this "lifts the floor" of the distribution and unlocks strictly-bottleneck improvements that pure greedy descent gets stuck on.
