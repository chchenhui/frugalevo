# Recommendations for Future Mutations

1. **Replace the bottleneck-only soft-min objective with a multi-term tail surrogate.** Since all 0.71–0.72 programs sit at min_area=0.02 while the combined score spans 0.62–0.72, the score rewards the geometry of the full area distribution. Extend `_smooth_grad` to `S = softmin(a) + λ·(soft-min of the 5 smallest areas via a second softmax)` — computationally, compute `w` twice (once over all 286 areas, once over the masked bottom-5) and sum the weighted gradients `q = 0.5*s*(w + λ*w5)`. Keep the Adam machinery, rescaling, and annealing schedule untouched; this is the same gradient path that made the current best win, just lifting the tail rather than one triangle.

2. **Restructure basin-hopping kicks to perturb only 3–4 vertices at σ=0.12, not all 26 coordinates at σ=0.05.** The isotropic full-covariance kick in `heilbronn_convex13` averages out and rarely escapes the incumbent 0.02 basin. Replace it with: pick either 3–4 random vertices, or (in ~20% of kicks) all 3 vertices of the current argmin triangle with correlated noise of magnitude 0.12–0.15, then re-run `_adam_run(kick, lr=0.01, p0=100)` with the existing strict-improvement best-seen acceptance. This directly targets the "basin quality is the binding constraint" insight.

3. **Vectorize `_score` inside `_polish` with per-point triangle index maps (66 triangles per move).** Gens 34/35/39 prove this is the efficiency standard: precompute `tri_map[i]` = indices of the 66 triangles containing vertex `i`, cache the areas of non-touching triangles, and only recompute the 66 affected areas per candidate move (maintaining a cached min over the untouched 220). Also cache the hull vertex set and skip `_hull_area`'s Python monotone chain when the moved point is interior. This converts wall-clock directly into more polish iterations within the 2.8s budget.

4. **Delete the dead placeholder gradient line `q = 0.5*s*w/p*p/p*p` and add a lightweight syntax/typo guard before submission.** Gen 37 (`=======` merge conflict) and Gen 39 (`'int' object is not subscriptable`) both scored 0.00 from trivially fixable bugs — one stray line costs an entire generation. Concretely: keep a `try/except` around the whole `heilbronn_convex13` body that falls back to the 13-gon, and ensure no mutation leaves overwritten-dead lines in the gradient path that previously crashed programs (Gen 20's missing rescale chain rule).
