# Updated Optimization Insights

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns

- **Soft-min Adam ascent is now the top performer (0.72)**: The current best (Gen 23/24, "softmin_adam_multistart_h13") matches the highest score in this batch by combining a differentiable soft-min surrogate (softmax over −p·area, exact chain-rule gradient) with Adam, 9 diverse geometric seeds, and a bottleneck-targeted + random-mix polish. This ties Gen 28's hybrid incremental descent (0.71) and beats all pure hill-climb/annealing variants (0.57–0.70).
- **Hybrid move mixes (50/50 targeted + random) outperform pure targeting**: The polish in the current best deliberately blends bottleneck-vertex moves with uniform random vertex moves, consistent with prior insight that pure bottleneck targeting over-commits (Gen 13–19 scored 0.57–0.63); the blended polish helps the best program reach 0.72.
- **Incremental triangle caching remains a proven efficiency win**: Gen 27 (0.70, "incremental_bottleneck_descent") and Gen 28 (0.71, "hybrid_incremental_bottleneck_descent") recompute only the 66 triangles touching the moved point, enabling far more accepted moves per second than full 286-triangle rescoring — the two highest non-gradient scores in the batch.
- **Basin-hopping kicks after the main search**: The current best's structure — seed sweep → polish → perturbation kick (σ=0.05) → short re-anneal (lr=0.01, p0=100) — extends Gen 14's successful second-restart-phase pattern (0.67→0.71→0.72).

## Ineffective Approaches

- **All approaches plateau at min_area_normalized = 0.02**: Every one of the 10 programs in this batch (Gens 23–32) hit exactly 0.02 min-area, far below the known optimum (~0.075). Neither gradient ascent, annealing, hybrid moves, nor incremental caching broke this barrier — indicating basin quality of seeds/local search, not iteration throughput, is the binding constraint.

## Implementation Insights

- **The current best's strengths**: (1) exact soft-min gradient via signed-area chain rule with softmax weights, including a correct sign fix (`s[s==0]=1`); (2) unit-hull rescaling each Adam step keeps the objective scale-invariant; (3) annealed sharpness p (30→600, ×1.15) gradually shifts from smooth landscape to true bottleneck; (4) best-seen tracking (`sc > best_sc + 1e-14`) returns the best configuration ever visited, not the last.
- **Hill-area normalization via monotone chain with dedup** (`np.round(pts,12)` + `dict.fromkeys`) is robust against degenerate hulls and shared across top scorers.
- **Adaptive sigma (grow 1.3x / shrink 0.6x) with batched candidate evaluation** persists in the current best's polish, matching the schedule of all 0.70+ programs.
- **Direct hull-vertex tracking beats recompute-to-check**: Gen 28's feedback notes that recomputing hull areas just to test membership wastes time — the current best avoids this by rescaling unconditionally, a simpler and cheaper choice.

## Performance Analysis

- **Score ranking**: Gen 23/24 (soft-min Adam) 0.72 > Gen 28 (hybrid incremental) 0.71 > Gen 27 (greedy incremental) 0.70 > Gen 26/29/30/31/32 (0.57) > Gen 25 (0.52). The gradient-based approach narrowly edged out the best local search despite both sharing the same 0.02 min-area plateau.
- **The combined score is decoupled from min_area at this plateau**: All programs sit at 0.02 min-area yet span 0.52–0.72, meaning the combined score rewards final configuration geometry (e.g., hull area, secondary triangle distribution) — future gains must optimize the full objective, not just the bottleneck triangle.
- **Speed did not predict score**: Gen 30 finished in 1.36s at 0.57 while Gen 25 used 7.0s at 0.52; the 0.72 winner used 2.82s. Time budget utilization is uncorrelated with quality.
- **Two proven paths converge at ~0.70–0.72**: smooth-gradient ascent (Gen 23/24) and incremental hybrid descent (Gen 27/28) are the only approaches above 0.70, suggesting combining exact-gradient soft-min search with incremental full-286 evaluation of the polish is the most promising architecture.

# Recommendations for Future Mutations

1. **Optimize the full objective, not just the bottleneck triangle.** The insight that all programs plateau at min_area=0.02 yet span 0.52–0.72 combined score means the score rewards overall geometry (hull area, secondary triangle distribution). Extend `_smooth_grad` to a multi-term soft-min: e.g., optimize a weighted surrogate `S = softmax-min + λ·(sum of soft-min over the 5 smallest areas)`, so the gradient lifts the bottom of the area distribution rather than a single triangle. Keep the same Adam machinery and exact chain rule.

2. **Add a second gradient phase using a soft-*quantile* objective with annealed sharpness.** After the current annealed soft-min Adam run (p: 30→600), run a second Adam pass minimizing the soft-τ-quantile of the 286 areas (τ ≈ 0.05–0.10). The pure min collapses gradient mass onto one triangle and can strand other near-degenerate triangles at the 0.02 plateau; the quantile spreads gradient weight over the weak tail and matches the full-objective structure better.

3. **Replace the Python-loop `_score` inside `_polish` with incremental 66-triangle evaluation.** Gen 27/28 proved that recomputing only triangles touching the moved vertex (with a cached min over the remaining 220) yields the highest non-gradient scores. Since polish acceptance needs the true min over all 286 areas, precompute the non-touching triangle areas once per accepted move and update only the 66 affected by vertex `i`; also cache hull vertex indices to skip `_hull_area`'s Python monotone chain when the moved point wasn't on the hull. This converts directly into more polish iterations and kick-restarts within the 2.8s budget.

4. **Restructure basin-hopping to kick 3–4 vertices at σ=0.12–0.15, not all 26 coordinates at σ=0.05.** The full-covariance kick averages out and rarely escapes the incumbent basin (all programs stuck at 0.02). Perturb only 3–4 randomly chosen vertices (or all 3 vertices of the current bottleneck triangle with correlated noise, capped at ~20% of kicks) before re-running Adam, keeping strict-improvement acceptance on the returned best-seen configuration. This targets basin transitions that isotropic small-σ kicks smooth out.

5. **Diversify and rebalance the seed portfolio toward distinct basins, with a second full restart sweep over the top-2 seeds.** Add: (a) a 9+4 two-cluster arc seed with different cluster ratio, (b) a reversed-chirality logarithmic spiral, (c) a perturbed convex 13-gon re-projected to the hull boundary at construction time. Then, instead of one 0.2s polish-kick loop around a single incumbent, keep the top-2 seeds after phase 1 and run short Adam re-anneals (lr=0.005, p0=200) on each alternately — the evidence (Gen 14, 23/24) shows restarts around diverse good basins drove every 0.67→0.72 gain, and per-seed time truncation (~0.25s) ensures all basins get explored before deadline.
