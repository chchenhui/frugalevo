<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation suggests extending the current basin-hopping idea into a multi-amplitude restart scheme applied to the top-3 ranked candidates rather than only the single best. Currently the main loop refines each seed independently and simply keeps the global best, throwing away all other strong local optima. Multiple distinct local optima often sit near each other; deterministic sinusoidal perturbations at several amplitudes (0.01–0.08) around each elite, followed by short coordinate-descent re-polish (_polish_full) and (optionally) base-space re-refinement, can hop into better basins. The scheme is fully deterministic (fixed amplitude list, fixed per-point deterministic pseudo-random signs), so reproducibility is preserved. Eval budget: 3 elites × 6 amplitudes × short polish — modest overhead given the current 8.7s runtime. Selection of elites is by true score; the kick uses a fixed deterministic direction field (sinusoidal pattern) applied to all 13 points, with a symmetric negative variant to double coverage cheaply.
