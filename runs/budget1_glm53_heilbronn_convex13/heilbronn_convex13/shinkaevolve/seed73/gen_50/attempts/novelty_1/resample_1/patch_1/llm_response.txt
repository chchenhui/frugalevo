<DESCRIPTION>
Two improvements based on the Heilbronn problem structure and prior-gen lessons:

1. **Two-pass ranking funnel**: The coarse climb is cheap but its output ranking is noisy. Following the recommendation (Gen 34's lesson: surrogate and exact rankings disagree near plateaus), after climbing every candidate we run a short exact mini-climb to re-rank, then spend expensive basin-hopping polish only on the top-2 candidates. This avoids wasting polish budget on mediocre basins while adding a cheap second-round screening pass over all candidates.

2. **Richer initialization set**: Add two structured, high-quality initializations known to be good for Heilbronn-type point packing: (a) a hexagonal/triangular lattice patch (hexagonal close-packing analogue, which gives uniform Voronoi cells and avoids near-collinear triples), and (b) a 6-fold rotationally symmetric configuration (center + two hexagonal orbits) which guarantees large minimum triangles between orbit points. These give the climber better basins than the 13-gon (which has many nearly-collinear consecutive triples and is a known local-optimum trap).

These changes increase the chance of finding a better basin (higher min_area_normalized) at modest time cost, since the polish budget is concentrated rather than spread over 16 candidates.
</DESCRIPTION>
