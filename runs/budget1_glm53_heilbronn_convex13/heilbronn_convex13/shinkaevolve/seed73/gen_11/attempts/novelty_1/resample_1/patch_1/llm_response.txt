<DESCRIPTION>
Two key issues limit the current solution: (1) the soft-min gradient spreads the update over all triangles weighted by proximity to the minimum, diluting the effort on the actual bottleneck triangles, and (2) a single initialization risks a poor local optimum. Heilbronn-type max-min problems are highly multi-modal, so a multi-start strategy over several ring geometries and seeds is the standard remedy. The new optimizer uses a *hard-min* vertex-repulsion scheme: at each iteration only the ~8 smallest triangles are enlarged by pushing their vertices along the gradient of their (signed) area, with an adaptive backtracking step (accept a move only if the new min does not decrease, otherwise shrink the step). This monotonically improves the true objective, avoids the ill-conditioned softmax, and converges robustly. Determinism is preserved via fixed seeds, and the best configuration across all restarts is returned.
