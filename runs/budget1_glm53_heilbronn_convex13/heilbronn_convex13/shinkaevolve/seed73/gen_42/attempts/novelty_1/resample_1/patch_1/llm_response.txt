optimize_true_ratio_objective</NAME>
<DESCRIPTION>
The refinement stages currently maximize the raw minimum triangle area while ignoring the convex hull area in the denominator. Since the benchmark score is min_area/hull_area, a configuration can be improved both by increasing the min triangle AND by shrinking the hull (e.g., pulling near-boundary "wasted" points inward so that small triangles with them grow relative to hull area). I make three consistent changes:

1. Refinement (`_refine_base` and `_polish_full`) now directly maximize the true objective `_true_score` (min area / hull area) rather than raw min area. The pattern search remains deterministic; each candidate is scored with the full ratio, so the optimizer can trade hull shrinkage against triangle growth — this is the key geometric insight: many near-optimal Heilbronn configurations sit strictly inside their hull, and fixing the hull (as the hexagon-clipped earlier programs did) is suboptimal.

2. Vectorize `_hull_area` with `scipy.spatial.ConvexHull` (with a pure-numpy fallback) as recommended: pattern search calls the score hundreds of times per sweep, so this multiplies the number of seeds/steps affordable in the same runtime.

3. Increase the deterministic seed pool (more 3-fold symmetric radial seeds plus more random seeds) to exploit the cheaper scoring, and use a slightly finer polish step. All seeds remain fixed/seeded, preserving determinism.
