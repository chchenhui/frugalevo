<DESCRIPTION>
The current program commits routes in a single savings-based order. Since the greedy sequential-discount heuristic is order-sensitive, generate several candidate orderings (ascending original cost, descending original cost, savings-based, and greedy-max-savings-dynamic), simulate the sequential discounted routing for each, and evaluate the resulting total transfer cost (sum of costs of unique committed edges). Then commit the ordering that yields the lowest total cost. This is a cheap wrapper around the existing proven discount machinery (which already achieved 749.49) and explores the ordering space directly, without changing the `[s, t, G[s][t]]` append format.
