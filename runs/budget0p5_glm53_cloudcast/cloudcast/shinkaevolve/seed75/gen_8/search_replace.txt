<DESCRIPTION>
The current algorithm sends every partition of every destination along the single cheapest Dijkstra path. This overloads the cheapest link (its price/cost typically rises with congestion, or throughput caps throttle completion), and it ignores alternative routes. A better strategy: compute the k-shortest simple paths from src to each dst (by cost), then assign partitions round-robin across these candidate paths, effectively spreading the load over parallel low-cost routes and exploiting multi-network topologies. This reduces redundant transfers on any single link and balances load across networks, lowering overall cost.
