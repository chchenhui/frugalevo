<DESCRIPTION>
Add persistent, numerically interior “island” layouts to prevent the fully elite-parented population from collapsing prematurely into one combinatorial active-constraint pattern.  The additional templates include both genuinely interior Dirichlet samples and mixed near-edge samples, where each movable point is independently biased toward one of the three sides without ever being placed exactly on a side.

During the exploratory and middle portions of evolution, a small number of low-ranked offspring are periodically replaced by perturbed island members.  Any island that discovers a superior active-triple structure immediately enters the ordinary elite population on the next generation, while the retained templates continue providing distinct geometric families.  This specifically addresses max-min landscape basins in which escaping requires coordinated relocation of several points rather than a small perturbation of the current elite family.
</DESCRIPTION>
