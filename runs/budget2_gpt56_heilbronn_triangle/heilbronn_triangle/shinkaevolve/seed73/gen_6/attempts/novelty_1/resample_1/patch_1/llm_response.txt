<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the independent single-point random walks with a hybrid elitist differential-evolution annealer. The previous population members could only communicate through the final saved best layout, so most evaluations explored unrelated local traps. The replacement retains every improved individual, uses the current global best as an attraction point, and uses differences between successful population members to propose scale-aware multi-coordinate moves. Periodic deterministic rejuvenation of the worst members around elite layouts prevents premature collapse. This is particularly useful for the Heilbronn objective, whose active minimum-area constraints change abruptly and generally require coordinated motion of several interior points.
</DESCRIPTION>
