<DESCRIPTION>
Anneal the low-tail bonus from a strong exploratory value to a small late-stage value, while tracking and returning configurations by their actual minimum triangle area rather than the surrogate score. This prevents the final result from being selected for improving non-active small triangles at the expense of the true Heilbronn bottleneck. A modest crossover is also added to combine useful point placements from distinct elite configurations, which is valid because all non-corner point labels are geometrically interchangeable.
</DESCRIPTION>
