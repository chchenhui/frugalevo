<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce controlled pointwise recombination between two elite layouts before the existing annealed mutation. Heilbronn layouts commonly have complementary subsets of well-positioned points, and inheriting complete barycentric point locations avoids the destructive averaging that can occur with arithmetic crossover. Additionally, reduce the surrogate tail weight so the true bottleneck area dominates selection, and add a small deterministic final maximin polishing phase evaluated using the exact minimum-area objective.
</DESCRIPTION>
