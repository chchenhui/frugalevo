<DESCRIPTION>
Initialize free points with the correct uniform-area distribution in barycentric coordinates rather than a distribution biased toward the top vertex. Replace the final isotropic-only random search with a hybrid coordinate-poll and random-direction refinement. Max-min triangle area objectives are nonsmooth: coordinate polling efficiently resolves active small triangles while random proposals preserve the ability to make coupled moves between local cells of the arrangement space.

    # The max-min objective is piecewise linear and usually has many narrow
    # cells.  Polling every parameter direction is more reliable near a cell
    # optimum than relying solely on isotropic perturbations; random proposals
    # still permit coordinated changes of several active triangles.
    best = population[np.argmax(scores)].copy()
    best_score = quality(best[None, :])[0]
    step = 0.055
    coordinate_moves = np.zeros((2 * dimensions, dimensions), dtype=float)
    coordinate_moves[2 * np.arange(dimensions), np.arange(dimensions)] = 1.0
    coordinate_moves[2 * np.arange(dimensions) + 1, np.arange(dimensions)] = -1.0
