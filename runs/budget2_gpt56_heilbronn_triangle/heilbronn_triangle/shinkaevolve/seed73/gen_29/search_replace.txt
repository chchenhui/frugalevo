<DESCRIPTION>
Seed a substantial, deterministic portion of the population with the boundary structure that is typical of strong Heilbronn configurations: the three container vertices, one additional point on each side, and five independently distributed interior points. The current stencil starts are all strictly interior (apart from accidental folding), which makes it unlikely that differential evolution discovers and maintains useful side-supported arrangements. These seeds retain varied side positions and noisy, well-spaced interior locations, while the remaining population remains fully random for global diversity. No point is constrained after initialization, so this improves discovery without excluding asymmetric optima.
</DESCRIPTION>
