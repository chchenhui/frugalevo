<DESCRIPTION>
The current barycentric decoder is feasible but not area-uniform: sampling its second parameter uniformly substantially over-samples the region near the top vertex. Transforming that parameter with the inverse triangular-area CDF makes initial candidates and differential-evolution mutations explore the equilateral container uniformly.

The final refinement currently moves all 16 parameters simultaneously, which becomes increasingly unlikely to improve a nonsmooth max-min objective once several nearly active triangle constraints exist. Use a mixture of one-coordinate, two-coordinate, and global perturbations. This permits precise motion of individual points while retaining occasional coordinated moves.
</DESCRIPTION>
