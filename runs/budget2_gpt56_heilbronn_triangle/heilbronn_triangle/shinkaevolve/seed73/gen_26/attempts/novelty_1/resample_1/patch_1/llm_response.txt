<DESCRIPTION>
Correct the p-best selection direction and add conservative elite recombination to the differential-evolution donor. The existing ascending argsort accidentally draws p-best guides from the worst fifth of the population, opposing selection pressure. Drawing from the final elite segment restores current-to-pbest behavior. For a subset of offspring, a convex blend of two elite layouts is used as the attraction target before the ordinary differential perturbation. Convex combinations preserve simplex feasibility prior to mutation and can combine complementary point placements without collapsing all offspring onto the single incumbent.
</DESCRIPTION>
