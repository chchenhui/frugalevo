<DESCRIPTION>
Add a late-generation active-set offspring class. In maximin triangle arrangements, progress near the optimum is controlled by the small collection of currently limiting triples rather than by the entire point set. The existing mutation is unbiased and useful globally, but it becomes inefficient once the search reaches the approximately 0.03 regime.

During the final 32% of generations, this edit reserves 28% of offspring for a small, sparse perturbation directed at the free point appearing most frequently among each offspring's twelve smallest-area triples. The perturbation remains random and deterministic under the existing seeded RNG, but concentrates search effort on coordinates responsible for active constraints. Its scale is substantially smaller than global mutation, preserving the delicate near-feasible separation achieved by elite inheritance. The original unbiased mutation remains in place for all offspring, maintaining global exploration and avoiding premature convergence to a particular active-set pattern.
</DESCRIPTION>
