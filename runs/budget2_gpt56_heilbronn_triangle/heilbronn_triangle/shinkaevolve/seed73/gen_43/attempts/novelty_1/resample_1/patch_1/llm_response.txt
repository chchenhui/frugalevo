elite_pointwise_recombination
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce conservative pointwise elite recombination after differential evolution and before simplex projection. The current population is largely descended from complete elite layouts, so free-point indices retain meaningful ancestry; selectively inheriting individual point locations from a second strong elite can combine complementary well-positioned subsets without the disruptive averaging of full-layout crossover. Restricting this to the middle search phase and a minority of non-elites preserves exploitation and the existing differential-search diversity. A small fraction of inherited points are convex barycentric blends, allowing interpolation when both elite parents have compatible local placements.
</DESCRIPTION>
