staged_elite_refinement
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Use a longer two-regime elite evolution schedule. The original differential mutation remains fairly large even at the final generation, which is useful for basin discovery but prevents precise maximin constraint polishing. The revised schedule retains strong early exploration and immigrants, then sharply reduces differential mutation frequency and magnitude during a substantially longer refinement phase. This follows the usual Heilbronn strategy of first finding a broadly separated configuration and then polishing its active near-minimal triangle constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>

<NAME>
preserve_global_champion
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Separate the local-search incumbent from the globally best feasible layout. The former code could replace `best` with a configuration whose true minimum area was slightly smaller in exchange for a support-score increase, and then return that inferior configuration. A separate local incumbent still permits those useful support-guided sideways moves, while `best` is now an invariant global champion and is always the returned layout.
</DESCRIPTION>
