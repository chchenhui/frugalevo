<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the all-score-based elite truncation with diversity-aware survivor selection. The current population rapidly becomes a collection of nearly identical relabelings or small perturbations of one basin, which makes both differential donors and crossover ineffective. This edit preserves the ten highest tail-guided candidates exactly, then selects the remaining six from a larger high-quality pool by greedy maximin distance after lexicographically sorting the eight movable points. Sorting makes the distance insensitive to arbitrary point labels. The true best layout remains explicitly retained because it is selected from the quality-leading prefix and separately tracked by the existing exact-objective archive.

        guaranteed_elites = min(10, elite_count)
        selected_indices = list(ranking[:guaranteed_elites])
        candidate_pool = ranking[guaranteed_elites:min(population_size, 72)]
        signatures = np.sort(
            population[:, 3:],
            axis=1,
        ).reshape(population_size, -1)

        while len(selected_indices) < elite_count:
            retained = signatures[np.asarray(selected_indices)]
            pool_signatures = signatures[candidate_pool]
            squared_distances = (
                (pool_signatures[:, None, :] - retained[None, :, :]) ** 2
            ).sum(axis=2)
            nearest_distance = squared_distances.min(axis=1)

            # Quality still matters within the diverse reserve; the normalized
