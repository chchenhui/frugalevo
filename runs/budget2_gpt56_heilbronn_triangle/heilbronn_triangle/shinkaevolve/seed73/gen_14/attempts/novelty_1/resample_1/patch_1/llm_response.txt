<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single-scale clone-and-jitter evolution with a staged schedule that preserves the successful elite mechanism while adding differential recombination during the exploratory phase. Pure elite mutation rapidly collapses population diversity, which is especially problematic for the nonsmooth Heilbronn maximin landscape because improving configurations often require correlated movement of several points. Differential proposals preserve such correlations and can escape the basin found by sparse mutation. The latter half retains smaller sparse perturbations for active-constraint polishing. The selection surrogate is also made much closer to the actual minimum-area objective, so tail-area tie breaking cannot displace a genuinely better minimum triangle.
</DESCRIPTION>
