<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the purely random differential-evolution population with deterministic perturbations of the boundary-supported triangular lattice pattern that has consistently produced stronger Heilbronn basins. This removes much of the expensive discovery phase spent finding useful boundary structure. Then, rather than performing only one frozen-orientation epigraph solve, repeatedly re-evaluate determinant signs and solve successive local orientation cells. The final acceptance is strictly based on the true absolute bottleneck area, preventing an infeasible sign-cell improvement from replacing a better layout.
</DESCRIPTION>
