<DESCRIPTION>
Add several analytic ascent proposals derived from differently sized sets of the currently smallest determinant constraints. The existing search uses random coordinate and joint perturbations, but near a good Heilbronn configuration these can fail when several competing small triangles require coordinated yet partially conflicting repairs. The new candidates form four constraint-balanced gradient fields from the smallest 6, 12, 24, and 40 triples. Within each field, triples involving overrepresented movable points are downweighted, reducing cancellation and preventing a single highly participating point from dominating the move. These deterministic directed proposals are inserted into otherwise unused candidate slots and preserve the incumbent candidate.
