<DESCRIPTION>
Replace uniformly chosen single-point mutations and overly broad all-active-point joint perturbations with participation-weighted sampling from the active constraints. In maximin Heilbronn layouts, several different near-minimal triples usually compete, so repeatedly moving a uniformly random point wastes proposals, while moving every active point simultaneously destroys useful structure. Sampling from a tempered participation distribution focuses effort on points implicated in restrictive triangles but retains baseline exploration. Small coupled moves sample two to four independently implicated points, which better repairs coupled active constraints without making disruptive global moves.
</DESCRIPTION>
