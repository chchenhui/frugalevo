<DESCRIPTION>
Bias each annealing move toward points occurring in the currently smallest triangles, rather than choosing all non-vertex points uniformly. In maximin configurations, only the active bottleneck constraints determine whether the minimum area can improve; targeting their incident points substantially improves useful proposal frequency. Occasional uniform and paired moves preserve the ability to escape local optima and make coordinated adjustments. The total number of objective evaluations is retained at a similar level while using more restarts.
</DESCRIPTION>
