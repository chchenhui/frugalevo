<DESCRIPTION>
Add a deterministic constrained maximin polishing stage after the stochastic search.  In simplex coordinates, signed determinants are normalized triangle areas.  The best simulated-annealing arrangement fixes a locally consistent orientation for every triple; maximizing an epigraph variable subject to those signed determinant constraints lets SLSQP directly equalize the active small-area triangles.  This is substantially more precise than random coordinate perturbations near the optimum, retains the three extremal vertices, and falls back safely to the annealed result if SciPy is unavailable or the polish is not genuinely better.
