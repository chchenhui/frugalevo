oriented_maximin_polish
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add an exact local maximin polish after the stochastic search. Once a promising configuration has determined the orientation of every triple, maximizing a common determinant lower bound subject to those fixed orientations is a smooth constrained optimization problem. SLSQP can substantially tighten the coupled active constraints that remain after annealed random repair. The candidate is independently re-evaluated and accepted only if its actual unsigned minimum area improves; missing SciPy or a numerical failure leaves the searched result unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
