<DESCRIPTION>
The current search changes only one interior point per iteration. In high-quality Heilbronn configurations, several nearly active triangle constraints typically need coordinated motion of multiple points; single-point moves are often unable to cross the narrow feasibility barriers between combinatorial arrangements.

This edit retains the existing active-constraint-guided coordinate search and global replacements, but reserves several batch candidates for small simultaneous perturbations of all eight free barycentric points. The perturbation is intentionally smaller than the single-point scale, is projected back into the simplex for every point, and keeps the incumbent candidate unchanged. This adds basin-transition capability without increasing the number of area evaluations or materially increasing execution time.
</DESCRIPTION>
