active_constraint_search

<DESCRIPTION>
Focus stochastic proposals on points participating in currently restrictive triples instead of selecting all movable points uniformly. In a maximin determinant problem, the active lower-tail constraints determine the feasible ascent directions; targeting them substantially increases the probability that an annealing proposal changes the current bottleneck while retaining occasional global moves to escape an incorrect oriented-matroid cell.
</DESCRIPTION>

<NAME>
directional_stencil_polish
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Augment the final local polishing stage with a fixed tangential barycentric stencil before its random directions. The min-area objective is piecewise linear in the position of one point, so coordinate-like simplex directions reliably test the faces and intersections induced by active determinant constraints. This removes avoidable dependence on random local directions while preserving random probes for oblique improvements.
</DESCRIPTION>
