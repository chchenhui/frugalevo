<DESCRIPTION>
Replace uniformly selected single-point mutations with bottleneck-driven proposals. In maximin Heilbronn search, the limiting constraints are a small set of low-area triples, so selecting points that participate in these triples concentrates work where it can increase the true objective. With moderate probability, the edit also selects a second active free point from the same low-area structure and moves both points jointly. This is important because many locally competitive order types require coordinated displacement of two points to relax several active determinant constraints simultaneously; independent moves often worsen another active triple and are rejected. The proposal retains occasional uniform selection and broad early relocation to preserve order-type exploration and remains fully deterministic under the existing generator seed.
</DESCRIPTION>
