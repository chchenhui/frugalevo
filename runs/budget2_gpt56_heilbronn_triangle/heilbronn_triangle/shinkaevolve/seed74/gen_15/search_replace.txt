<DESCRIPTION>
Retain several strong restart endpoints instead of relying exclusively on the single best trajectory state. Heilbronn maximin landscapes contain many distinct oriented-matroid basins, and endpoint configurations with slightly lower current minima can polish into superior active-constraint equilibria. The added deterministic elite refinement is deliberately short, uses the existing lexicographic minimum/tail merit, and then promotes the strongest refined seed into the existing more thorough polishing stage. This improves basin coverage without materially increasing the expensive global annealing budget.
</DESCRIPTION>
