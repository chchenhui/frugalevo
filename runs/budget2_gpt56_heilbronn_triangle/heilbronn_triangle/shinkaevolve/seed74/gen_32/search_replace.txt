<DESCRIPTION>
Restore bottleneck-directed search during annealing. Uniform single-point mutations spend most evaluations moving points that do not participate in the limiting triangles. This edit identifies points appearing in the lowest-area triples and preferentially moves them. It also adds occasional coordinated two-point proposals: tightly constrained arrangements often require two points to move together to relax intersecting active determinant constraints, which cannot be achieved by a one-point mutation without worsening another active triple. The proposal remains deterministic under the existing fixed generator and retains uniform mutations for order-type exploration.
