<DESCRIPTION>
Replace purely interior-biased Dirichlet restart initialization with a deterministic mixture of geometric templates and boundary-seeking Dirichlet samples. In the eleven-point Heilbronn problem, strong configurations commonly contain points close to each of the three sides together with a staggered interior core; ordinary Dirichlet samples often fail to enter these order types. The templates place one point near each side and five non-collinear interior points, then apply reproducible small barycentric jitter. The remaining restarts retain random exploration, including lower-concentration Dirichlet draws that more frequently sample edge-near configurations. This changes only initialization, preserving the established annealing and SLSQP polish machinery.
</DESCRIPTION>
