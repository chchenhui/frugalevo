<DESCRIPTION>
Add a conflict-aware non-adjacent exchange neighborhood after insertion refinement.

1. Adjacent exchanges only repair local inversions, while insertion moves can still miss schedules where two distant transactions must exchange places simultaneously.
2. A non-adjacent swap directly tests this important ordering change using the exact `get_opt_seq_cost` objective, rather than transaction-size proxies.
3. For small workloads, all pairs are evaluated; for large workloads, a broad randomized pair sample controls runtime.
4. Repeating best-improving pair swaps allows the search to remove several long-range conflict-ordering mistakes. The existing cost cache avoids re-evaluating schedules already examined by other neighborhoods.

This expands the local-search neighborhood without changing schedule validity or relying on approximate conflict estimates.
</DESCRIPTION>
