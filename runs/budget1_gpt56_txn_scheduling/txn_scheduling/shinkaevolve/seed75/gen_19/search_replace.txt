<DESCRIPTION>
Add deterministic destroy-and-repair restarts after the existing beam and local-search phase. The current descent is exact-cost based, but relocation and swap neighborhoods can still become trapped in a local optimum. A restart removes three transactions from well-separated positions, retains the relative order of all other transactions, and greedily reinserts each removed transaction at the position with the lowest simulator-measured makespan. This creates a substantially different feasible permutation while preserving useful structure from the incumbent.

Each repaired schedule is then passed through the established exact relocation/swap descent, so only simulator-validated improvements are retained. The search budget is enlarged and a restart reserve is kept while processing beam seeds, ensuring that restarts receive enough evaluations for both repair and subsequent local improvement rather than being starved by earlier seed searches.
</DESCRIPTION>
