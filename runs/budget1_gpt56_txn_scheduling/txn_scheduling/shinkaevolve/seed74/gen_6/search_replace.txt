<DESCRIPTION>
Add a conflict-aware two-transaction ejection/reinsertion phase after the existing insertion local search.

1. The current local search accepts only one-transaction insertion or exchange moves. Such a search can become trapped when two transactions must move together: moving either transaction alone temporarily preserves or increases the conflict-induced critical path, while moving both produces a lower makespan.
2. The new phase removes a pair of transactions from the best locally optimized schedule and exhaustively tests their reinsertion positions. Every candidate is evaluated through `get_opt_seq_cost`, so the decision uses the simulator's true conflict-aware makespan rather than operation-count proxies.
3. For small and medium instances all source transaction pairs and all destinations are examined. Larger instances use evenly distributed source and destination positions, preserving long-distance relocation opportunities without making the search prohibitively expensive.
4. Multiple successful rounds allow the optimizer to escape several interacting local minima. Only strictly lower makespan schedules are accepted, so this phase cannot degrade the schedule already found by beam search and single-transaction local search.
</DESCRIPTION>
