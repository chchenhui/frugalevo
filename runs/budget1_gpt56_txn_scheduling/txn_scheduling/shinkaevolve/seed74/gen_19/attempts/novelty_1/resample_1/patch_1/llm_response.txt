<DESCRIPTION>
Use a wider beam during the early half of construction, then reduce it for the later depths. Early transaction choices determine many of the eventual read/write dependency chains, so retaining more exact-cost prefixes at this stage gives the optimizer more structurally distinct complete schedules. The later narrower beam bounds evaluation cost once less of the ordering remains undecided.

Step-by-step:
1. Keep the existing exact simulator makespan as the sole ranking signal.
2. Increase the early beam capacity up to a bounded multiple of `num_seqs`, while applying a workload-size limit to avoid excessive expansion on large inputs.
3. Switch to the existing smaller beam capacity around the midpoint, preserving efficient completion.
4. Refine more final beam candidates, since schedules with slightly worse construction-time cost can improve more strongly under insertion/swap local optimization.
</DESCRIPTION>
