<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded contiguous block-relocation neighborhood before swap search. Single-transaction insertion can be trapped when two or more transactions must move together across the same conflict region: moving either one alone may not reduce the critical path, while moving the group preserves their favorable internal ordering and removes the inter-group delay. The new search evaluates relocations of blocks of length 2 through 4 using the same exact conflict-aware `evaluate` objective.

For workloads up to 30 transactions, all valid block starts and reinsertion points are examined. For larger workloads, the existing impact- and anchor-selected positions bound the neighborhood, preserving the scheduler's scalability while allowing coordinated moves around measured bottlenecks. Only strict improvements are accepted by the existing descent logic, so this cannot return a schedule worse than the prior local-search result.
</DESCRIPTION>
