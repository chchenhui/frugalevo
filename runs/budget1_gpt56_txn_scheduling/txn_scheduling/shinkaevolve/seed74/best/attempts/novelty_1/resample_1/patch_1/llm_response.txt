<DESCRIPTION>
Add a strict-improvement contiguous block-relocation neighborhood to the existing insertion, swap, and reversal descent.

A transaction schedule can contain groups of transactions whose internal ordering is already beneficial, but whose collective position delays a conflicting group elsewhere. Single-transaction insertion breaks such a group apart, while swaps and reversals cannot directly move it across the schedule. Relocating blocks of two to four transactions preserves useful local ordering while changing multiple conflict edges at once.

For workloads up to 30 transactions, the edit evaluates every block relocation of lengths 2–4. For larger workloads, it uses the existing objective-driven source and destination positions, retaining bounded runtime while concentrating moves around transactions identified by actual makespan impact. Every candidate is evaluated through `evaluate`, so acceptance is based exclusively on the conflict-aware simulator makespan.
</DESCRIPTION>
