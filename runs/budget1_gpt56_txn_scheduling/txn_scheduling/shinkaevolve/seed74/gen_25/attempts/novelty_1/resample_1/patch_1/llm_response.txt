adaptive_conflict_local_search
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The existing large-workload local search spends its evaluation budget on the first contiguous source positions, so later transactions and swap moves frequently receive no consideration. This edit distributes insertion sources across the entire schedule, while prioritizing transactions and positions involved in the most recently accepted improving move. Successful moves identify conflict-sensitive regions; examining those regions again is a direct-cost-guided way to continue shortening critical read/write dependency chains. The search remains deterministic and bounded, but it covers long-distance moves much more evenly and preserves budget for swap moves.
</DESCRIPTION>

<NAME>
broader_final_beam
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The beam was narrowed to at most eight final schedules, which can discard transaction orders that have slightly worse prefix costs but substantially better final conflict behavior. Increasing the bounded beam widths preserves more exact-cost alternatives before local search, while the existing size-based caps prevent excessive simulator evaluations. This is especially useful because makespan is non-local: an apparently expensive prefix can unlock parallelism after later conflicting writes are ordered.
</DESCRIPTION>
